Club Circuit keeps you informed about everything happening in your league — but not every notification is equally important to every player. Your notification preferences let you decide exactly which updates you receive and how you receive them, so you stay in the loop on what matters without feeling overwhelmed. You can adjust these settings at any time from your profile.

## Notification Channels

Club Circuit delivers notifications across three channels. You can enable or disable each channel independently for most notification types.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="In-App" icon="bell">
    Notifications appear in the bell icon in the top navigation bar. In-app notifications are always logged in your notification history, even if other channels are turned off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Notifications are delivered to the email address on your Club Circuit account. You can change your email address under **Profile → Account Settings**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Push (Mobile)" icon="mobile">
    Push notifications are sent to your device via the Club Circuit mobile app. You must allow notifications for the app in your device's system settings for push delivery to work.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Notification Types

The table below lists every notification type in Club Circuit and which channels you can configure for each.

| Notification Type | In-App | Email | Push |
|-------------------|:------:|:-----:|:----:|
| **Match Reminders** | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle |
| **Score Confirmation Requests** | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle |
| **Announcements** | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle |
| **Team Invitations** | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle |
| **Standings Updates** | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle | ✅ Toggle |

<Note>
  Some notification types cannot be fully disabled — see [Notifications You Cannot Turn Off](#notifications-you-cannot-turn-off) below.
</Note>

## Updating Your Notification Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar or name in the top-right corner of any page and select **Profile Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Notifications">
    Select the **Notifications** tab from the profile settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure each notification type">
    For each notification type, use the toggle switches to enable or disable delivery via In-App, Email, and Push. Changes are saved automatically — there's no separate save button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set match reminder timing (optional)">
    Under **Match Reminders**, use the **Reminder Lead Time** dropdown to choose how far in advance you want to be notified — options range from 1 hour to 3 days before your match start time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notifications You Cannot Turn Off

<Warning>
  Certain notifications are mandatory and cannot be disabled to ensure you always receive critical league information. These include:

  - **Urgent announcements** posted by your league organizer
  - **Weather or cancellation alerts** for your scheduled matches
  - **Disputed score escalations** that require your response

  These notifications are always delivered in-app. Email and push delivery for mandatory notifications follows your general channel settings.
</Warning>

## Setting a Preferred Reminder Time for Matches

<Tip>
  Use the **Match Reminder** lead time setting to fit your routine. If you typically need extra travel time to reach your facility, set reminders 2–3 hours in advance. If you play close to home and just need a quick heads-up, a 1-hour reminder keeps your notification feed clean. You can adjust this setting any time from **Profile → Notifications → Match Reminders → Reminder Lead Time**.
</Tip>

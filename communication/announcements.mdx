Keeping players informed is one of the most important parts of running a great league. Club Circuit's announcements tool lets you reach every player in your league — or just a specific division or team — with a single post. Whether you're notifying everyone about a schedule change or sending an urgent weather cancellation, announcements ensure the right people get the right message without requiring anyone to dig through a group chat.

## Creating and Publishing an Announcement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Announcements tool">
    From your league dashboard, go to **Communication → Announcements** and click **New Announcement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your announcement">
    Enter a clear, descriptive **Subject** line and type your message in the body. Use the formatting toolbar to add bold text, bullet points, or links if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an announcement type">
    Choose the type that best fits your message (see [Announcement Types](#announcement-types) below). The type determines the visual label players see and, in some cases, triggers additional notification behavior.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your audience">
    Select who should receive the announcement — all league members, specific divisions, or individual teams (see [Targeting Your Audience](#targeting-your-audience) below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or publish immediately">
    Click **Publish Now** to send the announcement immediately, or toggle **Schedule for Later** and pick a date and time to deliver it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Targeting Your Audience

You don't always need to message everyone. Club Circuit lets you narrow the audience to avoid notification fatigue and keep communications relevant.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="All League Members" icon="users">
    Sends to every player registered in your league, regardless of division or team. Use this for season-wide news and policies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Specific Divisions" icon="layer-group">
    Target one or more divisions. Ideal for division-specific schedule changes or standings updates that don't affect the whole league.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Specific Teams" icon="people-group">
    Send directly to one or more individual teams. Best for match-specific notices, makeup game reminders, or targeted messages.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Announcement Types

Selecting the correct type helps players quickly understand the urgency and nature of your message.

| Type | When to Use |
|------|-------------|
| **General** | Routine updates, welcome messages, season reminders |
| **Schedule Change** | Modified match times, court reassignments, added matches |
| **Weather / Cancellation** | Postponements or cancellations due to weather or venue issues |
| **Urgent** | Time-sensitive information requiring immediate player attention |

<Note>
  **Urgent** and **Weather / Cancellation** announcements trigger push notifications and emails immediately, regardless of a player's standard notification schedule. These types are designed for situations where players need to act quickly.
</Note>

## How Players Receive Announcements

When you publish an announcement, Club Circuit delivers it through two channels simultaneously:

- **In-app** — the announcement appears in the player's **Notifications** bell and in the **Announcements** feed under their league view. Unread announcements are highlighted until the player opens them.
- **Email** — a formatted email is sent to the address on the player's Club Circuit account. Players can manage their email notification preferences in their profile settings, but Urgent and Cancellation announcements always deliver.

<Tip>
  Keep your announcements short and direct. Lead with the most important information — players are often reading on mobile between games. Use the **Schedule Change** or **Weather / Cancellation** type for anything time-sensitive so it visually stands out in the feed.
</Tip>

Club Circuit matches follow a structured format designed to keep play consistent, fair, and appropriately competitive across all skill levels. Understanding the match format before you take the court ensures that you and your opponents are aligned on game length, serving procedures, and side-switching rules from the first point forward. Your league organizer may configure format variations for specific events, so always confirm the applicable format in your league dashboard before match day.

## Standard Match Format

The standard Club Circuit match is a **best-of-three series**:

- **Games 1 and 2:** First team to reach **11 points wins**, with a minimum winning margin of 2 points. If the score reaches 10–10, play continues until one team leads by 2.
- **Game 3 (Championship Game):** If the series is tied at one game each, a deciding third game is played to **15 points**, win by 2. The team that first reaches 15 with a 2-point lead wins the match.
- There are no sudden-death points. Win-by-2 applies at every score ceiling.

At the conclusion of the match, the winning team must submit the final score through the Club Circuit app or platform before leaving the facility. Unsubmitted scores default to the pre-match scheduled result and may be disputed by either team within 24 hours.

## Serving Order and Rotation Rules

Club Circuit follows standard USA Pickleball serving rules with the following structure:

- At the start of each game, the team that wins the pre-game coin toss may choose to **serve first** or select a side.
- In doubles, each player on the serving team has the opportunity to serve before the serve passes to the opposing team — **except at the very start of a game**, when only one player on the first serving team serves before a side-out occurs. This is called the "first server" exception and is standard in rally-scored doubles.
- The server rotates to the correct position (right service court when the serving team's score is even, left service court when odd) before each serve.
- After a side-out, the receiving team becomes the serving team. Both players on the new serving team will have a chance to serve, in the order they were in when the side-out occurred.

Announce the score clearly before every serve (see [Scoring](/rules/scoring) for the full score-calling format).

## Side Selection

Before the first game of every match, teams conduct a **coin toss** to determine starting positions:

- The winner of the coin toss chooses either the **side of the court** they wish to start on, or whether they want to **serve or receive** first.
- The loser of the coin toss is awarded the remaining choice.
- For Game 3, a new coin toss determines side selection and serve choice, giving both teams a fresh opportunity regardless of the first two games.

If your league uses a digital check-in system, the coin toss result may be logged in the app by mutual agreement of both teams.

## Side Switching Rules

To neutralize any court-condition advantages (sun position, wind, surface variations), teams switch sides according to the following rules:

- **Between games:** Teams switch sides at the end of every game.
- **During Game 3:** Teams switch sides when the first team reaches **8 points**. Play pauses briefly for the switch, then resumes with the same server and score.
- There is no time limit on side switches — complete the switch and resume play promptly.

## Timed Matches

Timed match formats are used at the discretion of the league organizer, typically when facility time is limited or during round-robin events with many teams. When timed matches are in effect:

- A match time limit is announced before play begins (commonly 45 or 60 minutes).
- When time is called, the team that is **currently leading** wins the game in progress. If the score is tied when time is called, one additional rally is played to determine the game winner.
- Games already completed before time is called count normally toward the match result.
- Your league dashboard will indicate whether timed match rules are active for a given round.

## Format Comparison

Use the table below to quickly reference how formats differ across match types in Club Circuit.

| Format | Games per Match | Points to Win (G1/G2) | Points to Win (G3) | Side Switch in G3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard League Match | Best of 3 | 11 (win by 2) | 15 (win by 2) | At 8 points |
| Tournament | Best of 3 | 11 (win by 2) | 15 (win by 2) | At 8 points |
| Playoff Match | Best of 3 | 11 (win by 2) | 15 (win by 2) | At 8 points |
| Timed Match | Time-limited | 11 (win by 2) | 15 (win by 2) or time | At 8 points |

<Note>
  League organizers may configure format variations — such as games to 15 in all games, single-game matches, or modified point caps — for specific leagues or events. Always verify the active format for your league in the **League Settings** section of your Club Circuit dashboard before match day. If the format displayed differs from what your opponent expects, contact your league organizer to resolve the discrepancy before starting play.
</Note>

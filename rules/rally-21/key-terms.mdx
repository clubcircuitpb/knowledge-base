What we mean by the words used throughout these rules.

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Match | One day of play between two teams: 8 rounds, 32 games. |
| Round | One set of games played at the same time on all 4 courts. |
| Game | A single doubles game to 21. Each game won counts toward the match. |
| Home team | The team playing at its own club. It serves first and sets its lineup second. |
| Away team | The visiting team. It sets its lineup first. |
| Lineup | Who plays which games, with which partner, in which round. Submitted in ClubCircuit. |
| Clean swap | Replacing one player with another who takes exactly the same games and partners, with nothing else changed. |
| Rally scoring | A point is scored on every rally, whichever team served. |
| Change of ends | When the first team reaches 11, teams switch sides and get 60 seconds for water. |
| Point differential | Total points your team won minus total points it lost across the match. Breaks a 16–16 tie. |
| Game penalty | Games taken off a team's total for the match because of a rule broken. |
| Forfeit | A game or match awarded to the other team because it couldn't be played or finished. |
| Draw | A match level on games and on point differential. Counts as half a win in the standings. |
| Substitute pool | Players registered in ClubCircuit who are available to sub for teams that need them. |
| Under review | A player flagged by ClubCircuit's eligibility check and awaiting a final decision. |
| Stacking | Partners lining up on the same side so each player stays on a preferred side. Allowed. |
| Drop serve | Dropping the ball and serving it after it bounces. Allowed. |
| Kitchen | The non-volley zone, the area within 7 feet of the net on each side. |

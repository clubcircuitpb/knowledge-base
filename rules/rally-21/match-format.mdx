**The short version:** 8 rounds, 4 courts, 32 games. The team that wins more games wins the match. You can't partner the same teammate more than twice in a match.

## How is a match set up?

A match is 8 rounds played on 4 courts during a 3-hour court booking. In every round, each team puts 4 men and 4 women on court.

| Round type | How many rounds | The 4 courts | Games |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mixed doubles | 4 | 4 mixed courts | 16 |
| Men's and women's doubles | 4 | 2 men's courts, 2 women's courts | 16 |
| **Whole match** | **8** |  | **32** |

Rounds are played in the order shown on the lineup sheet.

## How does my team win the match?

Every game counts as one point toward the match. Win 17 or more of the 32 games and your team wins. A 16–16 tie goes to the team with the better point differential (see [Scores and standings](/rules/rally-21/scores-and-standings)).

<Info>
  **Why every game matters:** a 21–19 loss on court 4 counts exactly as much as a 21–5 win on court 1. Point differential only matters if the match finishes 16–16.
</Info>

## How many games will I play?

It depends on how many players of your gender your team brings.

| Players of your gender | Games each, roughly |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | All 8 |
| 5 | 6 or 7 |
| 6 | 5 or 6 |

Your captain decides who plays which rounds.

## Who can I partner with?

You can partner the same teammate no more than twice in a match. The only exception is when an injury or emergency forces a change mid-match (see [Substitutes and injuries](/rules/rally-21/substitutes-and-injuries)).

## What if we're on the wrong court or facing the wrong opponents?

Fix it before either team reaches 11 points. Once a team reaches 11, finish the game where it is; the result counts. If several courts are mixed up and any of them has reached 11, all of those games are finished as they stand.

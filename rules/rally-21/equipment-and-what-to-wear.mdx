**The short version:** the home club provides new, USA Pickleball approved balls. Paddles must be USA Pickleball approved. Don't wear orange, green or other bright colors that make the ball hard to see.

## Which balls do we use?

The home club chooses the brand and model, as long as it's on the [USA Pickleball approved ball list](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/ball-list/). The list has hundreds of balls and changes often, so search it by brand and model to check. The home club should make every effort to provide new balls for league games, and all four courts should use the same model.

## Which paddles are allowed?

Only paddles on the USA Pickleball approved paddle list. Captains can check the other team's paddles before the match.

If an unapproved paddle is used and nobody catches it during the match, the results stand. The player gets a warning, and using it again can lead to suspension.

## Is there anything I can't wear?

Don't wear bright colors that could make the ball hard for your opponents to see. Orange and green clothing isn't allowed. If your clothing makes the ball hard to pick up, you may be asked to change before you play.

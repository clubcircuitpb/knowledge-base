**The short version:** one captain enters the scores in ClubCircuit and the other confirms them. Standings rank teams by matches won; a 16–16 match goes to the team with the better point differential.

## Who enters the scores?

Both teams keep score during the match, on paper or a phone. Before leaving, the two captains check the scores together. Either captain then enters them in ClubCircuit, and the other captain confirms them. If the other captain doesn't confirm or dispute them in time, they're confirmed automatically.

## Do our games count toward DUPR?

Only if both teams in the match have opted in. Each team decides for itself.

- **Who opts in:** the team's captain or its club, in the ClubCircuit app. The app shows which teams have opted in, so you can see before each match whether it will count.
- **When:** any time up to the midpoint of the regular season (end of week 4). It takes effect from the team's next match.
- **Once in, stays in:** a team that opts in stays in for the rest of the season, including the playoffs.
- **What gets submitted:** every game of a match where both teams opted in. Forfeits recorded as 1–0, and forfeited games with fewer than 13 total points, are never submitted. Nor are the games of a playoff match's deciding round.
- **Everyone needs a DUPR ID:** a game goes to DUPR only once all four players in it have a DUPR ID on their profile. Until then it waits, and it's sent with a later batch once they do.

  **Example:** your team has opted in, but this week's opponent hasn't. This week's games won't go to DUPR. Next week's opponent has opted in, so those games will.

## What happens if a match ends 16–16?

The team with the better point differential across all 32 games wins: total points won minus total points lost. If that's level too, the match is a draw. The same applies when penalties leave a match tied.

<Note>
  **Example:** both teams win 16 games. Your team scored 610 points and allowed 598 (+12); theirs scored 598 and allowed 610 (−12). Your team wins the match.
</Note>

## How are the standings ranked?

By match win percentage. A draw counts as half a win. If two teams are level, these break the tie, in order:

1. The result when those teams played each other
2. Percentage of games won
3. Average point differential per game
4. Average total points per game

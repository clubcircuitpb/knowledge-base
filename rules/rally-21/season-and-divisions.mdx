**The short version:** eight regular-season matches over seven weeks, then playoffs in week 8. Three divisions by rating. The top four in each division go to the playoffs.

## How long is the season?

Eight weeks: a seven-week regular season, then the playoffs in week 8 for the top four teams in each division. During the regular season your team plays eight matches, so in some weeks it plays two on the same day. The schedule shows which. Your matches are split as evenly as the schedule allows between your own club and other clubs. ClubCircuit publishes the full schedule, including playoff dates, about three weeks before the first match.

## What divisions are there?

Each club can enter one team per division.

| Division | Rating range |
| --- | --- |
| 3.0 | 3.000 to 3.499 |
| 3.5 | 3.500 to 3.999 |
| 4.0+ | 4.000 and up |

Which rating counts, when it is locked in, and whether you can play in more than one division are covered in the [Player Eligibility & Rating Policy](/rules/policies/eligibility-and-ratings).

## What does being the home team mean?

The team playing at its own club is the home team. That comes with two advantages:

- It picks the starting side and serves first in every game.
- It sets its lineup a day after the away team, so it can see who it is facing.

The home club also supplies the balls.

## How is the champion decided?

The top four teams in each division make the playoffs, and the winner of the final is the division champion. See [Playoffs](/rules/rally-21/playoffs) for the format. See [Scores and standings](/rules/rally-21/scores-and-standings) for how ties in the standings are broken.

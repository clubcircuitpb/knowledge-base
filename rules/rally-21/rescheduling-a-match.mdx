**The short version:** find a sub before asking to reschedule. Each team gets one automatic reschedule a season if it asks 3 weeks or more ahead. Asking later can mean paying for unused court time and being put on probation.

## Should we reschedule or find a sub?

Find a sub whenever you can. Reschedules affect the other team and the host club, so ClubCircuit approves them only when there's no reasonable alternative. If you think your team can't field a lineup, tell ClubCircuit immediately.

## What happens when we ask?

It depends on how far ahead you ask.

| How far ahead | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 3 weeks or more | Your team's first request of the season is approved automatically once the other captain picks a date. A second request needs ClubCircuit's approval. After that, contact ClubCircuit. |
| 1 week to under 3 weeks | May be approved. Your team covers any court time the host club can't rebook. |
| Less than 1 week | Same as above, and your team goes on probation. A second request this late in a season may lead to removal from the league. |

## How do we pick a new date?

When your captain asks to reschedule in ClubCircuit, they offer three dates and times. Each one has to be a time the host club has offered for its courts. The other team's captain picks one. To offer their own dates instead, they decline the request, then propose their own. If no date works for both teams, your team plays at the original time or forfeits the match.

A match can have only one open request at a time. If the other team's counter-proposal is accepted, it counts as that team's reschedule, not yours.

Rescheduled matches must be played before the regular season ends in week 7, unless ClubCircuit approves otherwise.

## What if the club closes or the weather is bad?

If a host club closes its courts, or ClubCircuit cancels a match for weather or safety, ClubCircuit reschedules it. It doesn't count as either team's request.

## Can a playoff match be rescheduled?

A semifinal can be, if both captains agree and ClubCircuit approves. ClubCircuit approves every playoff move, and the new date has to be before the final. The final's date is fixed. See [Playoffs](/rules/rally-21/playoffs#can-a-playoff-match-be-rescheduled).

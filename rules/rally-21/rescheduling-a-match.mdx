**The short version:** find a sub before asking to reschedule. Each team gets one automatic reschedule a season if it asks more than 3 weeks ahead. Asking later can mean paying for unused court time and being put on probation.

## Should we reschedule or find a sub?

Find a sub whenever you can. Reschedules affect the other team and the host club, so ClubCircuit approves them only when there's no reasonable alternative. If you think your team can't field a lineup, tell ClubCircuit immediately.

## What happens when we ask?

It depends on how far ahead you ask.

| How far ahead | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| More than 3 weeks | Your team's first request of the season is approved automatically. Later requests are decided case by case. |
| 1 to 3 weeks | May be approved. Your team covers any court time the host club can't rebook. |
| Less than 1 week | Same as above, and your team goes on probation. A second request this late in a season may lead to removal from the league. |

## How do we pick a new date?

Once ClubCircuit approves the request, the other team offers three dates and times. Your team picks one. If none works, your team either plays at the original time or forfeits the match.

Rescheduled matches must be played before the regular season ends in week 7, unless ClubCircuit approves otherwise.

## What if the club closes or the weather is bad?

If a host club closes its courts, or ClubCircuit cancels a match for weather or safety, ClubCircuit reschedules it. It doesn't count as either team's request.

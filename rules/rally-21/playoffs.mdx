**The short version:** the top four teams in each division play a finals day in week 8 at a neutral club: semifinals, then the final. Playoff matches are shorter, 4 rounds and 16 games, and each pair can play together only once. Captains take turns setting each round's lineup, so both teams get to respond. Playoff matches can't end in a draw.

## Who makes the playoffs?

The top four teams in each division's standings at the end of the regular season. Ties in the standings are broken as described on Scores and standings.

## When are the playoffs?

On one day in week 8, at a neutral club ClubCircuit chooses before the regular season ends. The semifinals are played first and the final follows the same day. ClubCircuit publishes the date with the season schedule; plan your availability from the start, because the final can't be moved.

## Who plays whom?

| Round | Matchup |
| --- | --- |
| Semifinal A | 1st vs 4th |
| Semifinal B | 2nd vs 3rd |
| Final | Semifinal winners, later the same day |

The higher seed serves first in every game and gets the last say on lineups (see below). The host club supplies the balls.

## How is a playoff match played?

Each playoff match is 4 rounds on 4 courts, 16 games in all, about an hour and a half.

| Round | Courts | Lineup set first by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 mixed | Higher seed |
| 2 | 4 mixed | Lower seed |
| 3 | 2 men's, 2 women's | Higher seed |
| 4 | 2 men's, 2 women's | Lower seed |

Win 9 or more of the 16 games and your team wins. Each round fields 4 men and 4 women from each team, as in the regular season.

<Info>
  **Pairs play together once:** in a playoff match, two teammates can partner each other only once. The deciding round is the only exception.
</Info>

## How are playoff lineups set?

Round by round, at the venue, in the ClubCircuit app. The teams take turns going first, so each team leads two rounds and responds in two.

1. **The leading captain sets its pairs** for the round: which pairs play, and on which courts.
2. **The other captain sees them and responds,** choosing which of its pairs plays each court.
3. **The round starts.** Lineups are locked once submitted.

The higher seed leads rounds 1 and 3 and responds in rounds 2 and 4, so it has the final response of the match. The lower seed leads rounds 2 and 4.

Each captain has 5 minutes: the leader from when either captain taps **Round 1 is finished** (or 2, or 3) in the app, or from the scheduled start for round 1, and the responder from when the leader submits. Until the round before is marked finished, the next leader's clock hasn't started. If the app isn't working, captains write their pairs on paper and swap them in the same order.

## Who is the champion?

The winner of the final is the division champion.

## What if a playoff match ends 8–8?

It's settled by a deciding round of three games, played at the same time on three courts:

1. **Each captain picks** one men's pair, one women's pair and one mixed pair. Pairs that already played together in the match may play together again.
2. **Both captains reveal their picks at the same time,** so neither can react to the other.
3. **Games are rally scoring to 15,** win by 2, with ends switched at 8.
4. **The team that wins two of the three games** wins the match.

If the deciding round can't be played, for example because court time runs out, the team with the better point differential across the 16 games wins. If that's level too, the higher seed wins. A team that deliberately delays or avoids the deciding round forfeits the match; the league office decides this.

## What if a penalty causes the tie?

- **A penalty known before or during the match** that leaves it tied still goes to the deciding round. If the round can't be played, the team without the penalty wins.
- **A penalty applied after the match** that leaves it tied isn't replayed. The team without the penalty wins.

## Who can play in the playoffs?

A player can play in the playoffs without a penalty if they've played at least 14 regular-season games in that division. Playoff games don't count toward the 14.

- **Games that count:** games forfeited because of an injury or an emergency still count for the players scheduled in them.
- **Games that don't count:** games from a match that was forfeited entirely, and any game forfeited for another reason.

## Can we use substitutes in the playoffs?

| Player | Allowed? | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Has 14+ regular-season games for your team | Yes | None |
| A regular on your club's lower-division team with 14+ games there | Yes | None |
| A regular on your club's higher-division team | No | Every game they play is forfeited, once the league office rules on it |
| Anyone else: a teammate short of 14 games, a sub-pool player or someone new | Only with ClubCircuit's approval | Half a game per game they play, added up for your team in each match, then rounded up |

<Note>
  **Example:** in a semifinal, one sub-pool player plays 3 games for you and another plays 2. That's 5 games, or 2.5, rounded up to a 3-game penalty. Your team's total for the match is rounded up, not each player's share.
</Note>

A player who is a regular on two of your club's teams counts as a regular in both divisions, as long as they have 14 games in the one they're playing.

## What do we submit before finals day?

Your playoff roster: up to 6 men and 6 women who will be there. Submit it in ClubCircuit by 8 PM the day before. Only players on it can play that day, including in the final. Round lineups are set at the venue, as above.

<Warning>
  **Costs 2 games:** a playoff roster submitted late, in each match you play that day.
</Warning>

## Can a playoff match be rescheduled?

A semifinal can be, if both captains agree and ClubCircuit approves. ClubCircuit approves every playoff move, however far ahead it's agreed. The new date has to be before the final. Moving a match earlier is preferred; moving it later needs a good reason, since it can hold up the final.

Your captain proposes the new dates in ClubCircuit, the same way as any reschedule. See [Ask to reschedule a match](/captains/request-a-reschedule).

{/* src: src/lib/reschedule/policy.ts (checkProposal, approvalRoute: LEAGUE for every playoff match; completionDeadline: before the final); rr-rally-21 preset playoffReschedulesAllowed: true; docs/adr/0081-finals-day.md "Playoff reschedules" */}

The final's date is fixed and it can't be rescheduled. If a finalist can't play, it forfeits, and ClubCircuit may offer its place to the team it beat in the semifinal. The league office handles this itself.

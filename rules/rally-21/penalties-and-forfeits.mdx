**The short version:** a penalty takes games off your team's total for that match, which can turn a win into a tie or a loss. A forfeited game goes to the other team. Every rule that can cost games is in the table below.

## Which rules cost games?

| What happens | What it costs | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lineup submitted late, without the other captain's written OK | 2 games | From week 3 |
| Lineup changed without notifying the other captain and ClubCircuit | 2 games | Any week |
| Player more than 15 minutes late, with no sub | That player's first game | Any week |
| Player injured and can't continue | That game | Any week |
| A game can't be filled | That game | Any week |
| A substitute is ruled ineligible | Every game that sub played | Any week, even after the match |
| Fewer than 4 men or 4 women show up | The whole match, 32–0 | Any week |
| A substitute who needs ClubCircuit's approval plays in a playoff match | Half a game per game they play, rounded up | Playoffs |
| A regular from a higher division plays in a playoff match | Every game that player played | Playoffs |
| Playoff roster submitted late | 2 games in each match that day | Playoffs |
| A playoff round lineup or response more than 5 minutes late | 1 game | Playoffs |

## How does a penalty change the score?

The penalty is subtracted from your team's games won for that match.

<Note>
  **Example:** your team wins 17–15 but has a 2-game penalty for a late lineup. Your total drops to 15, so the match finishes 15–15 and is decided by point differential (see [Scores and standings](/rules/rally-21/scores-and-standings)).
</Note>

If you know a penalty is coming, play the rest of the match with it in mind, even if ClubCircuit hasn't issued it yet.

## How is a forfeited game scored?

- If the forfeiting team was behind, the game is recorded at the score when play stopped.
- If the forfeiting team was level or ahead, or the game was never played, it's recorded as 1–0 to the other team.

## What happens if we forfeit a whole match?

It counts as a 32–0 loss. A team that forfeits or cancels a match may be placed on probation for the rest of the season. A second forfeit may lead to the team being removed from the league.

## Can we appeal a late-lineup penalty?

Yes, but only for one of these reasons:

- A ClubCircuit system problem stopped you from submitting on time.
- The opposing captain agreed in writing to the late submission.
- Something truly extraordinary happened. This is granted rarely.

Your own internet, device or login problems don't count. Send the appeal, with proof, to support@clubcircuitpb.com within 24 hours of the scheduled match start. The penalty stays in place while ClubCircuit reviews it.

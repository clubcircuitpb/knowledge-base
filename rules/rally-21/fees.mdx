**The short version:** regular players pay $79 for the season. Each team gets one free captain registration. Subs join the pool free and pay $12.50 per division at their first match. Everyone, subs included, pays a court share for each match they play.

How each fee works is explained in the [Player fee schedule](/rules/policies/fees). The app always shows the exact amount before you pay.

## Registration

| Who | Registration |
|---|---|
| Regular player | $79 per season |
| Captain | Free, one per team |
| Sub | Free to join the pool. $12.50 per division at your first match |

You pay it once per division, at your first match there. A sub can play up to two matches for the same team. To play a third for that team, you join its roster, and the $12.50 you paid in that division comes off your $79. See [Substitutes and injuries](/rules/rally-21/substitutes-and-injuries). {/* src: rules/rally-21/substitutes-and-injuries; docs/adr/0058 (credit toward registration: $79 − $12.50 = $66.50) */}

## Court shares

Each hosted match has a court charge: $120 for the home team and $240 for the away team. Each team's charge is split evenly among its players on court that day.

| Players on court | Home | Away |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | $15 each | $30 each |
| 10 | $12 each | $24 each |
| 12 | $10 each | $20 each |

You pay only for matches you play. {/* src: Club Partnership Deck, slides 4 and 9; leagues-app Ruleset.matchFeeSplit (EVEN_SPLIT). Per-club court fees are set in Club HQ: confirm each host club's home and away targets are 12000 and 24000 cents. */}

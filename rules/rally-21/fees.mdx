**The short version:** regular players pay $79 for the season. Each team gets one free captain registration. Subs join the pool free and pay $12.50 per division at their first match. Everyone, subs included, pays a court share for each match they're in the lineup for.

How each fee works is explained in the [Player fee schedule](/rules/policies/fees). The app always shows the exact amount before you pay.

## Registration

| Who | Registration |
|---|---|
| Regular player | $79 per season |
| Captain | Free, one per team |
| Sub | Free to join the pool. $12.50 per division at your first match |

You pay it once per division, at your first match there. A sub can play up to two matches for the same team. To play a third for that team, you join its roster, and the $12.50 you paid in that division comes off your $79. See [Substitutes and injuries](/rules/rally-21/substitutes-and-injuries). {/* src: rules/rally-21/substitutes-and-injuries; docs/adr/0058 (credit toward registration: $79 − $12.50 = $66.50) */}

## Court shares

Each hosted match has a court charge: $120 for the home team and $240 for the away team. Each team's charge is split evenly among its players in that match's lineup.

| Players in the lineup | Home | Away |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | $15 each | $30 each |
| 10 | $12 each | $24 each |
| 12 | $10 each | $20 each |

You pay only for matches you're in the lineup for. The court share is charged when the lineups lock, to each player your captain's posted lineup puts in at least one game. You don't pay if you're on the bench, only said you're available, or were in a lineup your captain never posted. If you're swapped out on the day, the league office refunds you.

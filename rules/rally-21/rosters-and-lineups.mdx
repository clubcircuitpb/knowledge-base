**The short version:** bring 4 to 6 players of each gender. Captains submit lineups in ClubCircuit by 8 PM: the away team two days before the match, the home team the day before. Late or unannounced changes cost 2 games.

## How big is a team?

Each team rosters 4 to 6 men and 4 to 6 women, plus a captain. Five of each is ideal: everyone gets solid court time and you have cover if someone drops out.

## How many players do we need on match day?

At least 4 men and 4 women, and no more than 6 of each. Only players on your submitted lineup can play that day, including as substitutes.

<Warning>
  **Costs the whole match:** with fewer than 4 men or 4 women, your team forfeits the match 32–0.
</Warning>

## When are lineups due?

Captains submit lineups in ClubCircuit. All times are Eastern.

| Team | Deadline | Example: Thursday match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Away | 8 PM, two days before | Tuesday, 8 PM |
| Home | 8 PM, the day before | Wednesday, 8 PM |

The home team sees the away lineup first and sets its own in response.

## What if our lineup is late?

Weeks 1 and 2 are a warning period with no penalty. From week 3 on, a late lineup costs your team 2 games, unless the opposing captain agreed in writing to the late submission.

<Warning>
  **Costs 2 games:** a late lineup from week 3, without the other captain's written OK.
</Warning>

## Can we change our lineup after the deadline?

Only to replace a player who can no longer make it. You can't change it to fit in someone who has become available. When you replace a player:

1. **Make a clean swap.** The new player takes exactly the games the missing player was scheduled for. Nothing else changes.
2. **Tell everyone right away.** Notify the opposing captain and ClubCircuit in ClubCircuit or by email as soon as you make the change.

<Warning>
  **Costs 2 games:** changing a lineup without notifying the other captain and ClubCircuit, even if the change itself was allowed.
</Warning>

## Can the other team respond to our change?

- If the **away** team changes its lineup after the deadline, the home team may adjust its own lineup in response.
- If the **home** team changes, the away team can't respond.
- Captains can always agree to any change between them, in writing.

## What if I paired teammates more than twice by mistake?

You may fix that error after the deadline. Change only the pairings needed to fix it.

## What if we're short a player and can't find a sub?

- **More than 2 hours before the match:** you may rearrange that gender's games so no one partners the same teammate more than twice.
- **Within 2 hours of the match:** clean swap only, no rearranging.
- **No one to fill a game:** that game is forfeited to the other team.

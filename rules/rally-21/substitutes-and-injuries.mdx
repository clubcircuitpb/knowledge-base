**The short version:** subs must be registered in ClubCircuit and eligible for the division. A sub can play up to two matches for the same team. An injured player who can't continue forfeits that game; subs come in from the next round.

## Who can be a substitute?

Anyone registered in ClubCircuit, on your roster or in the substitute pool, who is eligible for your division under the Player Eligibility & Rating Policy. Captains request subs through ClubCircuit. Someone who isn't registered can't play.

<Warning>
  **Costs every game they played:** if a substitute turns out to be ineligible, each game that player played is forfeited.
</Warning>

## What if a sub is still being reviewed?

Sometimes ClubCircuit's eligibility check flags a player for a manual review. A captain can still use that player, at the team's own risk, but only one such player per match.

If ClubCircuit later rules the player ineligible, every game they played while under review is forfeited. A player who was listed but didn't play causes no forfeit.

## How many times can someone sub for us?

Up to two matches per season for the same team. To play a third match for that team, the player must register as a regular player on it, and from then on can only play for that club.

## What happens if a player gets hurt during a game?

The injured player has up to 5 minutes to recover. If they can't continue, that game is forfeited. You can't bring in a sub partway through a game; a sub can take over from the next round.

A forfeited game is recorded at the score when play stopped, if the injured player's team was behind. If that team was level or ahead, it is recorded as 1–0 to the other team.

## What if an injury leaves us with only four players of a gender?

The player who was scheduled to sit out can take over the injured player's remaining games. This may mean two partners play together more than twice; that's allowed in this situation.

## Can a player come back after missing a round?

Yes. A player who sat out a round because of injury, an emergency or another good reason can rejoin in a later round, once both captains know.

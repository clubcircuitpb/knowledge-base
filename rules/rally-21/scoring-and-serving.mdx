**The short version:** rally scoring to 21, win by 2. Every rally scores a point, game point included.

## How does rally scoring work?

A point is scored on every rally, no matter which team served. Games go to 21 and must be won by 2.

## Do we have to be serving to win the game?

No. A point is scored on every rally, including game point, so a team can win the game while receiving. This follows the 2026 USA Pickleball rules.

<Note>
  **Example:** you lead 20–17 and win a rally on their serve. That's 21–17, and the game is yours.
</Note>

Games must still be won by 2. At 20–20, play continues until one team leads by 2, such as 22–20.

## Who serves first, and when do we switch ends?

The home team picks its starting side and serves first. Teams switch ends when the first team reaches 11, with up to 60 seconds for a water break.

## Can partners switch sides?

Only at the 11-point change of ends. Before a game starts, either captain can ask both teams to declare starting positions: the home team says where each of its players will start, then the away team does the same.

## Are stacking and drop serves allowed?

Yes, both. There are no lets on serves: a serve that clips the net and lands in is in play.

## What if we disagree about the score?

Go back to the last score both teams agree on and play on from there. If you can't agree on any earlier score, restart the game at 0–0. The exception: if both teams agree someone had reached 11, restart at 11–11.

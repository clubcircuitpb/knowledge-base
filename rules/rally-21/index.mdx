**The short version:** your club is your team. Each week your team plays one match against another club: 8 rounds on 4 courts, 32 games in all. Win more of the 32 games and your team wins the match.

## What happens on a match day?

1. **Before the match:** captains set lineups in ClubCircuit by 8 PM. The away team sets its lineup two days before; the home team sets its lineup the day before.
2. **Arrive on time:** courts open at the scheduled time for warm-up. The first round starts 15 minutes later.
3. **Play 8 rounds:** 4 rounds of mixed doubles and 4 rounds of men's and women's doubles, all 4 courts at once.
4. **Every game:** rally scoring to 21, win by 2.
5. **After the match:** captains check scores together, and the home captain enters them in ClubCircuit by noon the next day.

## Which rules can cost my team games?

<Info>
  **These are the ones to know.** Each takes games off your team's total for the match, or forfeits them outright.
</Info>

| What happens | What it costs | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lineup submitted late (from week 3) | 2 games | [Rosters and lineups](/rules/rally-21/rosters-and-lineups) |
| Lineup changed without telling the other captain and ClubCircuit | 2 games | [Rosters and lineups](/rules/rally-21/rosters-and-lineups) |
| A player arrives more than 15 minutes late | That player's first game, unless a sub takes it | [On match day](/rules/rally-21/on-match-day) |
| A substitute turns out to be ineligible | Every game that sub played | [Substitutes and injuries](/rules/rally-21/substitutes-and-injuries) |
| A game can't be filled | That game | [Penalties and forfeits](/rules/rally-21/penalties-and-forfeits) |
| Fewer than 4 men or 4 women show up | The whole match, 32–0 | [Penalties and forfeits](/rules/rally-21/penalties-and-forfeits) |

## What about anything not covered here?

These pages include the core USA Pickleball rules, so you shouldn't need the official rulebook during a match. For anything they don't cover, the current USA Pickleball Official Rulebook applies. Conduct, player eligibility and fees are covered in separate ClubCircuit policies.

If both teams knowingly agree to play something differently from these rules, the result stands. Questions go to support@clubcircuitpb.com.

## Who settles questions these rules don't answer?

ClubCircuit interprets these rules and decides situations they don't cover, aiming for fairness, safety and competitive integrity. If these rules and the ClubCircuit Terms of Use disagree on a competitive question, these rules apply.

## Can the rules change during the season?

ClubCircuit may update them. Any change during the season is announced to affected captains and players before it takes effect.

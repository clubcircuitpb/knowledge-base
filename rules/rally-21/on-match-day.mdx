**The short version:** arrive by the scheduled time, warm up for 15 minutes, then play straight through with no timeouts. Players on court make the calls; coaching happens between rounds.

## When does play start?

Courts open at the scheduled match time for warm-up. The first round starts 15 minutes later.

## What if I'm running late?

If you arrive more than 15 minutes after the scheduled time, you lose your first-round game unless a substitute takes it as a clean swap. Let your captain know as early as you can.

<Warning>
  **Costs a game:** arriving more than 15 minutes late, unless a sub covers your first game.
</Warning>

## Is there a break between rounds?

No. Each round starts as soon as the last one ends. If you're sitting out a round, warm up while it's being played.

## Can we call a timeout?

No. The only breaks are:

- 60 seconds at the change of ends when a team reaches 11
- up to 5 minutes for an injury (see [Substitutes and injuries](/rules/rally-21/substitutes-and-injuries))

## When can someone coach us?

Between rounds, anyone can coach: teammates, coaches, spectators. During a game, coaching is allowed only during the 60-second change of ends at 11.

## Who makes line calls?

The players on court. Spectators and teammates off the court can't make calls or weigh in on them.

## Who calls foot faults?

Players can call kitchen (non-volley zone) and service foot faults on their opponents. If a foot-fault call is disputed, replay the point.

If foot faults are called more than once in a match, either captain can ask each team to name one courtside observer. From then on, only the observers call foot faults on that court. If the two observers disagree, replay the point.

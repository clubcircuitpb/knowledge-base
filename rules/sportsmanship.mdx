Pickleball is built on a tradition of integrity, respect, and enjoyment — and Club Circuit leagues exist to uphold and extend that tradition at every skill level. The quality of your league experience depends not just on the points you score, but on how you conduct yourself from the moment you arrive at the court to the moment you log your final score. Hold yourself to a high standard of sportsmanship in every match, every interaction, and every dispute.

## Line Call Responsibility

In non-officiated Club Circuit matches, players are responsible for making honest, accurate calls on their own side of the court:

- **Kitchen (non-volley zone) calls:** Each player is responsible for calling their own kitchen violations. If your foot touches the kitchen line or enters the kitchen during or after a volley, call it on yourself immediately.
- **In/out calls:** You call balls that land on your side of the net. Your opponent calls balls on their side. Do not make line calls for your opponent's side of the court.
- **Benefit of the doubt:** If you are not certain whether a ball was in or out, call it **in**. A ball must be clearly out for you to call it out. Uncertainty is not grounds for an out call.
- **Overturning a call:** If you call a ball out and your partner immediately and clearly saw it as in, you may correct the call before the next rally begins. Once play has resumed, the call stands.

Make all line calls promptly, clearly, and audibly. Delayed calls that conveniently follow the outcome of a rally are a form of unsportsmanlike conduct.

## Respectful Communication

How you communicate on and off the court directly affects the experience of everyone around you. Club Circuit expects all players to:

- Use courteous, constructive language at all times during matches, warmups, and post-match interactions.
- Avoid excessive celebration, trash talk, or comments intended to intimidate, distract, or demean opponents.
- Keep your voice at an appropriate volume — loud enough to call the score and make line calls, not so loud as to disrupt adjacent courts.
- Resolve disagreements calmly and in good faith. If a discussion becomes heated, take a breath, lower your voice, and focus on the facts of the dispute.

Language or gestures that are demeaning, offensive, or discriminatory will not be tolerated under any circumstances.

## Conduct Toward Opponents, Partners, and Officials

Your responsibilities extend beyond your own play:

- **Toward opponents:** Treat your opponents as you want to be treated. Acknowledge good shots, accept close calls gracefully, and never intentionally create distraction or confusion during a rally.
- **Toward your partner:** Support your partner constructively. Avoid blame, frustration-venting, or negative reactions to their mistakes. Partners who publicly berate each other negatively affect the atmosphere of the entire match.
- **Toward officials:** If a referee or line judge is present, address them respectfully and through the correct process — raise your hand and wait for a stoppage; do not argue or speak over them. Accept rulings and escalate disagreements through the formal protest process if necessary.
- **Toward spectators:** Acknowledge that spectators, including other players waiting for the court, are watching. Conduct yourself as a representative of Club Circuit and the sport.

<Warning>
  Unsportsmanlike conduct is taken seriously in Club Circuit leagues. Depending on severity and frequency, violations may result in:
  - A **verbal warning** from a referee or league organizer
  - **Forfeiture of the current game** or match
  - **Suspension from one or more future matches**
  - **Permanent removal** from the league without a refund of registration fees

  Repeated or egregious conduct violations — including physical intimidation, verbal abuse, or deliberate cheating — are reported to Club Circuit's Trust and Safety team and may affect your eligibility to register for future leagues on the platform.
</Warning>

## Post-Match Conduct

How you close out a match matters just as much as how you played it:

- **Handshakes:** At the conclusion of every match, both teams meet at the net for a handshake or equivalent acknowledgment. This is not optional — it is a required part of every Club Circuit match.
- **Score confirmation:** The winning team is responsible for entering the final score in the Club Circuit app. Both teams should verify the submitted score before leaving the facility. If you believe a score has been entered incorrectly, dispute it through the platform within **24 hours** of match completion.
- **Court etiquette:** Clear the court promptly after your match concludes. Remove all personal belongings, collect any balls used during play, and leave the court ready for the next group.

A close loss handled with grace leaves a stronger impression — and builds a better league community — than any winning score.

<Note>
  If you experience or witness a sportsmanship issue during or after a match, report it through the Club Circuit platform. Navigate to your match record, select **Report an Issue**, and choose **Sportsmanship Concern**. Provide a factual description of the incident, including the time, court, and players involved. All reports are reviewed by your league organizer and, where appropriate, escalated to Club Circuit's Trust and Safety team. Reports are handled with discretion.
</Note>

Disagreements happen in competitive play — line calls, score confusion, and rule interpretations are a normal part of the game. Club Circuit provides a clear process for resolving disputes fairly, whether they are handled on the spot or escalated through the platform. Knowing this process before a dispute arises helps you respond calmly, protect your team's interests, and keep matches moving with minimum disruption.

## Handling On-Court Disputes

The first step in any dispute is always to attempt a good-faith resolution between the players involved. Most disagreements — a contested line call, uncertainty about the score, or a rules question — can and should be resolved on the court.

### Attempt resolution between players first

- State your concern clearly and calmly. Explain what you saw or what you believe the correct rule to be.
- Listen to your opponent's perspective. If both sides present a reasonable case and neither can be confirmed, **replay the point**. A replayed point is always a fair and acceptable resolution when certainty is not possible.
- Do not delay the game with extended arguments. If you cannot reach agreement within a reasonable time, replay the point and move on.

### Call for a league official if available

If your match is being supervised by a league official or referee, call them to the court before continuing play. Do not attempt to resolve a serious rules dispute without an official present if one is available.

- Signal the official clearly and wait for them to approach.
- Both players should present their positions calmly and without interruption.
- The official's on-court ruling is final for that point. You may still file a formal dispute afterward if you believe the ruling was in error.

<Note>
  Not all Club Circuit divisions include on-site officials. Check your division's format in the platform to understand what level of oversight applies to your matches.
</Note>

## Filing a Formal Dispute Through Club Circuit

If an on-court dispute cannot be resolved — or if you believe a match result was affected by a rules violation, misconduct, or scoring error — you may file a formal dispute through the Club Circuit platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to your match record">
    Log in to Club Circuit and go to **Schedule → Match History**. Locate the match in question and open the match detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'File a Dispute'">
    On the match detail page, click the **File a Dispute** button. This option is available for 24 hours after the official match end time. Disputes filed after this window may not be accepted unless extenuating circumstances are documented.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue clearly">
    Complete the dispute form with a factual description of what occurred. Include the specific point, game, or moment in question; the rule you believe was violated; and the outcome you are requesting. Attach any supporting evidence — photos, screenshots of score records, or witness names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and confirm receipt">
    Submit the form and confirm you receive a case number by email. Save this case number — you will need it to track the dispute and reference it in any follow-up communication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cooperate with the review">
    Your league administrator may contact you for additional information. Respond promptly and honestly. Failure to respond within the requested timeframe may result in your dispute being closed without a ruling in your favor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the League Administrator Reviews

When a dispute is submitted, your league administrator evaluates the case based on available evidence and Club Circuit rules. Administrators review:

- The match score record submitted by both teams
- Any discrepancies between the two teams' reported scores
- The specific rule or situation cited in the dispute
- Supporting evidence submitted by either party
- Relevant precedent from USAPA rules or Club Circuit league guidelines
- The conduct and history of both players or teams involved, where applicable

The administrator does not take sides — they evaluate the facts presented. Submitting clear, factual, and well-documented disputes gives your case the best chance of a fair review.

## Dispute Outcomes

After completing the review, the league administrator will issue one of the following rulings:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Dispute Upheld" icon="circle-check">
    The administrator finds sufficient evidence to support your claim. The match result may be corrected, a point or game awarded, or a rules violation formally recorded.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dispute Overturned" icon="circle-xmark">
    The administrator finds insufficient evidence to support your claim, or determines the original outcome was correct. The match result stands as recorded.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Replay Ordered" icon="rotate">
    In cases where the dispute cannot be definitively resolved and the outcome of the match may have been materially affected, the administrator may order the match or affected portion to be replayed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

All dispute outcomes are communicated to both parties in writing through the Club Circuit platform. The ruling will include a brief explanation of the decision.

## Frivolous Disputes

<Warning>
  Disputes must be submitted in good faith. Repeated submission of disputes that lack factual basis or supporting evidence — particularly disputes used as a tactic to delay results or pressure opponents — may result in a **formal sportsmanship warning**. A second warning within the same season may lead to suspension from league play. File a dispute when you genuinely believe a rules violation or error affected your match, not as a competitive strategy.
</Warning>

## Resolution Timeline

Club Circuit aims to resolve all formal disputes **within 48 hours** of submission. Complex cases involving replay requests, multiple witnesses, or conduct violations may take longer. You will receive a status update if a dispute extends beyond the 48-hour window.

| Stage | Typical Timeframe |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of submission | Within 2 hours |
| Administrator review begins | Within 12 hours |
| Resolution issued | Within 48 hours |
| Replay scheduled (if ordered) | Within 5 business days |

## Appeals Process

<Note>
  If you disagree with the administrator's ruling on your dispute, you may submit a **formal appeal** within 24 hours of receiving the decision. Navigate to your dispute case in the Club Circuit platform and select **Appeal This Ruling**. Appeals are reviewed by a separate administrator or a designated league oversight committee — not the original reviewer.

  An appeal must include new information or a specific argument that the original review misapplied the rules. Appeals that simply restate the original dispute without new grounds will not be accepted. The outcome of the appeal is final.
</Note>

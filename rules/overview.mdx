The Club Circuit Pickleball rulebook establishes the official standards for competitive play across all leagues on the platform. Whether you are a first-time league participant or a seasoned competitive player, these rules define how matches are conducted, how disputes are resolved, and what standards of conduct are expected of every player. Familiarizing yourself with this rulebook before your first match is not optional — it is your responsibility as a registered participant.

<Note>
  These rules apply to all Club Circuit leagues unless your league organizer has explicitly configured custom rules for your specific league. When custom rules are in effect, those rules take precedence over the defaults described here. Check your league dashboard for any organizer-defined rule modifications.
</Note>

## Rule Updates and Versioning

Club Circuit reviews and updates league rules at the start of each new season. When a rule change is introduced, the affected section of this rulebook is updated and a version note is added to reflect the change date. You can find the current rulebook version number in the footer of each rules page.

If your league is mid-season when a rule update is published, the updated rules take effect at the start of the following season unless your league organizer applies them immediately. Your league dashboard will display a banner notification whenever a rule change has been published.

To review the history of rule changes, visit the **Rule Changelog** section in your league organizer's settings panel, or contact Club Circuit support.

<Info>
  You are responsible for knowing and following the current version of these rules. Claiming unfamiliarity with a rule is not grounds for overturning a match result or disciplinary decision. Review this rulebook at the start of every season and any time you receive a rule update notification.
</Info>

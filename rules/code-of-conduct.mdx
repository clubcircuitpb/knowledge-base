Club Circuit exists to provide a competitive, inclusive, and enjoyable pickleball league experience for players of every skill level. The quality of that experience depends entirely on the behavior of every person who participates. This Code of Conduct sets the minimum standard of behavior expected of all registered players, coaches, and team representatives — on the court, at the facility, and in all platform communications. You are responsible for understanding and upholding these standards at all times during your participation in Club Circuit leagues.

<Info>
  By completing registration for any Club Circuit league, you confirm that you have read and agreed to this Code of Conduct in full. Failure to comply is grounds for disciplinary action, up to and including permanent removal from the league.
</Info>

## Core Principles

Club Circuit's conduct standards are built on four principles that guide how every player is expected to behave:

- **Respect** — Treat every player, official, facility staff member, and spectator with dignity, regardless of skill level, score, or outcome. Disagreements are handled through proper channels, not through confrontation.
- **Integrity** — Play honestly. Make accurate line calls, report scores correctly, and compete within the rules. The spirit of the game matters as much as the letter of the rules.
- **Inclusion** — Club Circuit welcomes players of all backgrounds, abilities, ages, and identities. Exclusionary behavior of any kind — whether overt or subtle — has no place in our league.
- **Safety** — Protect the physical and emotional safety of everyone at the facility. This includes safe play on the court, responsible conduct in shared spaces, and immediate reporting of any unsafe situation.

## Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviors are prohibited in all Club Circuit activities — including matches, warm-ups, league communications, and platform interactions:

- **Verbal harassment** — yelling at, belittling, or using derogatory language toward any player, official, or spectator.
- **Threatening language** — any statement, written or spoken, that a reasonable person would interpret as a threat of harm.
- **Physical aggression** — pushing, striking, throwing equipment, or any physical contact made in anger or intimidation.
- **Discrimination** — treating any individual differently or making derogatory remarks based on race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, nationality, age, or disability.
- **Deliberate cheating** — intentional misreporting of scores, knowingly using illegal equipment, or manipulating match results.
- **Unsportsmanlike conduct** — excessive gloating, taunting opponents, prolonged arguing, or behavior designed to intimidate or destabilize your opponent.
- **Misuse of league communications** — sending abusive, threatening, or harassing messages through the Club Circuit platform's messaging or dispute systems.

<Warning>
  Engaging in prohibited conduct — even once — may result in immediate disciplinary action. You do not receive a warning before consequences are applied to serious violations.
</Warning>

## Zero-Tolerance Policy

Certain behaviors result in **immediate suspension from league play** pending a full investigation. No warning is issued before suspension in these cases. Zero-tolerance violations include:

- Any form of physical aggression toward another player, official, or facility staff member
- Directed hate speech or discriminatory slurs of any kind
- Threatening another player with physical harm — verbally, in writing, or through gesture
- Deliberately tampering with another team's equipment or match records
- Retaliating against a player who submitted a conduct report in good faith

A zero-tolerance suspension removes you from all active matches immediately. You will be notified in writing through the Club Circuit platform. Matches scheduled during your suspension will be forfeited to your opponents.

## Reporting a Conduct Violation

If you witness or experience conduct that violates this Code, report it through the Club Circuit platform. Do not attempt to confront the person yourself — report the incident and allow the league to handle it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Document what happened">
    As soon as it is safe to do so, write down exactly what occurred — the time, location, people involved, what was said or done, and any witnesses present. Accurate, specific details strengthen your report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Conduct Report form">
    Log in to Club Circuit and navigate to **Support → Report a Conduct Violation**. Select the match or event associated with the incident, or indicate that it occurred outside of a match context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete and submit the report">
    Fill out the form with the details you documented. You may submit your report anonymously if you prefer, though providing your name allows the administrator to follow up if clarification is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preserve any evidence">
    If the violation occurred in platform messaging, take screenshots before submitting your report. Screenshots submitted with the form are reviewed as part of the investigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await confirmation">
    You will receive a confirmation that your report was received. If you provided your contact information, you may be contacted for additional details. You will receive a notification when the investigation is closed, though specific disciplinary details may remain confidential.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disciplinary Process

All conduct reports are reviewed by a Club Circuit league administrator. The process follows these stages:

### Investigation

Upon receiving a report, the administrator reviews the submitted details, contacts any named witnesses, and may request a statement from the reported player. Both parties are expected to cooperate fully and promptly. The investigation is conducted confidentially — participants should not discuss the case publicly or with other players while it is open.

### Outcome

After completing the investigation, the administrator issues one of the following outcomes:

| Outcome | Description |
|---|---|
| **No action** | Insufficient evidence to confirm a violation occurred. Report is logged but no disciplinary measure is applied. |
| **Formal warning** | A violation is confirmed but does not warrant suspension. A formal warning is added to the player's record. Two formal warnings in a season may result in suspension. |
| **Match forfeiture** | One or more match results are overturned due to conduct-related violations. |
| **Temporary suspension** | The player is suspended from league play for a defined period, typically one to four weeks depending on severity. |
| **Permanent removal** | The player is removed from Club Circuit leagues indefinitely. Applied to severe or repeated violations. |

### Appeal

A player who receives a formal warning, suspension, or removal may appeal the decision within **48 hours** of receiving the outcome notification. To file an appeal, navigate to **Support → My Conduct Cases** and select **Appeal This Decision**. Appeals must include a specific reason why the original decision was incorrect — they are not an opportunity to restate your original account. Appeals are reviewed by a different administrator or oversight committee and are final.

<Note>
  The league administrator retains discretion to apply conduct measures appropriate to circumstances not explicitly listed in this Code of Conduct. The intent of this Code is to protect the integrity and safety of the league — administrators will act in accordance with that intent, even in situations that do not fit neatly into a defined category.
</Note>

## A Note on Sportsmanship

The rules in this Code of Conduct represent the floor — the minimum standard of acceptable behavior. Club Circuit encourages you to go further: congratulate your opponents after a hard-fought match, acknowledge great shots from the other side of the net, and help newer players feel welcome in the league. The players who make Club Circuit a great community to be part of are the ones who bring more to the court than just their game.

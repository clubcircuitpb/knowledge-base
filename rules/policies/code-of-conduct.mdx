**The short version:** call your lines fairly, respect your opponents and the host club, and keep team chat civil. If something goes wrong, report it to ClubCircuit. ClubCircuit decides what happens, from a warning up to removal from the league, and you can appeal once.

This policy applies in every ClubCircuit league. Your league's rulebook adds the penalties that cost games during a match.

## What we expect on court

- **Call it fairly.** Make line calls on your side honestly. When you're not sure, the ball is in.
- **Respect everyone.** Opponents, teammates, the host club's staff and other players at the venue. No abusive language, threats, or thrown paddles or balls.
- **Follow the host club's rules.** It's their building. Their staff can stop play that isn't safe.
- **Leave the courts as you found them.** Take your bottles, balls and rubbish with you.
- **Captains pass it on.** Your captain makes sure the team knows this policy.

## What we expect off court

Team chat and messages follow the same standard. Don't harass or threaten anyone, send discriminatory content or spam, pretend to be someone else, or share another player's private details. ClubCircuit can remove messages and limit who can use chat. {/* src: leagues-app src/lib/legal/content.ts, Terms of Use §9 */}

## Who's responsible at the venue?

The host club runs its building: access, safety, staff and the courts. The host has the courts, balls and a contact person ready. Each club answers for its own players' and guests' behaviour. ClubCircuit makes the competitive and disciplinary decisions. {/* src: Club Participation Agreement §3, §6; decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 (item 3) */}

## How do I report a problem?

- **Players in the match:** open the match in ClubCircuit and tap **Report a conduct problem**. You can do this from the match's start until the time your season's rules set; the match page says until when. Only the league office sees your report. See [Report a conduct problem](/players/report-a-conduct-problem).
- **Anyone else, or after that time:** email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) with the match, what happened and who was there.

Any player, captain or club admin can report. Report as soon as you can after the match, while people still remember it clearly.

## What happens after a report?

ClubCircuit reviews it, hears from the people involved, and aims to decide within 7 days. If someone at ClubCircuit was involved, someone else decides. {/* src: Club Participation Agreement §6; decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 (item 6) */}

## What are the consequences?

They usually go in steps:

1. **Warning.**
2. **Probation** for the rest of the season. Another problem moves you to the next step.
3. **Suspension** for one or more matches.
4. **Removal** from the league.

Serious incidents can go straight to suspension or removal. These include threats, violence, harassment, fraud, and manipulating ratings or results. {/* src: decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 (item 4); Terms of Use §21 (grounds for suspension) */}

## Can I appeal?

Yes, once. Email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) with why you think the decision is wrong. Send it within the appeal window in your league's rulebook. The decision on your appeal is final. {/* src: decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 (item 7); Ruleset.appealWindowHours */}

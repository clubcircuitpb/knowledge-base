**The short version:** a club enters teams, names a captain for each one, and hosts its home matches. Each team must reach the division's minimum roster by registration close. A club can only leave mid-season by agreement with ClubCircuit, and the team's results are handled as set out below.

This policy applies in every ClubCircuit league. The roster numbers are in your league's rulebook.

## What a club commits to

- Name an admin and a captain for each team.
- Keep its club page, venue and court details accurate.
- Host its home matches on the courts it confirmed, and give visiting players access on the terms it shared.
- Look after safety at its venue, and tell ClubCircuit about closures or incidents.

There's no fee for a club to take part. {/* src: Club Participation Agreement §3; Club Partnership Deck ("No participation fee" for clubs); leagues-app ClubSeasonEnrollment.feeCents (zero at launch) */}

## Roster rules

Each division has a minimum and maximum roster size, and can set limits for each gender. A team below the minimum can't be confirmed. Every team must reach the minimum by the time registration closes, or its place can go to another club. {/* src: Division.rosterMin, rosterMax, rosterMinPerGender, rosterMaxPerGender; src/lib/rules/codes.ts ROSTER_MINIMUM; decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 (item 16) */}

## Will my division run?

A division runs once it has enough teams. ClubCircuit confirms or cancels each division by a set date. If it's cancelled, registration fees are refunded under the [Refund Policy](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/legal/refunds). {/* src: Division.minTeamsToRun, goLiveDecisionBy, formationStatus; Club Participation Agreement §5 */}

## Team names

A team plays under its club's name, plus a short name of its own if the club wants one. Teams from clubs without their own courts still play under their club's name. ClubCircuit can refuse a name that's offensive. {/* src: decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 (item 14) */}

## Leaving during a season

A club can only withdraw a team mid-season by agreement with ClubCircuit. When a team leaves:

- **Before playing half its matches:** all its results come out of the standings, as if it never played.
- **After that:** its results stand, and its remaining matches are recorded as forfeits.

Registration fees aren't refunded unless the [Refund Policy](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/legal/refunds) says so. {/* src: Club Participation Agreement §12 (withdrawal during an active season requires written agreement); decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 (item 15) */}

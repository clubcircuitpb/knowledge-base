**The short version:** regular players pay a registration fee once a season. Captains can register free. Subs join the pool free and pay a sub registration fee only when they first play in a division. Everyone, subs included, pays a court share for the matches they're in the lineup for, and nothing for matches they aren't.

This policy explains how each fee works in every ClubCircuit league. The amounts are set each season. For the winter season, see [Rally to 21 league: fees](/rules/rally-21/fees). You always see the exact amount in the app before you pay.

## Registration fee

A one-time fee for the season, paid when you register. It isn't a subscription and doesn't renew. {/* src: leagues-app src/lib/legal/content.ts, Terms of Use §10; Season.registrationPriceCents, Division.registrationPriceCents */}

## Captains

Each team can have a set number of free captain registrations, usually one. Your club names its captain. {/* src: Ruleset.freeCaptainsPerTeam (default 1); Club Participation Agreement §1 (one complimentary captain registration per team) */}

## Substitutes

Joining the sub pool is free. A sub pays a sub registration fee for a division only when they first play there:

1. A captain asks you to sub, and you say yes.
2. You pay that division's sub fee.
3. Your spot is confirmed, and the captain can put you in the lineup.

The fee covers a set number of matches in that division. To play more, you join a roster, and the sub fee you paid comes off your registration fee. If the league cancels the match you paid for and you have nothing else in that division, you get the fee back.

The sub registration fee doesn't cover courts. Subs pay a court share for each match they're in the lineup for, like everyone else. {/* src: docs/adr/0058-substitute-activation-per-division.md (pay at first confirmed placement; credit toward registration; refund on cancellation); Ruleset.subActivationFeeCents, maxSubAppearancesPerTeam, subActivationCreditsRegistration; decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 */}

## Court shares

Clubs charge for their courts on match day. You pay only for matches you're in the lineup for. Usually the court share is charged when the lineups lock, to each player your captain's posted lineup puts in at least one game. You don't pay if you're on the bench, only said you're available, or were in a lineup your captain never posted. If you're swapped out on the day, the league office refunds you. The fee is charged to your saved card, or you get a pay link. See [Pay a court fee and see what you’ve paid](/players/pay-court-fees-and-see-payments).

If your club charges each team a total for its courts, that total is split evenly among the team's players who pay. Other clubs charge a set price per player, or split one amount for the match. The match page shows how your host club charges.

An unpaid court fee that goes overdue has to be paid before you can register for another season. {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/registration.ts (COURT_FEE_UNPAID) */}

## Refunds

Registration fees follow the [Refund Policy](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/legal/refunds). In short, you get a refund if we can't place you on a team, or if your division doesn't go ahead. Fees you pay a club directly follow that club's own policy. {/* src: Terms of Use §12; Refund Policy */}

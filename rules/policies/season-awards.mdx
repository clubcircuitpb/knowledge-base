**The short version:** each division has four awards, worked out from the season's results. Nobody votes. To win a player award, you need to have played enough of your team's games.

## The awards

- **Division champion:** the team that wins the final. If there's no final, the team in first place when the season ends.
- **Player of the season:** the division's top-ranked player.
- **Most improved:** the biggest rise in DUPR rating from the start of the season to the end.
- **Iron player:** whoever played the most rounds.

Ties go to the higher-ranked player. {/* src: leagues-app src/lib/season/awards.ts:1-20 */}

## Who can win?

To win a player award, you must have played a minimum share of your team's games. Your league's rulebook sets the share. {/* src: Ruleset.awardsMinGamesPct */}

If your profile is set to **Private**, you're never named for an award, and it goes to the next player down. {/* src: src/lib/season/awards.ts (PRIVATE players never named) */}

## Where to see them

Awards show on your division's season pages in ClubCircuit. Each one links to the results it came from. {/* src: src/lib/season/awards.ts ("each links to where it came from") */}

## All-Stars

There's no All-Star selection in the first season. {/* src: decision, Larry 2 Nov 2026 (item 18) */}

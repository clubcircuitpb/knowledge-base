Eligibility rules exist to keep Club Circuit leagues fair, competitive, and safe for every participant. Before you register for a league — as an individual player or as part of a team — confirm that you meet all requirements for the division you are entering. Registering for a league you are not eligible for may result in your removal from that league without a refund of any registration fees.

## Age Requirements

Club Circuit leagues are open to players who are **18 years of age or older** by the registration deadline of the league. This is the default age minimum for all standard adult leagues on the platform.

Some leagues may offer **youth divisions** for players under 18. If a youth division is available in your area, it will appear separately in the league browser with its own age bracket and eligibility criteria. Youth division participants must have a parent or legal guardian complete the registration and consent process on their behalf.

If you have questions about age eligibility for a specific league, contact your league organizer directly through the Club Circuit messaging system.

## Skill Division Eligibility

Club Circuit uses a verified skill rating system to ensure players compete at an appropriate level. You must register for a division that is **within one rating level** of your current verified skill rating.

- Your verified skill rating is displayed on your player profile.
- You may play **up** one rating level from your verified rating.
- You may **not** play down a rating level from your verified rating.
- If you do not yet have a verified rating, you will be assigned a provisional rating during registration based on your self-assessment and any available match history.

Skill ratings are reviewed at the end of each season. If your performance data indicates that your current rating no longer reflects your skill level, Club Circuit may adjust your rating before the next season begins.

## Team Eligibility

Teams must meet the following requirements to be eligible to compete in a Club Circuit league:

- **Minimum roster size:** Every team must have at least 2 registered players for doubles leagues. Singles leagues require individual registration only.
- **Maximum roster size:** Roster limits vary by league. Check your specific league settings for the maximum number of players allowed per team.
- **Mixed division requirements:** In mixed-gender divisions, teams must include at least one player of each gender as defined by league organizer settings.
- **Skill balance in mixed skill divisions:** In leagues that allow mixed skill levels on a single team, no team may roster more than one player rated more than two levels above the division's stated skill floor.

All team members must be individually registered and have accepted the Club Circuit participant agreement before they appear as eligible on your team roster.

## Registration Deadlines

Registration deadlines are set by the league organizer and are visible on the league's detail page. Club Circuit enforces these deadlines strictly:

- **Player registration:** You must complete your registration, including payment of any fees, before the deadline to be included in the season schedule.
- **Roster locks:** Once the roster lock date passes, you cannot add new players to your team or make changes to your registered lineup. The roster lock date is displayed on your team management page.
- **Late registration:** Some leagues allow late registration at the organizer's discretion. If late registration is available, it will appear as an option in the league browser after the standard deadline has passed.

<Warning>
  Playing in an incorrect skill division — whether by misrepresenting your rating or registering outside your eligible range — is a serious violation of Club Circuit rules. If discovered before or during a season, you risk **immediate disqualification** from the league, forfeiture of all match results, and a temporary ban from future league registration. Do not register for a division you are not eligible for.
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I play up a skill level?">
    Yes. You are permitted to register for a division that is **one level above** your verified skill rating. Playing up is allowed because it presents a greater challenge rather than an unfair advantage over other players. However, you may not register two or more levels above your verified rating. If you want to play at a higher level permanently, focus on improving your rating through match performance so that your verified rating advances naturally.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I transfer to a different team mid-season?">
    Team transfers mid-season are only allowed under specific circumstances and must be approved by your league organizer. To request a transfer, navigate to your player profile, select **Team Settings**, and submit a transfer request. The organizer will review your request and respond within the timeframe specified in your league rules. Transfers are generally not permitted after the roster lock date. Any match results recorded before your transfer remain attributed to your original team.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are there guest player rules?">
    Some leagues allow teams to use guest players when their registered roster cannot field a full lineup for a scheduled match. Guest player rules vary by league — check your league's settings page to see whether guests are permitted, how many may play per match, and what skill rating restrictions apply to guests. A guest player must be registered on the Club Circuit platform and must not be rostered on a competing team in the same league. Results from matches involving guest players count toward the team's standing unless the organizer has configured otherwise.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Some clubs charge a court fee when you play on their courts. To pay court fees automatically, open **Payments** from **Your account** and tap **Save a card**. Without a saved card, we email you a pay link, or you can open **Payments** and tap **Pay** next to the fee.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/me/page.tsx:298; src/app/me/payments/page.tsx:32,80-90,99-101 */}

**Before you begin**

- Only clubs that collect their court fee through ClubCircuit charge you here.
- Your card is kept by Stripe. ClubCircuit never sees the number.

{/* src: src/app/me/payments/page.tsx:97,103,111 */}

## Save a card for court fees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your account">
    Open your account menu and tap **Account**.
    {/* src: src/components/shell/account-menu.tsx:143-146 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Payments">
    Tap **Payments**.
    {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:298 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a card">
    Under **Card for court fees**, tap **Save a card**.
    {/* src: payments/page.tsx:92,107 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your card with Stripe">
    Enter your card on the Stripe page that opens, and confirm. You come back to **Payments** when you're done.
    {/* src: src/app/me/payments/actions.ts:23 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** Your card shows under **Card for court fees**. When you play at a club that collects through ClubCircuit, its fee is charged to this card and you get a receipt. {/* src: payments/page.tsx:95-97 */}

## Pay a fee yourself

If you have a fee to pay, **Payments** shows **A court fee to pay** with a **Pay** button for each one, showing the amount, the club and the date. Tap it, check the amount, then tap **Pay** on the next page. You can also tap the pay link in the email we send you. {/* src: payments/page.tsx:70,78-82; src/app/pay/court-fee/[token]/page.tsx:73,96-98 */}

## Pay on time

Each season sets how long you have to pay. If a fee goes overdue, your captain can't place you in a lineup until it's paid, and you can't register for a new season. Pay it and you're available again straight away. Check your season's rules in the app for the grace period. {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/lineup.ts:251-260 (COURT_FEE_OVERDUE); src/lib/rules/rules/registration.ts:39 (COURT_FEE_UNPAID); Ruleset.courtFeeGraceDays; pay/court-fee/[token]/page.tsx:78 */}

## What each status means

- **Charging:** Your saved card is being charged.
- **Paid:** Done. Your receipt is in your email.
- **To pay:** You owe this fee. Pay it before the grace period ends.
- **Overdue:** Past the grace period. Pay it to get back into lineups.
- **Waived:** You don't owe this fee.
- **Refunded:** Your money was returned.

## Why isn't this working?

### My card was declined
**Cause:** Your bank turned the charge down. The reason shows under the fee's status.
**Fix:** Tap **Replace card** to save a different card, or tap **Pay** next to the fee and pay with another card. {/* src: payments/page.tsx:90,138 (lastError) */}

### "Thanks — Stripe has your payment and we’re waiting on confirmation"
**Cause:** Stripe is still confirming your payment. This usually takes a few seconds.
**Fix:** Refresh the page in a moment. Your receipt arrives by email. {/* src: pay/court-fee/[token]/page.tsx:82-84 */}

### "There’s nothing to pay on this court fee."
**Cause:** The fee was already paid, waived or cancelled.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. Check **Payments** to see its status. {/* src: pay/court-fee/[token]/page.tsx:77-80 */}

### I want to stop automatic charges
**Fix:** On **Payments**, tap **Remove card**. You'll get a pay link by email for each new fee instead. {/* src: payments/page.tsx:99-101,110-114 */}

### I think I was charged twice or charged by mistake
**Fix:** Email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) with the club and the date of the match.

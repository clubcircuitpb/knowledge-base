To see your badges, open your account menu and tap **Court Cred**. It shows your newest badges, the two you're closest to and where you stand this season. Tap **See all** for every badge and how to earn it. Badges (also called achievements) are awarded on their own after each confirmed match and each weekly DUPR refresh.

{/* src: leagues-app src/components/shell/account-control.tsx (Court Cred row); src/app/me/court-cred/page.tsx; src/app/me/court-cred/badges/page.tsx ("How badges work") */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be signed in as a player.
- Badges count *games*. A game is one pairing on one court in one round. A *match* is the whole evening against the other club.

{/* src: src/app/me/court-cred/guard.ts (requirePlayer); src/lib/badges/registry.ts (badges count games; ADR 0075 §1) */}

## See your badges

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Court Cred">
    Open your account menu and tap **Court Cred**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen first. It's also on your **Profile**.
    {/* src: account-control.tsx (personal rows); src/app/me/profile/page.tsx (Court Cred link) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what's new">
    A new badge shows at the top as **New badge unlocked**. Under **Badges**, you'll see the ones you've earned and the two you're closest to, with how far along you are.
    {/* src: src/app/me/court-cred/page.tsx (banner, Badges panel, closest two with progress) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="See every badge">
    Tap **See all** to open the badge guide. Tap **Earned**, **In progress** or **Locked** to filter it.
    {/* src: page.tsx ("{earned} of {total} · See all"); badges/page.tsx (FILTERS: All, Earned, In progress, Locked) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a badge">
    Tap any badge to see what it's for. An earned badge shows when you earned it and the match it came from. One you haven't earned yet shows **Not earned yet** and how far along you are.
    {/* src: src/app/me/court-cred/badges/[badgeKey]/page.tsx */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You can see every badge you hold and what it takes to earn the next one. {/* src: badges/page.tsx */}

## Choose the badges on your card

Your card shows up to three badges. Until you choose, it shows your latest best ones.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an earned badge">
    In **Court Cred**, tap a badge you've earned.
    {/* src: src/components/badges/badge-tile.tsx */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin it">
    Tap **Pin to my card**. To take it off, open it again and tap **Unpin from my card**.
    {/* src: [badgeKey]/page.tsx; src/lib/badges/pins.ts (MAX_PINS) */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** The badge shows under **On your card** in **Court Cred**. {/* src: src/app/me/court-cred/page.tsx ("On your card") */}

## How badges work

- Badges update after every confirmed match.
- Rating badges update with the weekly DUPR refresh.
- Badges follow your stats: if a match result is corrected, your badges are recalculated.
- Rating milestones stay earned even if your rating later dips.
- Season honors (your finish in the player standings, your team's placement and an unbeaten season) are awarded when the season closes. To earn a league finish, you need to have played in enough of your team's matches to be ranked in the player standings.

## Why isn't this working?

### I played but didn't get a badge
**Cause:** Only confirmed matches count, and rating badges wait for the weekly DUPR refresh. Rating badges also need a DUPR rating on file.
**Fix:** Ask your captain to check the score was entered and confirmed. For rating badges, check your DUPR ID is on your profile. {/* src: src/lib/badges/load.ts (CONFIRMED only); src/lib/dupr/checks.ts (rating badges after readings) */}

### A badge disappeared
**Cause:** A match result was corrected, and the badge's rule isn't met any more. **Court Cred** says "A match result was corrected, so your badges were updated." If the badge was on your card, it comes off. Rating milestones never disappear.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. If the correction is put right, the badge comes back. {/* src: src/app/me/court-cred/page.tsx (correction note); src/lib/badges/run.ts (revoke and unpin); src/lib/badges/diff.ts (rating badges permanent) */}

### "Your card shows 3 badges. Unpin one first, then pin this one."
**Cause:** Your card already has three badges pinned.
**Fix:** Open one of them, tap **Unpin from my card**, then pin the new one. {/* src: src/lib/badges/pins.ts */}

### A badge just says "???"
**Cause:** It's a hidden badge. You see a hint until you earn it.
**Fix:** Follow the hint. {/* src: badges/page.tsx oneTimeBlock (hidden) */}

### I don't get told when I earn a badge
**Cause:** Badge notifications are switched off.
**Fix:** Open **Notifications** and switch **Badges** on. To get them on your phone too, switch on **Push notifications on this device** under **Push**. {/* src: src/lib/notifications/categories.ts (BADGES); src/app/me/notifications/page.tsx:157 ("Push"); src/components/pwa/push-toggle.tsx:89 */}

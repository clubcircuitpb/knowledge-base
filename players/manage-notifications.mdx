To choose which notifications you get, open your account menu and tap **Notifications**. For each kind of notification, pick how you get it, or switch it off, then tap **Save preferences**. Quiet hours and push notifications are on the same page.

{/* src: leagues-app src/components/shell/account-control.tsx:127; src/app/me/notifications/page.tsx:96-126 */}

**Before you begin**

- Some emails always come, whatever you choose: sign-in links, payment receipts, registration approvals, and news about a division being confirmed or cancelled.
- The list only shows notifications that apply to you. Captains and club admins see a few more.

{/* src: page.tsx:160-165; src/lib/notifications/categories.ts (categoriesForRoles) */}

## Choose your notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    Open your account menu and tap **Notifications**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen first.
    {/* src: account-control.tsx:127 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how you get each one">
    For each kind, such as **My matches** or **League news**, choose **In-app and email**, **In-app only**, **Email only** or **Off**. Some are a single box: tick it to get the email.
    {/* src: page.tsx:96-121; categories.ts */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save preferences**.
    {/* src: page.tsx:126 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Saved.** at the top of the page. {/* src: page.tsx:60-66 */}

## Set quiet hours

Tick **Quiet hours**, set **From** and **Until**, then tap **Save quiet hours**. During those hours only what can't wait, like your lineup or a payment receipt, reaches your phone or inbox. Everything else waits in your ClubCircuit inbox. Times use your device's time zone. {/* src: src/app/me/notifications/quiet-hours-form.tsx:24-48 */}

## Get push notifications on this phone

Under **Push**, tap **Enable push on this device** and allow notifications when your phone asks. You get timely alerts, like a match change or a sub opening. Do this on each phone or computer you use. To stop, tap **Turn off on this device**. {/* src: src/components/pwa/push-toggle.tsx:89-120 */}

## Direct messages

At the top of the page, tap **Turn on DMs** to let people in your league message you. Your teammates and captain can always message you. Tap **Turn off DMs** to stop. {/* src: page.tsx:57-72 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "You’ve blocked notifications for this site."
**Cause:** Your browser or phone has notifications turned off for ClubCircuit.
**Fix:** Turn them back on in your browser or phone settings, then reload the page. {/* src: push-toggle.tsx:101-103 */}

### "This browser doesn’t support push."
**Cause:** Some browsers can't get push notifications. On an iPhone, push only works once ClubCircuit is added to your Home Screen.
**Fix:** Add ClubCircuit to your Home Screen and open it from there, or rely on email. {/* src: push-toggle.tsx:17,97 */}

### I turned off emails but still get some
**Cause:** Sign-in links, receipts, registration approvals and division news always come. They keep you and your money safe.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. These can't be switched off. {/* src: page.tsx:160-165 */}

### I stopped getting notifications at night
**Cause:** Quiet hours are on.
**Fix:** Untick **Quiet hours** and tap **Save quiet hours**, or change the times. {/* src: quiet-hours-form.tsx */}

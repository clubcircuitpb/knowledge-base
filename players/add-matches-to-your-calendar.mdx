To add every match to your calendar, open your team's **Schedule** and tap **Subscribe (.ics)**. Tap **Subscribe to my matches**, then pick your calendar app. Your matches show up with the date, time, venue and directions, and update themselves if a match moves or is cancelled.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/[market]/[season]/schedule/page.tsx:85-87; src/components/calendar/subscribe-panel.tsx:60-88 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be on a team's roster. Your club puts you on a team after you register.

{/* src: src/app/[market]/[season]/schedule/page.tsx:84 (button shows only with myTeam) */}

## Subscribe to all your matches

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team's schedule">
    Open **Schedule** and choose **Your team**.
    {/* src: src/lib/nav/play.ts:43; schedule/page.tsx */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Subscribe (.ics)">
    Tap **Subscribe (.ics)** at the top. This opens **Your matches in your calendar**.
    {/* src: schedule/page.tsx:85-87; subscribe-panel.tsx:60 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on your calendar link">
    Tap **Subscribe to my matches**. You only do this once.
    {/* src: subscribe-panel.tsx:88 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your calendar">
    Tap **Apple Calendar (iPhone, Mac)** or **Google Calendar**. For Outlook or another app, tap **Copy link (Outlook & others)** and paste the link into your calendar's "add calendar from URL" option.
    {/* src: subscribe-panel.tsx:62-70 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in your calendar app">
    Your calendar app asks you to confirm the subscription. Say yes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** Every match you're scheduled for shows up in your calendar, and changes on its own when a match moves or is cancelled. {/* src: subscribe-panel.tsx:63-65; src/lib/calendar/feed.ts */}

## Add just one match

On a match's page, tap **Add to calendar** to save that one match, or **Google Calendar** to add it there. This saves a copy, so it won't update if the match moves. {/* src: src/components/calendar/add-to-calendar.tsx; src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:202-204,530 */}

## Keep your link private

Your calendar link is private to you. Don't share it. If it gets out, tap **reset it** under the buttons. Any calendar using the old link stops updating, so subscribe again with the new one. {/* src: subscribe-panel.tsx:28,38,73-83 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### I don't see Subscribe (.ics)
**Cause:** The button only shows on your own team's schedule.
**Fix:** Choose **Your team** on the **Schedule** page. If you're not on a team yet, it appears once your club adds you. {/* src: schedule/page.tsx:84 */}

### My calendar didn't change when a match moved
**Cause:** Calendar apps check for updates on their own timetable, and some take hours. A single match saved with **Add to calendar** never updates.
**Fix:** Wait, or refresh the calendar in your app. If you saved one match, delete it and subscribe instead. {/* src: add-to-calendar.tsx (one-off .ics download) */}

### My matches stopped showing up
**Cause:** Your calendar link was reset, so the old one stopped working.
**Fix:** Remove the old ClubCircuit calendar from your app, then subscribe again. {/* src: subscribe-panel.tsx:28 */}

### I don't see Add to calendar on a match
**Cause:** It doesn't show for draft, bye or cancelled matches.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. It appears once the match is scheduled. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:202-204 */}

To add every match to your calendar, open your account menu, tap **Availability & calendar**, then tap **Subscribe to my matches** and pick your calendar app. You can also tap **Add to my calendar** on your team's **Schedule**. Your matches show up with the date, time, venue and directions, and update themselves when something changes.

**Before you begin**

- Your matches appear once you're on a team's roster, or once you've accepted a sub spot. Your club puts you on a team after you register.

{/* src: src/lib/calendar/events.ts:155-158 (personalEvents: active memberships and accepted sub spots) */}

## Subscribe to all your matches

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Availability & calendar">
    Open your account menu and tap **Availability & calendar**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen first. Or, on your team's **Schedule**, tap **Add to my calendar**.
    {/* src: src/lib/nav/personal-rows.ts:22; src/components/shell/play-chrome.tsx:386; src/app/[market]/[season]/schedule/page.tsx:83-89 (→ /me/availability#calendar) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Your matches in your calendar">
    Scroll to **Your matches in your calendar**, below your matches.
    {/* src: src/app/me/availability/page.tsx:75-77; src/components/calendar/subscribe-panel.tsx:60-62 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on your calendar link">
    Tap **Subscribe to my matches**. You only do this once.
    {/* src: src/components/calendar/subscribe-panel.tsx:96-99 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your calendar">
    Tap **Apple Calendar (iPhone, Mac)** or **Google Calendar**. For Outlook or another app, tap **Copy link (Outlook & others)** and paste the link into your calendar's "add calendar from URL" option.
    {/* src: src/components/calendar/subscribe-panel.tsx:70-81 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in your calendar app">
    Your calendar app asks you to confirm the subscription. Say yes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** Every match you're scheduled for shows up in your calendar. Once your team's lineup is in, each match also shows the rounds you're playing. {/* src: src/components/calendar/subscribe-panel.tsx:63-66; src/lib/calendar/events.ts:14-15,155-158 */}

## What updates on its own

Your calendar entries change when:

- A match moves or is cancelled
- The venue changes
- Your team's lineup is submitted or changed, so your rounds are up to date

Cancelled matches stay in your calendar marked as cancelled, so they don't silently linger. {/* src: src/lib/calendar/events.ts:18-19,60-74 (SEQUENCE raised for every change to an event's text, including venue and lineup) */}

## Add just one match

On a match's page, tap **Add to calendar** to save that one match, or **Google Calendar** to add it there. This saves a copy, so it won't update if the match changes. {/* src: src/components/calendar/add-to-calendar.tsx:14,19; src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:236,572 */}

## Keep your link private

Your calendar link is private to you. Don't share it. If it gets out, tap **reset it** under the buttons. Any calendar using the old link stops updating, so subscribe again with the new one. {/* src: src/components/calendar/subscribe-panel.tsx:35-50,82-93 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### I don't see Add to my calendar on the schedule
**Cause:** The button only shows when you're on a team in that season.
**Fix:** Open **Availability & calendar** from your account menu instead. Your matches appear once your club adds you to a team. {/* src: src/app/[market]/[season]/schedule/page.tsx:83-89 */}

### My calendar didn't change when a match moved
**Cause:** Calendar apps check for updates on their own timetable, and some take hours. A single match saved with **Add to calendar** never updates.
**Fix:** Wait, or refresh the calendar in your app. If you saved one match, delete it and subscribe instead. {/* src: src/components/calendar/add-to-calendar.tsx (one-off .ics download) */}

### My rounds aren't in the calendar entry
**Cause:** Your team's lineup hasn't been submitted yet.
**Fix:** Wait for your captain to post the lineup. The entry updates when they do. {/* src: src/lib/calendar/events.ts:155-158 */}

### My matches stopped showing up
**Cause:** Your calendar link was reset, so the old one stopped working.
**Fix:** Remove the old ClubCircuit calendar from your app, then subscribe again. {/* src: src/components/calendar/subscribe-panel.tsx:38 */}

### I don't see Add to calendar on a match
**Cause:** It doesn't show for draft, bye or cancelled matches.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. It appears once the match is scheduled. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:236,572; src/lib/calendar/events.ts:17-18 */}

This section is for anyone playing in a ClubCircuit season. You play for your home club, against other clubs in your market, and your playing history stays with you from season to season.

If you also captain a team or help run your club, those jobs have their own sections.

To get back here from the app, open your account menu and tap **Help**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen, then tap **Help**. You'll also find **Help** on **Your account**, just above **Sign out**. {/* src: leagues-app src/components/shell/account-menu.tsx:138-142 (computer, and Club HQ on a phone); src/components/shell/play-chrome.tsx:156-167 (initials open the account sheet), :459-467 (Help row, above Sign out); src/app/me/page.tsx:367-376 */}

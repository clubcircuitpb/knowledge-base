To tell your captain whether you can play, open **Home** in ClubCircuit and tap **I’m in**, **Maybe** or **Can’t** under **Can you play?** on your next match. Your answer saves as soon as you tap. You can also answer straight from the email we send before each match, without signing in.

{/* src: leagues-app src/components/match/next-match-card.tsx:88; src/components/availability-control.tsx:12-14 (chips post on tap, no save button) */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be on a team's roster. Your club puts you on a team after you register.
- Your captain builds the lineup from these answers (also called availability or an RSVP), so answer as early as you can.

{/* src: src/components/match/next-match-card.tsx (shown only to roster players); src/app/a/[token]/one-tap-recorder.tsx:44-45 ("Your captain sees this when building the lineup.") */}

## Answer in the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Sign in and open **Home**.
    {/* src: src/lib/nav/play.ts:49 (label "Home") */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Can you play? on your next match">
    On the card for your next match, find **Can you play?**
    {/* src: src/components/match/next-match-card.tsx:88 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap your answer">
    Tap **I’m in**, **Maybe** or **Can’t**. There's no save button. Your answer saves as soon as you tap.
    {/* src: src/components/availability-control.tsx:12-14 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** The card shows your answer in words, such as **You’re in** or **You can’t make it**. {/* src: src/components/match/next-match-card.tsx:22-25 */}

To answer for later matches, open **Schedule**, choose **Your team**, and tap **In**, **Maybe** or **Can’t** on each match. To see every upcoming match on one page, go to [app.clubcircuitpb.com/me/availability](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/me/availability). {/* src: src/lib/nav/play.ts:43 ("Schedule"); src/app/[market]/[season]/schedule/page.tsx:94,172; src/components/availability-control.tsx:17 (short labels); src/app/me/availability/page.tsx */}

## Answer from the email

Before each match we email you **Can you play?** with two buttons. You don't need to sign in to use them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Open the email with **Can you play?** in the subject.
    {/* src: src/emails/registry.tsx:263 (subject "Can you play? {{ whenLabel }}") */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a button">
    Tap **I’m available** or **Can’t make it**.
    {/* src: src/emails/registry.tsx:106,109 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the page that opens">
    Wait for the page to finish saving.
    {/* src: src/app/a/[token]/one-tap-recorder.tsx:33-37,54-64 (the answer saves once the page opens in a browser) */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** The page says **You’re in — thanks** or **Thanks for letting us know**, with the match date. {/* src: src/app/a/[token]/one-tap-recorder.tsx:42-45 */}

The email has no **Maybe** button. To answer maybe, use the app. {/* src: src/lib/availability/token.ts:17 */}

## Change your answer

Tap a different answer on **Home** or **Schedule**, or tap the other button in the email. The newest answer replaces the old one. Your captain doesn't get a separate notice when you change it, so if the lineup is already posted, tell your captain too. {/* src: src/lib/availability/token.ts:11-12 ("answering again just records the new answer"); no notification on change (agent research of src/lib/availability/, src/lib/notifications/dispatch.ts) */}

<Tip>
  **Maybe** tells your captain you're not sure yet, but they still need a yes or no to build the lineup. Change it to **I’m in** or **Can’t** as soon as you know. {/* src: src/lib/play/home.ts:335 ("You’re a maybe for {when} — your captain needs a yes or no.") */}
</Tip>

## Reminders

If you haven't answered, we send a reminder email, and your captain can send you a nudge as well. If you've turned on notifications for this device, you also get a **Can you play?** notification. How far ahead the emails go out is set by your league for each season. {/* src: src/lib/availability/cron.ts:59-62,117 (Ruleset.availabilityRequestDaysBefore / availabilityReminderDaysBefore); src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/nudge-button.tsx:22; src/lib/notifications/push-content.ts:18 */}

To turn these off or on, open your notification settings at [app.clubcircuitpb.com/me/notifications](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/me/notifications). They're under **My matches**. {/* src: src/lib/notifications/categories.ts:16 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### I don't see Can you play? on my home page
**Cause:** You're not on a team's roster yet, or your team has no upcoming match.
**Fix:** Your club places you on a team after registration closes. If the season has started and you still don't see a match, ask your club's admins whether you're on a roster. {/* src: src/components/match/next-match-card.tsx (onRoster); src/app/join/[season]/[division]/registration-form.tsx:118 ("Your club puts you on a team once registration closes.") */}

### "Your session ended — sign in again and retry."
**Cause:** You were signed out when you tapped an answer.
**Fix:** [Sign in](/players/sign-in) again and tap your answer again. {/* src: src/lib/auth/action.ts:30 */}

### "That didn’t save — check your connection and try again."
**Cause:** The email button's page couldn't reach ClubCircuit, usually because of a weak signal.
**Fix:** Move somewhere with better signal, then tap **Yes, I can play** or **Confirm I can’t play** on the page to try again. {/* src: src/app/a/[token]/one-tap-recorder.tsx:30,64 */}

### "This link didn’t work"
**Cause:** The email link was cut off or changed, often when an email is forwarded or copied.
**Fix:** Select **Sign in to ClubCircuit** on that page and answer from **Home** instead. {/* src: src/app/a/[token]/page.tsx:36-39,121 */}

### "We couldn’t find that match"
**Cause:** The match was probably rescheduled, so the old email link no longer points to it.
**Fix:** Sign in and answer on **Home** or **Schedule**, which show the new date. {/* src: src/app/a/[token]/page.tsx:67-68 */}

### I tapped an answer but it didn't change
**Cause:** The match may have started, been cancelled, or you may no longer be on that team's roster. In those cases the answer isn't saved.
**Fix:** Refresh the page. If the match is still upcoming and you're still on the team, contact your captain directly. {/* src: src/app/me/availability/actions.ts:36,42,48 (returns without saving) */}

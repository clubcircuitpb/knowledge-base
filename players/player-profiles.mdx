Your player profile is your identity on Club Circuit Pickleball. It tells league organizers, teammates, and opponents who you are, how you play, and what level of competition you're ready for. A complete, accurate profile helps you get placed in the right divisions, find compatible teammates, and make the most of every match. Take a few minutes to set it up properly — it pays off throughout the season.

## Editing Your Profile Details

You can update your profile information at any time from your account dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner of any page, then select **Edit Profile** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your display name">
    Enter the name you want other players and organizers to see. This can be your real name or a preferred nickname — just keep it recognizable and appropriate for your league community.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your bio">
    Use the **Bio** field to share a little about yourself — how long you've been playing, where you're based, or what you love about pickleball. Keep it brief and friendly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your location">
    Add your city or region so league organizers can match you with nearby events. Your full address is never displayed publicly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an avatar">
    Click **Change Photo** to upload a profile picture. Supported formats are JPG and PNG. Your avatar appears next to your name in standings, match results, and team rosters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Profile** at the bottom of the page. Your updates appear immediately across Club Circuit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Skill Rating

Your skill rating tells the platform — and other players — how experienced and competitive you are. Club Circuit uses a numeric rating scale aligned with standard pickleball skill classifications, ranging from **2.0 (Beginner)** to **5.0 (Professional)**.

Your rating serves two purposes: it helps league organizers place you in the right division, and it powers Club Circuit's matchmaking when open play or challenge matches are available in your area.

### How your rating is calculated

When you first join Club Circuit, you self-report your skill level. As you play more matches and record results on the platform, your rating is automatically adjusted based on your performance — including wins, losses, and the relative ratings of your opponents.

The system updates your rating after each scored match. You do not need to do anything manually once your results are logged.

### Updating your rating manually

If your self-reported rating no longer reflects your actual play level — for example, after training or a break from the game — you can request a manual update:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your profile settings">
    Navigate to **Edit Profile**, then select the **Skill Rating** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new rating">
    Choose the level that best reflects your current ability from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Click **Request Rating Update**. Your league organizer will review the request and approve or adjust it before the change takes effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your skill rating as accurate as possible. An inflated rating can place you in divisions where the competition feels discouraging or unfair to others. A rating that's too low means you'll win easily but miss the challenge that makes the game rewarding. Honest self-assessment leads to better matches for everyone.
</Tip>

## Preferred Play Style and Position

Club Circuit lets you indicate how you like to play so teammates and organizers can factor in your preferences when building rosters or assigning courts.

Navigate to **Edit Profile → Play Preferences** to set the following:

- **Preferred position** — Kitchen/net player, baseline, or no preference
- **Play format** — Singles, doubles, mixed doubles, or all formats
- **Competitive intensity** — Casual, social competitive, or highly competitive
- **Availability** — Days and times you're generally available to play

These preferences are visible to your teammates and league organizers, and they inform how Club Circuit suggests open matches and team placements. You can update them at any time.

## Privacy Settings

You control who can see your profile information on Club Circuit.

Navigate to **Settings → Privacy** to configure the following options:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Profile visibility | Public, League members only, or Private |
| Skill rating visibility | Visible to all, League members only, or Hidden |
| Location visibility | City/region only, or Hidden |
| Contact preferences | Allow messages from everyone, teammates only, or no one |

Setting your profile to **Private** hides it from other players browsing the platform, but your name, rating, and results remain visible to the organizers of any leagues you're enrolled in.

<Note>
  League organizers always have access to your display name, skill rating, and match history within their league — regardless of your privacy settings. This is necessary for scheduling, standings, and dispute resolution. Your personal contact information is never shared with organizers without your consent.
</Note>

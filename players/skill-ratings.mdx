Club Circuit uses a skill rating system to make sure every player competes at the right level. Whether you're brand new to pickleball or a seasoned competitor, your rating helps the platform place you in the most appropriate division, connect you with evenly matched opponents, and give league organizers the information they need to structure fair, competitive play. Your rating starts with what you tell us and evolves automatically as you play — so the more you compete, the more accurate it becomes.

## Skill Level Descriptions

Club Circuit's rating scale runs from **2.0** to **5.0** and aligns with the standard pickleball skill classifications used nationally. Use the descriptions below to find the level that best matches your current game.

| Rating | Level | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **2.0** | Beginner | You're new to pickleball and still learning the basic rules, court positioning, and how to keep a rally going. Consistency and shot placement are still developing. |
| **2.5** | Novice | You can sustain short rallies and understand the basics of scoring and kitchen rules. You're working on your serve and return, but unforced errors are still common. |
| **3.0** | Intermediate | You have a reliable serve, can execute forehand and backhand groundstrokes, and understand strategy basics like dinking and the importance of the non-volley zone. You're competitive in casual play. |
| **3.5** | Advanced Intermediate | You play with consistency and purpose. You're developing a third-shot drop, controlling the kitchen line effectively, and starting to exploit opponent weaknesses. You compete regularly in organized play. |
| **4.0** | Advanced | You have strong shot variety, can execute resets and speed-ups reliably, and demonstrate solid court awareness. You compete in leagues and tournaments and win consistently at lower levels. |
| **4.5** | Expert | You play at a high competitive level with few unforced errors. Your game includes advanced spin, precise placement, and smart pattern play. You've placed in tournaments or compete at the open level. |
| **5.0** | Professional | You compete at the national or professional level. Your game is elite across all dimensions — power, consistency, strategy, and mental toughness. |

<Tip>
  If you're joining your first league and aren't sure where to start, choose the level where you feel **challenged but competitive** — not the highest level you've ever played on a great day. It's always better to play up after proving yourself than to dominate a division where you're clearly overqualified. Most players new to organized pickleball find that **2.5 or 3.0** is the right starting point.
</Tip>

## How Ratings Are Calculated

Your initial rating on Club Circuit is self-reported when you create your account or join your first league. Once you begin playing rated matches, the system automatically adjusts your rating based on your performance.

Club Circuit's rating algorithm accounts for:

- **Match outcomes** — Wins and losses against rated opponents
- **Score margins** — Winning or losing by a wide margin carries more weight than a narrow result
- **Opponent rating** — Defeating a higher-rated opponent increases your rating more than defeating a lower-rated one, and vice versa for losses
- **Match volume** — Your rating stabilizes and becomes more accurate the more rated matches you play

Your rating updates automatically within 24 hours of a match result being confirmed in the system. You can view your rating history — including how each match affected your score — on the **Ratings** tab of your player profile.

<Info>
  Club Circuit integrates with **DUPR (Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating)**, the sport's most widely recognized rating system. If you have an existing DUPR rating, you can link your DUPR account in **Settings → Connected Accounts** to import your rating directly. Linked DUPR ratings are treated as verified and bypass the manual review process. Your Club Circuit rating and your DUPR rating will continue to update independently once linked.
</Info>

## Challenging or Updating Your Rating

Your rating updates automatically as match results come in, but there are situations where you may need to request a manual adjustment — for example, if your self-reported starting rating was too high or too low, or if a long absence from the game has significantly changed your skill level.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to your profile">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Edit Profile**, then open the **Skill Rating** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your current rating">
    Check your current rating and your recent match-based rating history to understand how the system has been tracking your performance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request an adjustment">
    Click **Request Rating Adjustment** and select the level you believe better reflects your current ability. Include a brief explanation — for example, noting that you've been playing at a different level in unsanctioned games, or that you've recently returned after a long break.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for organizer review">
    Your request is sent to the league organizer(s) you're currently registered with. They'll review it and either approve the change, suggest a different adjustment, or ask for more context before deciding. You'll be notified of the outcome by email and in-app notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Rating adjustment requests are reviewed by league organizers, not by Club Circuit support. If you have questions about a specific decision, reach out directly to your league organizer through the **Messages** tab on the league page.
</Note>

## Rating Requirements for Divisions

League organizers set minimum and maximum rating requirements for each division within their league. This ensures that all players in a given division are competing at a similar level, keeping matches fair and results meaningful.

Here's what rating requirements mean for you as a player:

- **Minimum rating** — If a division has a minimum, you must meet or exceed that rating to register. This prevents players from entering divisions where the competition level would be too advanced.
- **Maximum rating** — Some divisions — particularly beginner and intermediate brackets — cap the rating to keep the field appropriately matched. If your rating is above the cap, you'll need to register for a higher division.
- **Verified rating requirement** — Some organizers require a verified rating (either DUPR-linked or organizer-approved) rather than a self-reported level. If this applies, you'll be prompted to link your DUPR account or request organizer verification before your registration is accepted.

If your rating doesn't meet a division's requirements, Club Circuit will display a clear message when you attempt to register and suggest eligible divisions that match your level. If you believe you've been placed incorrectly or have a special circumstance, contact your league organizer directly through the **Messages** tab on the league page — organizers can manually approve exceptions on a per-player basis.

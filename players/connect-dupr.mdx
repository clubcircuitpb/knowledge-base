To add or change your DUPR ID, open your account menu and tap **Profile**. Under **About you**, type it into **DUPR ID** and tap **Save profile**. You don't type your rating: it comes from DUPR, through the league office's checks.

{/* src: leagues-app src/lib/nav/personal-rows.ts:21; src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:186-194,219; src/app/me/actions.ts:63-65 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need a player profile. You get one when you register for a season.
- Find your DUPR ID in the DUPR app, under your profile. It's the letters and numbers under your name.

{/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:63-64,187; src/app/me/actions.ts:50 */}

## Add or change your DUPR ID

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Open your account menu and tap **Profile**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen, tap **Profile**, then tap **Edit your profile**.
    {/* src: src/lib/nav/personal-rows.ts:21; src/components/shell/play-chrome.tsx:386; src/app/player/[playerId]/page.tsx:205-207 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find DUPR ID">
    Scroll to **About you**. If your profile isn't finished yet, you can tap **Add your DUPR ID** in the **Finish your profile** box to jump there.
    {/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:29,136,175,186 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your DUPR ID">
    Type your ID into **DUPR ID**, exactly as it appears in the DUPR app.
    {/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:187 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save profile**.
    {/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:219 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Saved.** Your DUPR ID is on your profile. Next to it, **DUPR doubles rating** shows your checked rating, or **Not checked yet** until the league office checks it. {/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:97-101,191-193 */}

## Where your rating comes from

You can’t edit your rating in ClubCircuit. The league office checks it against DUPR, either by hand or with a DUPR lookup, and your profile shows what they found. {/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:193; src/app/me/actions.ts:63-65; src/lib/dupr/lookup.ts:8-15 */}

If you change your DUPR ID, it counts as unconfirmed until the next check, and the change is recorded. {/* src: src/app/me/actions.ts:65-76 (duprVerifiedAt cleared, audited) */}

## Why your DUPR ID matters

- **Your rating:** it tells captains where you sit. Some seasons also use your rating to decide which division you can play in. Each season sets its own rating rules, so check your season's rules in the app before you register. {/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:29; src/app/join/[season]/[division]/page.tsx:69-75 */}
- **Your games on DUPR:** your games count toward DUPR only if both teams in the match opted in. See [Do our games count toward DUPR?](/rules/rally-21/scores-and-standings). When they do, a game can only be uploaded to DUPR once all four players in it have a DUPR ID on ClubCircuit. {/* src: src/lib/dupr/submission-rules.ts:1-12; src/lib/dupr/results.ts:47-48,178 */}

People signed in to ClubCircuit can see your DUPR ID on your player page, so they can find you on DUPR. {/* src: src/app/player/[playerId]/page.tsx:239-240 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "That doesn’t look like a DUPR ID. It’s the letters and numbers under your name in the DUPR app."
**Cause:** What you typed isn't a DUPR ID. It might be your rating, your name or have a typo.
**Fix:** Open the DUPR app, copy the ID under your name, and paste it into **DUPR ID**. {/* src: src/app/me/actions.ts:46-52 */}

### I want to type in my rating
**Cause:** Players set only their DUPR ID. Your rating comes from DUPR.
**Fix:** Make sure your **DUPR ID** is right. If your rating is still wrong or says **Not checked yet** for a long time, email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com). {/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:192-193 */}

### I can't find a Connect DUPR button
**Cause:** ClubCircuit doesn't sign in to DUPR for you. You add your ID by typing it in.
**Fix:** Follow the steps above. {/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:186-194 (plain field, no connect flow) */}

### My games didn't show up on DUPR
**Cause:** Either one of the teams didn't opt in to DUPR, or a player in the game has no DUPR ID on ClubCircuit.
**Fix:** Ask your captain whether both teams opted in. Check your own **DUPR ID** is saved. {/* src: src/lib/dupr/results.ts:47-48,178 */}

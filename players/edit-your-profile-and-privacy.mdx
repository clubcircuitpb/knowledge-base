To edit your profile, open your account menu and tap **Profile**. Change your photo, display name or the line about you, choose who can see your profile under **Who can see your profile**, then tap **Save profile**. Your photo saves on its own as soon as you pick it.

{/* src: leagues-app src/components/shell/account-control.tsx:124; src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:186,207,227 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need a player profile. You get one when you register for a season.
- Your profile is what teammates and opponents see. Your legal name, date of birth, phone and emergency contact are kept on **Your account** and are never shown on your profile.

{/* src: src/app/me/profile/page.tsx:60 (no player → /me), 97; src/app/me/page.tsx:197 */}

## Update your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Open your account menu and tap **Profile**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen first.
    {/* src: account-control.tsx:124; src/components/shell/play-chrome.tsx */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or change your photo">
    Tap **Add a photo** (or **Change photo**) and pick a picture. It saves as soon as you pick it. A square crop works best.
    {/* src: src/app/me/profile/photo-editor.tsx:73,81 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how your name appears">
    Under **About you**, fill in **Display name** if you want standings and lineups to show something other than your full name. Leave it blank to use your full name.
    {/* src: page.tsx:178,184 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a line about you">
    In **A line about you**, say how long you've played or what you like about the game.
    {/* src: page.tsx:187 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can see it">
    Under **Who can see your profile**, pick **Private**, **League** or **Public**. See below for what each means.
    {/* src: page.tsx:20-24,207 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save profile**.
    {/* src: page.tsx:227; src/app/me/actions.ts:63 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Saved.** at the top of the page, and the card at the top shows your changes. If you're on a team, tap **See it as others do** to check. {/* src: page.tsx:105-107,133 */}

## Who can see your profile

- **Private:** Teammates, your club's owners and league managers. Opponents see your name and club only.
- **League:** Anyone signed in to ClubCircuit. This is the default.
- **Public:** Anyone, including people not signed in. Your page can be shared by link.

Teammates, your club and league managers can always see your profile, whichever you choose. Your phone number and date of birth are never on your profile. {/* src: page.tsx:19-24,208; CLAUDE.md "Personal data" */}

## Finish your profile

Until your profile is complete, a **Finish your profile** box lists what's left: a photo, a line about you, your DUPR ID, and whether you'd sub. Tap the button next to each one to jump to it. {/* src: page.tsx:146-170; src/lib/player/profile-steps.ts */}

## Why isn't this working?

### My changes didn't save
**Cause:** Only your photo saves on its own. Everything under **About you** saves with the button.
**Fix:** Make your changes again and tap **Save profile** at the bottom. {/* src: page.tsx:180 */}

### "Could not save that photo."
**Cause:** The picture didn't upload.
**Fix:** Try a smaller photo, or try again on a stronger connection. {/* src: photo-editor.tsx:34 */}

### I don't see See it as others do
**Cause:** That button only shows once you're on a team's roster.
**Fix:** Check back after your club puts you on a team. {/* src: page.tsx:73,128 */}

### I want to change my legal name, phone or emergency contact
**Cause:** Those are private details, so they're on a different page.
**Fix:** Open your account menu, tap **Account**, change them, then tap **Save details**. {/* src: src/components/shell/account-menu.tsx:145; src/app/me/page.tsx:254 */}

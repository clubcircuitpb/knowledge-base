To register for a season, go to [app.clubcircuitpb.com/register](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/register), pick your division, fill in your details and pay. You don't need an account first. You play for one club all season, and your club puts you on a team once registration closes.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/register/page.tsx:21 ("Register to play"); src/app/join/page.tsx (/join redirects to /register); docs/adr/0042-one-public-register-link.md; registration-form.tsx:162 */}

**Before you begin**

- ClubCircuit is for adults. You'll need your date of birth, and the form shows the minimum age for your league.
- If your league uses DUPR ratings, have your DUPR ID ready. It's on your profile in the DUPR app.
- Don't have a club? You can still register. Choose **I don’t have a club — find me one**, and the league office finds you a club with room in your division.
- If your club emailed you an invitation, use the link in it. It registers you with that club and fills in the name and email your club entered.

## Register and pay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration page">
    Go to [app.clubcircuitpb.com/register](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/register). Under **Where do you play?**, choose your location. If only one location is open, you skip this step.
    {/* src: src/app/register/page.tsx:138-140; skipped when one location is open (page.tsx:79-82) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your division">
    Under **Pick your division**, choose the division you want to play in. Each one shows its rating range and price. If you're signed in, divisions you can enter are listed first, and the reason is shown under any you can't.
    {/* src: src/app/register/page.tsx:180,183,265 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your club">
    In **Which club are you playing for?**, choose your club, or **I don’t have a club — find me one**. If you came from your club's invitation, the club is already chosen and shows **Invited by** and the club's name. Not playing for them? Select **Not playing for** and the club's name to choose another.
    {/* src: registration-form.tsx:148-152 (invitation: "Invited by {club}", "Not playing for {club}?"), :158,175 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter your name, **Date of birth**, gender and **Jersey size**, plus your **DUPR ID** and **DUPR doubles rating** if asked. Your phone number is optional, and only the league office sees it. If the league office has already checked your rating, that checked rating is the one used, whatever you type.
    {/* src: registration-form.tsx:197-291; src/lib/registration/finalize.ts:166-177 (stored rating wins; S7) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the agreements">
    Tick the three boxes for the **Participation Waiver**, **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use & Participation**.
    {/* src: registration-form.tsx:307-337 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your price, then continue">
    Above the button, **Your price:** shows what you'll pay for the club you picked, or **$0.00** if your registration is free. Select **Continue to payment**. If you're not signed in, the button says **Email me a code to finish**: type the 6-digit code from the email to continue.
    {/* src: registration-form.tsx:346-355 ("Your price:"), :372; actions.ts:221-222 (signed out → /login/code → /apply/finalize) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay for your place">
    Select **Pay** and the amount. Payment is on Stripe's secure page, so your card details never reach ClubCircuit. If you have a discount code, choose **Add promotion code** on that page.
    {/* src: done/page.tsx:141-157 ("Pay {amount}", "Add promotion code"); docs/adr/0043-discount-codes-are-stripe-promotion-codes.md */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Payment received and your place is confirmed**, and we email you a receipt. Your club then tells you which team you'll play on. {/* src: done/page.tsx:130; src/lib/registration/notify.ts (receipt after payment) */}

<Note>
  Nothing is charged until the last step. If you close the payment page, your place is held and you can come back to pay. {/* src: registration-form.tsx:376; done/page.tsx:144-145 */}
</Note>

## If you don't need to pay

If the league office waived your fee, your club named you captain, or a code covers the whole price, you won't be asked for a card. The form shows **Your price: $0.00** before you submit, and afterwards the page says **You’re in** and **Your place is confirmed and there’s nothing to pay**. {/* src: registration-form.tsx:348-349 (free price before submit, signed in or via an invitation link); done/page.tsx:77,131,156-157; src/lib/registration/finalize.ts (fee waivers matched by email or invitation) */}

## If your division is full

You join the waitlist instead. The page says **You’re on the waitlist**. We email you as soon as a place opens, and you're charged nothing unless one does. {/* src: done/page.tsx:75,123-125 */}

## If you're a team captain

Most leagues give each team's captain a free registration. Your season's rules say whether yours does. There are three ways it reaches you:

- **Your club named you captain before you registered.** Register in that division, for that club, and your registration is free. It works with or without the link in your club's email: use the email address your club gave, or use the link, which works whatever address you register with. The registration page tells you **named you captain** and in which division, and the form shows **Your price: $0.00** before you submit.
- **You're made captain after you register.** Your fee is waived if you haven't paid yet, or refunded in full if you have. You stay on your team either way.
- **The league office sent you a discount code.** Enter it with **Add promotion code** on the payment page.

If you pick a different division or club, or **I don’t have a club — find me one**, we stop and ask before you pay anything. Choose **Register with** your club **in** your division **— free** to go back, or **Register here and pay** to carry on. If you're not signed in, you see the free price, or this choice, right after you enter your 6-digit code. {/* src: docs/adr/0056-captains-register-free.md; docs/adr/0068-invitations-tie-player-to-club.md (email match with or without the link; token matches whatever address; steer and confirm; signed-out after the code); src/lib/registration/captain-steer.ts:16,26 ("{club} named you captain in {division} — register there and your registration is free."); src/app/join/[season]/[division]/actions.ts:71-72 ("Register with {club} in {division} — free", "Register here and pay {price}"); src/app/apply/confirm/page.tsx:37-49; Ruleset.freeCaptainsPerTeam, default 1; docs/adr/0043-discount-codes-are-stripe-promotion-codes.md */}

## Why isn't this working?

### I was named captain but I was asked to pay
**Cause:** You registered in a different division or club from the one your club named you for and chose **Register here and pay**, or you registered without your club's link under a different email address from the one your club gave.
**Fix:** If you haven't paid yet, go back and register in the division and club you were named for, using your club's link or the email your club has. If you've already paid, ask your club's admins to make you captain of your team: your fee is refunded in full. {/* src: docs/adr/0068-invitations-tie-player-to-club.md (residual risk); docs/adr/0056-captains-register-free.md (made captain after registering → refunded, stays on team) */}

### "named you captain in … — register there and your registration is free"
**Cause:** Your club named you captain in another division, and this isn't it.
**Fix:** Select **Register in** and the division name, or **Go to that registration** on the form. Your club and division are filled in, and the price is $0.00. {/* src: src/lib/registration/captain-steer.ts:16; src/app/register/page.tsx:193; registration-form.tsx (named.href "Go to that registration") */}

### "If you register here instead, you pay …"
**Cause:** You chose a different division or club from the one your club named you captain for, where it's free.
**Fix:** Select **Register with** your club **in** your division **— free** to go there, or **Register here and pay** if you really mean to play here. Nothing is charged until you choose. {/* src: src/lib/registration/captain-steer.ts:26; actions.ts:71-72; src/app/apply/confirm/page.tsx */}

### "That invitation has expired."
**Cause:** Your club's invitation link is from an earlier season, or registration has closed since it was sent.
**Fix:** You can still register: pick your club on the form, or ask your club to send a new invitation. {/* src: src/lib/registration/invitation.ts:41 */}

### "That invitation has already been used to register."
**Cause:** Someone has already registered with this invitation link.
**Fix:** If that wasn't you, pick your club on the form and tell your club. {/* src: src/lib/registration/invitation.ts:42 */}

### "That invitation link didn’t work"
**Cause:** The link was cut off when it was copied or forwarded.
**Fix:** Pick your club on the form, or ask your club to send it again. {/* src: src/lib/registration/invitation.ts:43 */}

### "Your invitation is from … which isn’t taking registrations in …"
**Cause:** The club that invited you isn't playing in the division you picked.
**Fix:** Pick another division, or pick your club on the form. {/* src: src/app/join/[season]/[division]/page.tsx:116 */}

### "Nothing is open for registration yet"
**Cause:** No season is taking registrations in your location right now.
**Fix:** Check back later. Seasons usually open about two months before play starts. {/* src: src/app/register/page.tsx:82-83 */}

### "Registration for that season has closed."
**Cause:** The season's registration window has ended.
**Fix:** You can still sign up to sub for teams that need players. Look for **Sign up to sub** on the registration page. {/* src: src/lib/registration/finalize.ts:142; src/app/register/page.tsx:243 */}

### A division says "Not eligible"
**Cause:** Something about your rating, age, gender, past results or unpaid court fees doesn't fit that division's rules for this season. The reason is shown under the division.
**Fix:** Read the reason and pick a division that fits. If the reason is wrong, for example your rating is out of date, email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com). {/* src: src/app/register/page.tsx:262-276; src/lib/rules/rules/registration.ts (eligibility refusals) */}

### "You have … in unpaid court fees from earlier matches."
**Cause:** You owe court fees from earlier matches. They have to be paid before you can register.
**Fix:** Tap **Go to Payments** under the message, pay the fees, then come back. You can register straight away. See [Pay a court fee](/players/pay-court-fees-and-see-payments). {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/registration.ts:37-48 (COURT_FEE_UNPAID, link Payments); src/app/register/page.tsx:271-276 */}

### "… needs an active DUPR rating, and we haven’t confirmed yours yet."
**Cause:** That division needs an active DUPR rating, and the league office hasn't checked yours yet.
**Fix:** Add your DUPR ID to your profile so the league office can check it, or register for a division that doesn't require one. See [Add your DUPR ID](/players/connect-dupr). {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/registration.ts:135-140 (RATING_ACTIVE_REQUIRED) */}

### "ClubCircuit is for players … and over, so you can’t register yet."
**Cause:** The date of birth you entered is under the minimum age for this league.
**Fix:** If you typed it wrong, correct it and try again. Players under the minimum age can't register. {/* src: registration-form.tsx:397; actions.ts:175; docs/adr/0003-adults-only-at-launch.md */}

### My rating is "above this division’s" ceiling or "below" its floor
**Cause:** The DUPR rating you entered is outside the division's range.
**Fix:** Pick a division that fits your rating, or check the number you typed. If you don't have a rating yet, leave it blank. {/* src: registration-form.tsx:419,422 */}

### "A DUPR rating is between 1.000 and 8.000. Check the one you entered, or leave it blank if you don’t have one."
**Cause:** The rating you typed isn't on DUPR's scale.
**Fix:** Copy your doubles rating from the DUPR app, or leave the field blank. {/* src: src/lib/dupr/scale.ts:6-13 (DUPR's own scale, not a league rule); src/lib/registration/finalize.ts:170 */}

### "Tick the box to accept the Participation Waiver — you need to accept it to play."
**Cause:** One of the agreement boxes isn't ticked.
**Fix:** Tick all three boxes and select the button again. {/* src: actions.ts:55-57 */}

### "That division isn’t open any more. Go back and pick another."
**Cause:** The division closed or filled while you were filling in the form.
**Fix:** Go back to the [registration page](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/register) and pick another division. {/* src: actions.ts:173 */}

### "We couldn’t start checkout. Try again in a moment."
**Cause:** The payment page didn't open.
**Fix:** Wait a moment and select **Pay** again. Your place is still held. {/* src: done/actions.ts:56 */}

### I registered twice by mistake
**Cause:** You submitted the form again for the same season.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. We keep your first registration and don't create a second one. {/* src: done/page.tsx:84-85; actions.ts:128 */}

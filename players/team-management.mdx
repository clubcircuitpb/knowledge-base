Teams are the foundation of league play on Club Circuit Pickleball. Whether you're forming a squad with regular playing partners or building a competitive roster from scratch, Club Circuit gives you the tools to create and manage your team from a single dashboard. As a Team Captain, you control your roster, send invitations, and represent your team in league communications. This guide walks you through everything you need to get your team match-ready.

## Creating a New Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Teams section">
    From your dashboard, click **Teams** in the left navigation menu, then select **Create New Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your team details">
    Fill in the following fields:
    - **Team name** — Choose something unique within your league. Check availability as you type.
    - **Team description** — A short line about your team's vibe, hometown, or playing style (optional but recommended).
    - **Team logo** — Upload a JPG or PNG image to represent your team in standings and rosters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a league">
    Choose the league you want to register your team in from the dropdown. If the league hasn't opened registration yet, you can save your team and register later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and create">
    Click **Create Team**. You're automatically assigned as the Team Captain. Your team page is now live and ready for players.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inviting Players to Your Team

You can invite players to your team by email address or their Club Circuit username. Invitations can be sent before or after registering for a league.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your team page">
    From **Teams**, select your team, then click the **Roster** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Players">
    Select **Invite Players** in the top-right corner of the roster panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email addresses or usernames">
    Type each player's email address or Club Circuit username into the invite field. You can add multiple players at once by separating entries with a comma.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an optional message">
    Include a personal note with your invitation — this appears in the notification email the player receives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitations">
    Click **Send Invites**. Each player receives an email and an in-app notification. Their status shows as **Pending** in your roster until they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Players who don't have a Club Circuit account yet will receive an email with a link to sign up and join your team in one step.
</Note>

## Team Roles

Club Circuit uses two roles within every team:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Team Captain" icon="star">
    The Team Captain creates and manages the team. Captains can invite and remove players, update team details, register the team for leagues, and transfer captaincy to another player. Each team has exactly one Captain at a time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Player" icon="user">
    Players are active roster members. They can view team details, see match schedules, log scores (if permitted by the organizer), and communicate with teammates. Players cannot edit team settings or manage the roster.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Accepting or Declining Team Invitations

When another captain invites you to their team, you'll receive both an email notification and an alert in your Club Circuit inbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your notifications">
    Click the bell icon in the top navigation bar to view pending invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the invitation">
    Click the invitation to see the team name, captain, league, and any personal message included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or decline">
    Click **Accept** to join the roster or **Decline** to remove the invitation. If you accept, you're immediately added to the team's roster and can view the team page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing Players from Your Team

As Team Captain, you can remove a player from your roster at any time from the team's **Roster** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team roster">
    Navigate to your team page and select the **Roster** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the player">
    Find the player you want to remove and click the **⋯** (more options) icon next to their name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Remove from Team">
    Click **Remove from Team**, then confirm the action in the dialog that appears. The player is notified by email that they've been removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a player from your team mid-season may affect your team's eligibility and standings. If your league has minimum roster requirements, dropping below that threshold could result in forfeited matches or disqualification from the current season. Before removing a player, check your league's rules or contact your organizer to understand the impact.
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I be on multiple teams at once?">
    It depends on your league's rules. Some leagues allow players to compete on multiple teams across different divisions or formats (for example, a doubles team and a mixed doubles team). Other leagues restrict players to a single roster per season. Check the specific rules for each league you're considering — they're listed on the league's registration page under **Rules & Eligibility**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I transfer captaincy?">
    To transfer the Team Captain role to another player on your roster, go to your team page, open the **Roster** tab, click the **⋯** icon next to the player you want to promote, and select **Make Team Captain**. Confirm the transfer in the dialog. The selected player immediately gains captain permissions, and your role changes to Player. This action cannot be undone without the new captain's cooperation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my team when a season ends?">
    When a season concludes, your team's registration for that season is archived, but your team profile — including its name, logo, roster, and history — is preserved. You can re-register the same team for the next season without starting over. Any players who were on your roster remain members unless they leave or are removed. Past season results and standings remain viewable in your team's history tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

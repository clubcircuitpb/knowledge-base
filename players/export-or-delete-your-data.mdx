To download your data, open your account menu, tap **Account**, then tap **Your data**. A file downloads with everything ClubCircuit holds about you. To delete your account, open **Delete your account** on the same page, type `DELETE` and tap **Delete my account**. Deleting can't be undone.

{/* src: leagues-app src/components/shell/account-menu.tsx:143-146; src/app/me/page.tsx:309,318,350-355 */}

## Download your data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your account">
    Open your account menu and tap **Account**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen first.
    {/* src: src/components/shell/account-menu.tsx:143-146; src/components/shell/play-chrome.tsx:393 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Your data">
    Under **Settings**, tap **Your data**.
    {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:305,309 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the file">
    A file named like `clubcircuit-data-2026-11-02.json` downloads. Save it somewhere safe.
    {/* src: src/app/api/me/export/route.ts:34-40 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** The file holds what ClubCircuit has about you:

- Your account and player details, and your notification settings
- The seasons you registered for and what you paid, including court fees
- Your teams, your availability answers and your stats
- The lineups you were picked in, and the games you played, round by round, with the score from your side
- The messages you wrote: direct messages, lineup threads and team chat
- Your DUPR checks and snapshots, and any exception the league office made to an eligibility rule for you
- Disputes and conduct reports you sent, and your answers to "same team next season?"
- Your perks card and its uses, and the terms you agreed to

It doesn't include other players' details. Other people's replies aren't in it, and opponents appear by team name only. {/* src: src/lib/account/export.ts:7-61 */}

## Delete your account

<Warning>Deleting your account happens straight away and can't be undone.</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your account">
    Open your account menu and tap **Account**.
    {/* src: src/components/shell/account-menu.tsx:143-146 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Delete your account">
    Scroll to **Settings** and tap **Delete your account**.
    {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:311-319 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what goes and what stays">
    The panel lists what's removed and what stays. If you have a saved card, it names the card. If you owe court fees, it shows the total. You don't need to remove your card first.
    {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:326-348 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type DELETE">
    In **Type DELETE to confirm**, type `DELETE`. Capitals or lower case both work.
    {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:350; src/lib/account/delete-confirm.ts:3-11 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Tap **Delete my account**.
    {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:354-356 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You're signed out and land on the sign-in page with a message that your ClubCircuit account is deleted. You can't sign in to it again. {/* src: src/app/me/actions.ts:216-220; src/lib/account/delete-confirm.ts:13-15 */}

## What's removed

- Your name, email, phone, date of birth, gender, photo and emergency contact
- Your DUPR details
- Your saved card. It's removed from Stripe too.
- Any unpaid court fees. Deleting cancels them, so your card is never charged for them. To pay them first, open **Payments** before you delete.
- Your notification settings, phone notifications and calendar feed
- Any captain or club admin role you had, and your sub sign-up

{/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:326-343; src/lib/account/delete.ts:16-28 */}

## What stays

- Results of matches you played, under "Former player"
- Your team-chat and lineup-thread messages, also under "Former player"
- Payments and refunds already made
- The record of the terms you agreed to (when, and from which device), which shows you agreed to them

Standings and other players' history still add up, without your name. {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:344-348; src/lib/account/delete.ts:30-38 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "Type DELETE to confirm you want your account deleted."
**Cause:** The box was empty, or it didn't say DELETE.
**Fix:** Type `DELETE` in the box and tap **Delete my account** again. {/* src: src/app/me/actions.ts:210-213 */}

### Nothing happened when I tapped Your data
**Cause:** Your browser saved the file without showing you.
**Fix:** Check your Downloads folder, or your phone's Files app. {/* src: src/app/api/me/export/route.ts:39 (content-disposition: attachment) */}

### I want to pay what I owe before I delete
**Fix:** Open **Payments** from the link in the delete panel, or from your account menu, and pay the court fees there. Then come back and delete. [Pay a court fee](/players/pay-court-fees-and-see-payments). {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:335-339 */}

### I want to delete only some of my details
**Fix:** You don't need to delete your account. Change or clear them under **Your details** on **Your account**, or on your profile. To hide your profile, choose **Private** under **Who can see your profile**. {/* src: src/app/me/page.tsx:202-204; src/app/me/profile/page.tsx */}

### I changed my mind after deleting
**Cause:** Deleting can't be undone.
**Fix:** To play again, sign in with your email. That starts a new account, and you register for a season as a new player. Your old history stays under "Former player". {/* src: src/lib/account/delete-confirm.ts:14-15 */}

To sign in, go to [app.clubcircuitpb.com/login](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/login), enter your email and select **Email me a sign-in code**, then type the 6-digit code we email you and select **Sign in**. There's no password to remember.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/login/page.tsx:58,101,105; src/app/login/code/code-form.tsx:40,56 */}

**Before you begin**

- Have your email inbox open on the same phone or computer, or nearby. The code comes by email.
- New to ClubCircuit? You don't need a separate sign-up (also called creating an account or registering an account). Signing in with your email for the first time creates your account. {/* src: src/lib/auth/config.ts:18-19 (sign-up is open); docs/adr/0001-public-consumer-auth.md */}

## Sign in with an emailed code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to [app.clubcircuitpb.com/login](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/login), or select **Sign in** at the top of any ClubCircuit page.
    {/* src: src/app/login/page.tsx:57 (h1 "Sign in"); src/components/shell/public-frame.tsx (header Sign in link) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type your address in **Email address**, then select **Email me a sign-in code**.
    {/* src: src/app/login/page.tsx:101,105 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the email">
    Open the email with the subject **Your ClubCircuit sign-in code**. The code is in large type in the email. The email has no link to tap.
    {/* src: src/lib/auth/sign-in-code.tsx:31 (subject "Your ClubCircuit sign-in code: {code}"), :45-47; docs/adr/0027-email-code-sign-in.md */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">
    Back on the **Enter your code** page, type the 6 digits into **6-digit code**. On most phones, the keyboard offers to fill in the code from the email for you.
    {/* src: src/app/login/code/page.tsx:47; code-form.tsx:40,45-46 (inputMode numeric, autoComplete one-time-code) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Sign in">
    Select **Sign in**.
    {/* src: src/app/login/code/code-form.tsx:56 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You're signed in and taken to your ClubCircuit home. If you're new and haven't registered for a season yet, you're taken to registration instead. You stay signed in on that device until you sign out. {/* src: src/lib/nav/landing.ts:27-34 (landing; no roles or roster → /join → /register); src/lib/auth/config.ts:113 (database session) */}

<Note>
  Each code works once and expires 10 minutes after we send it. The **Enter your code** page shows how long you have. {/* src: src/lib/auth/sign-in-code.tsx:13 (SIGN_IN_CODE_TTL_MINUTES = 10), :49-50; src/app/login/code/page.tsx:49 */}
</Note>

## Why isn't this working?

### I didn't get the email
**Cause:** It went to your spam or junk folder, or the address has a typo.
**Fix:** Check spam and junk first, and search your inbox for "ClubCircuit sign-in code". If it's not there, select **request a new code** on the **Enter your code** page and check that you typed your address correctly. {/* src: src/app/login/code/page.tsx:55-57 ("Didn’t get it? Check spam, or request a new code.") */}

### "That code was wrong or has expired. Enter your email to get a new one."
**Cause:** The code was mistyped, it's more than 10 minutes old, or you already used it.
**Fix:** Enter your email again and select **Email me a sign-in code**. Use the newest email. Codes from older emails stop working. {/* src: src/app/login/page.tsx:38; src/lib/auth/sign-in-code.tsx:49 (single use) */}

### "Enter the 6-digit code from the email."
**Cause:** What you typed isn't 6 digits.
**Fix:** Copy the code exactly as it appears in the email. Spaces are fine. {/* src: src/app/login/code/actions.ts:27 */}

### "That’s a lot of codes at once. Wait a minute and try again."
**Cause:** You've asked for several codes in a short time.
**Fix:** Stop requesting codes and check your inbox, including spam, for one that already arrived. If the message keeps appearing, wait up to an hour before trying again. {/* src: src/app/login/page.tsx:91; src/lib/rate-limit/limiter.ts:99 (magicLinkPerEmail: 5 per hour) */}

### "Too many attempts. Wait a few minutes, then request a new code."
**Cause:** A code was entered incorrectly several times in a row.
**Fix:** Wait a few minutes, then go back to the sign-in page and request a new code. {/* src: src/app/login/code/actions.ts:33; src/lib/rate-limit/limiter.ts:104 */}

### "Enter a valid email address."
**Cause:** The address is missing part of it, like the `@` or the ending.
**Fix:** Check the address and try again. {/* src: src/app/login/page.tsx:75 */}

### "Something went wrong. Start again from the sign-in page."
**Cause:** The code page lost track of your email address, often because the page was opened from an old tab.
**Fix:** Go back to [the sign-in page](https://app.clubcircuitpb.com/login) and start again. {/* src: src/app/login/code/actions.ts:23 */}

### I'm looking for a "Sign in with Google" button
**Cause:** ClubCircuit signs you in with an emailed code. There's no Google option on the sign-in page.
**Fix:** Use the email address on your Google account, or any email you check. {/* src: src/app/login/page.tsx (no Google provider button); docs/adr/0027-email-code-sign-in.md */}

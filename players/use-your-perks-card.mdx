To use your perks card, open your account menu and tap **Perks card**. Show staff the QR code, or find an offer under **Offers**, tap **Use it**, then **Yes, use it**, in front of them. The card shows a live, ticking clock, so staff can tell it's not a saved picture.

{/* src: leagues-app src/components/shell/account-control.tsx:126; src/app/me/card/page.tsx:55,92,123; src/components/perks/use-offer-button.tsx:16,37; src/components/perks/live-clock.tsx */}

**Before you begin**

- You get a perks card when you're playing in a season that has perks switched on. That's usually once your registration is approved, or once you've paid a sub activation. Each season decides who qualifies.
- Your card works until shortly after the season's last day of play.

{/* src: src/lib/perks/card.ts:33-79 (includeRegistered, includeActivatedSubs, graceDays); src/app/me/card/page.tsx:81-82 */}

## Show your card or use an offer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your card">
    Open your account menu and tap **Perks card**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen first.
    {/* src: account-control.tsx:126; src/components/shell/play-chrome.tsx:155,388 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the QR">
    Tap **Show QR full size** and let staff scan it. They see whether your card is active right now.
    {/* src: src/app/me/card/page.tsx:92,109; src/app/card/[token]/page.tsx */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find an offer">
    Under **Offers**, find the offer for the place you're at. It says how many uses you have left this season.
    {/* src: page.tsx:123,166-172 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in front of staff">
    Tap **Use it**, then **Yes, use it**. Each tap counts as one use, so only do this when you're at the desk.
    {/* src: use-offer-button.tsx:16,22,37 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the next screen">
    Show staff the screen that opens. It says **Staff: apply the offer.**, with the time ticking.
    {/* src: src/app/me/card/redeemed/[id]/page.tsx:61 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** Staff apply the offer, and it counts as one of your uses this season. {/* src: src/lib/perks/offers.ts:76-121 */}

Some offers say **Show staff your card’s QR — they record this one.** For those, show the QR instead, and the club's staff record the use. {/* src: page.tsx:180; src/app/card/[token]/page.tsx:95-106 */}

## What staff see

When staff scan your QR, they see your first name and last initial, your photo if you've added one, the season, and whether the card is active right now. They never see your surname, email, phone number or date of birth. {/* src: src/app/card/[token]/page.tsx:15-19,64-80 */}

## Find perks before you go

Your league's **Perks** page lists every participating club and local partner, and which divisions each offer is for. Find it in the menu at the top of the league's pages. {/* src: src/lib/nav/app-nav.ts:58; src/app/[market]/[season]/perks/page.tsx:19-91 */}

## Save it to your phone's wallet

If **Add to Apple Wallet** or **Save to Google Wallet** shows under your card, tap it. The pass opens the same live check as the app. If you don't see those buttons, wallet passes aren't available yet, so show the card in the app. {/* src: src/app/me/card/page.tsx:94-107; src/lib/perks/wallet.ts:8-13 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "No perks card yet"
**Cause:** You're not in a season with perks switched on yet, your registration isn't approved yet, or your season doesn't include your group.
**Fix:** Check back once your registration is approved. If you're a sub, your card arrives once you've paid a sub activation. {/* src: src/app/me/card/page.tsx:81-82; src/lib/perks/card.ts:50-79 */}

### "This season’s card has ended."
**Cause:** The season, and its grace period, is over.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. Your next season's card arrives when you register again. {/* src: page.tsx:117; card.ts:33-40 */}

### "The league office has cancelled this card."
**Cause:** The league office turned off your card.
**Fix:** Email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) if you think that's wrong. {/* src: page.tsx:117 */}

### "That offer is for visiting players — it’s your own club."
**Cause:** Some clubs' offers are only for players visiting from other clubs.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. Use your card at other clubs and partners. {/* src: src/lib/perks/offers.ts:127-137 */}

### "You’ve used this offer … — that’s the limit this season."
**Cause:** Each offer can be used a set number of times a season.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. The offer resets next season. {/* src: src/lib/perks/offers.ts:127-137 */}

### "No offers for your division yet."
**Cause:** The league hasn't added offers for your division yet.
**Fix:** Check back later. Offers appear on your card as they're added. {/* src: page.tsx:126 */}

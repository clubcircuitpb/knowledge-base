To form your teams, open **Club HQ** > **Registrations** and tap **Team builder**. Add a team for each division you're playing in, then tap a player under **Players to place** and tap **Place here** on their team. When a team has enough players to field a match, tap **Confirm team**.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/club/[slug]/admin/formation/page.tsx:158; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/teams/page.tsx:206-212; team-builder.tsx:96,147,165,600 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be a club admin or owner.
- Your club must be entered in the season. See [Apply to bring your club into the league](/clubs/apply-to-join-the-league).
- Your league sets how many players a team needs and how many it can have, for each division.

{/* src: src/app/club/[slug]/admin/teams/page.tsx:218-223; src/lib/club/capacity.ts (Division.rosterMax, rosterMin/MaxPerGender, format minPlayersPerGender) */}

## Build your teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team builder">
    In **Club HQ**, tap **Registrations**, then **Team builder**.
    {/* src: club-hq.ts:26; formation/page.tsx:158 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a team">
    Tap **Add a team**, choose the **Division**, and tap **Add team**. Leave **Team name (optional)** blank to name it after your club and the division.
    {/* src: teams/page.tsx:206-212; add-team-form.tsx:63-102 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a player">
    Under **Players to place**, tap the player you want to place.
    {/* src: team-builder.tsx:165,290-317 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place them on a team">
    Tap **Place here** on the team they should play for. On a team card, the button shows their first name: **Place … here**.
    {/* src: team-builder.tsx:147,473-480 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the team">
    When the team shows **Can field**, tap **Confirm team…**, then **Confirm team**.
    {/* src: team-builder.tsx:461,600,638 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** Each player you place gets an email saying which team they're on. A confirmed team is entered in its division and counts toward the division running. {/* src: team-builder.tsx:58,622-623; registry PLAYER_PLACED_ON_TEAM */}

<Warning>
  After you confirm a team, its roster changes go through the league office. You can't place players on it, or move or take anyone off it, from Club HQ. You can still rename it. Confirm a team only when you're happy with it. {/* src: team-builder.tsx:49-52,122-128,467-470,526-547,566-568; src/lib/club/admin.ts:271,396; docs/adr/0082-club-roster-moves-tryouts-and-reschedule-requests.md (decision 3) */}
</Warning>

## Let ClubCircuit suggest a start

Under **Want a head start?**, ClubCircuit suggests who fills your short teams, strongest players first, filled to what each team needs. On a team, tap **Accept — place … on** that team to place them all and email each one, or ignore it and place people yourself. {/* src: src/app/club/[slug]/admin/teams/page.tsx:278-356; src/lib/club/propose-teams.ts:78 (skips teams that can field or are confirmed) */}

## Players you can't place yet

On a player under **Players to place**, open the **⋯** menu:

- **Put on your waitlist** emails them that there's no spot yet. You can still place them later.
- **Suggest they sub instead** emails them a link to sign up as a sub.

{/* src: team-builder.tsx:318-341 */}

## Players invited to a tryout

On your **Registrations** page, under **Tryouts**, you can invite players you haven't placed yet. We email them when and where. They stay in **Players to place**, marked **Invited to tryout** or **Came to tryout** with the tryout's name, until you place them. {/* src: formation/page.tsx:409-414,667-687; team-builder.tsx:311-316; src/lib/formation/tryouts.ts (TRYOUT_INVITATION email); docs/adr/0082 (decisions 1, 2) */}

## Move a player or rename a team

On a placed player, open the **⋯** menu:

- **Move to another team** puts them straight onto another of your teams this season, and emails them their new team. A confirmed team is listed, but you can't choose it.
- **Take off this team** sends them back to **Players to place**. Nobody is emailed.

To rename a team, open the **⋯** menu on the team's card and tap **Rename team**. Enter the **Team name** and tap **Save name**. No two teams in a division can share a name.

{/* src: team-builder.tsx:512-547,652-727 (MoveToTeam), 729-806 (RenameTeam); src/lib/club/teams.ts:245-264; docs/adr/0082 (decisions 4, 5) */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "Your club isn’t entered in … yet, so you can’t add teams to it."
**Cause:** Your club hasn't entered this season.
**Fix:** On **Club HQ**, accept the league's invitation, or tap **Tell the league we’re in**. {/* src: teams/page.tsx:222-232; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/page.tsx:141 */}

### "… is full — it's at its …-player cap."
**Cause:** The team has as many players as your league allows.
**Fix:** Move someone off, place the player on another team in that division, or add them to the sub roster. {/* src: src/lib/club/capacity.ts:27-28 */}

### "… already has … — the most this division allows."
**Cause:** The team already has the most players of that gender the division allows.
**Fix:** Place them on another team in the division, or move someone of that gender off first. {/* src: src/lib/club/capacity.ts:73 */}

### The Confirm button says "Confirm when it can field"
**Cause:** The team doesn't have enough men or women to field a match yet.
**Fix:** Place more players until the team shows **Can field**. {/* src: team-builder.tsx:600; src/lib/club/capacity.ts:85-92 */}

### "… is confirmed with the league, so its roster changes go through the league office."
**Cause:** You tried to place a player on a confirmed team, or move someone on or off one. A confirmed team has no **Place here** button.
**Fix:** Email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) with the change you need, or use a team that isn't confirmed yet. {/* src: src/lib/club/placement.ts:28-30; src/lib/club/admin.ts:271,396; team-builder.tsx:122-128,467-470; src/lib/club/teams.ts:172 */}

### "Another team in … is already called “…”. Pick a different name."
**Cause:** A team in the same division already has that name.
**Fix:** Choose a different **Team name** and tap **Save name** again. {/* src: src/lib/club/teams.ts:264 */}

### A player is registered for a different division
**Cause:** The team builder warns when you place someone on a team outside the division they registered for.
**Fix:** Check with the player, or place them on a team in their own division. {/* src: team-builder.tsx:144,481-485 */}

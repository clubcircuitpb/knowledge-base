To tell the league when your courts can host, open **Club HQ** and go to **Schedule** > **Host availability**. Pick a date, tap the courts and times you can offer, and tap **Save this day**. To repeat it every week, tick **Also every** before you save.

{/* src: leagues-app src/lib/nav/club-hq.ts:43; src/components/hosting/host-grid.tsx:208,464-468 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be a club admin or owner.
- Your venue's court count must be set first. The grid has one column per court.

{/* src: src/app/club/[slug]/admin/availability/page.tsx:48 (requireClubAdminBySlug), :86-93; src/components/venue/venue-form.tsx:25,107 */}

## Offer your courts for a day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Host availability">
    In **Club HQ**, go to **Schedule** > **Host availability**. If you see **First, how many courts does your venue have?**, tap **Add your court count**, fill in **Courts at this venue**, and come back.
    {/* src: club-hq.ts:43; availability/page.tsx:86-93; venue-form.tsx:107 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date">
    Use **Previous day** and **Next day**, the date picker, or tap one of the season's dates.
    {/* src: host-grid.tsx:219-254 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the courts and times you can offer">
    Under **Courts you can offer**, tap a court at a time to offer it. One tap is one match's worth of time. Tap again to take it back. To offer a court all day, tap **offer all day** under its name.
    {/* src: host-grid.tsx:120-140,211,330 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat it weekly, if you like">
    Tick **Also every** to offer the same courts on that weekday until the season ends.
    {/* src: host-grid.tsx:464; src/lib/club/host-blocks.ts:161-164 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save this day**. If you're repeating it, the button says **Save every** and the weekday.
    {/* src: host-grid.tsx:468 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see a message confirming the day and the courts you offered. The shaded rows show when the league would like matches to start. {/* src: host-grid.tsx:184-191,211 */}

## Tell the league you can't host a day

Open the date, make sure nothing is offered (tap **Clear the day** if needed), then tap **Can’t host this day**. A day you never touch counts as no objection. {/* src: host-grid.tsx:459,467-476; src/lib/club/host-blocks.ts:166-197; docs/adr/0063-host-availability-grid.md */}

## Name courts and set opening hours

Tap **Courts and hours** to name each court, hide a court the league shouldn't use, and set when you open and close. Tap **Save courts and hours**. {/* src: host-grid.tsx:549-604 */}

## How the league uses this

The league's schedule places your home matches on days you can host, as long as enough courts are free. If a day needs more courts than you have, Club HQ tells you under **Not enough courts**, and the league may move a start time or a venue. {/* src: src/lib/admin/schedule.ts:205-212; src/components/hosting/hosting-panel.tsx:90-112 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "Add your venue and how many courts it has first"
**Cause:** Your venue's court count isn't set.
**Fix:** Go to **Club settings** > **Home venue**, fill in **Courts at this venue**, and save. {/* src: src/lib/club/host-blocks.ts:153; venue-form.tsx:107 */}

### My changes disappeared
**Cause:** You changed the date before saving. The grid shows **Not saved yet** while you have unsaved changes.
**Fix:** Paint the day again and tap **Save this day**. {/* src: host-grid.tsx:478 */}

### "Already scheduled here: …"
**Cause:** A match is already scheduled on that day at your courts.
**Fix:** Keep at least those courts offered so the match still fits. {/* src: host-grid.tsx:272-273 */}

### "… needs … courts at once — you have …"
**Cause:** More matches are scheduled at your club that day than your courts can hold at once.
**Fix:** Tap **Open that day** and offer every court you can, so the league can adjust. {/* src: hosting-panel.tsx:102-112 */}

### "Closing time has to be at least an hour after opening."
**Cause:** The opening hours you set are too short.
**Fix:** Set a later closing time. {/* src: src/lib/club/host-blocks.ts:216 */}

To message your club's players, open **Club HQ** and tap **Reach people**. Under **Send to**, choose everyone in your club or one team, write a subject and message, and tap **Send announcement**. Players get it by email and in the app.

{/* src: leagues-app src/lib/nav/club-hq.ts:49-50; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/comms/page.tsx:52-98; src/lib/notifications/dispatch.ts:68,92 (LEAGUE_ANNOUNCEMENT, transactional) */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be a club admin or owner.
- An announcement only goes to players who are on one of your teams' rosters.

{/* src: src/lib/admin/announce.ts:68-90,176 */}

## Send an announcement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reach people">
    In **Club HQ**, tap **Reach people**.
    {/* src: club-hq.ts:49-50 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who it's for">
    Under **Send to**, choose **Everyone in** your club, or pick one team.
    {/* src: comms/page.tsx:69-77 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it">
    Add a **Subject** and your **Message**.
    {/* src: comms/page.tsx:86-95 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Tap **Send announcement**.
    {/* src: comms/page.tsx:95 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see how many people it was sent to, and it's listed under **Sent**. {/* src: comms/page.tsx:64,106,118 */}

<Note>
  Announcements are always delivered. Players can't turn off club news, and they can't reply to an announcement. For a conversation, use your team's captains or team chat. {/* src: comms/page.tsx:98; src/lib/admin/announce.ts */}
</Note>

## Message another club

To reach another club in your league about reschedules, courts or anything else captains don't cover, open **Reach people** > **Messages**. Under **Start a conversation with**, choose the club and tap **Start**. Their club's admins get your message. {/* src: club-hq.ts:53; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/messages/page.tsx:50-69; src/lib/messaging/club.ts:86-95 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "No one is on a roster there yet, so there’s no one to send to."
**Cause:** The team or club you chose has no players placed on a roster yet.
**Fix:** Place players on your teams first, or message them once they're on a team. {/* src: src/lib/admin/announce.ts:176 */}

### "You can only message clubs in your market."
**Cause:** The other club is in a different league.
**Fix:** Email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) if you need to reach them. {/* src: src/lib/messaging/club.ts:46 */}

### A player says they didn't get my announcement
**Cause:** It may be in their spam folder, or they're not on a roster yet.
**Fix:** Ask them to check spam and the notification bell in the app. Check they're on one of your teams. {/* src: src/lib/notifications/inbox.ts:34; announce.ts:68-90 */}

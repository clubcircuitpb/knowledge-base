To message your club's players, open **Club HQ** and tap **Reach people**. Under **Send to**, choose everyone in your club or one team, write a subject and message, and tap **Send announcement**. Players get it by email and in the app, signed with your club's name.

{/* src: leagues-app src/lib/nav/club-hq.ts:49-50; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/comms/page.tsx:56-60,74-112; src/lib/admin/announce.ts:249-260 (clubName: signed with the club's name); src/lib/notifications/dispatch.ts (LEAGUE_ANNOUNCEMENT, transactional) */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be a club admin or owner.
- **Everyone in** your club reaches this season's people only: the players on your teams, and those registered with your club for a season that's registering or in play. People from past seasons don't get it.
- A message to one team reaches the players on that team's roster.

{/* src: src/lib/admin/announce.ts:68-133 (CURRENT_SEASON_STATUSES; paid or approved registrations; a team scope is its roster only); comms/page.tsx:78-82 */}

## Send an announcement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reach people">
    In **Club HQ**, tap **Reach people**.
    {/* src: club-hq.ts:49-50 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who it's for">
    Under **Send to**, choose **Everyone in** your club, or pick one team. The hint under it names the seasons **Everyone in** reaches.
    {/* src: comms/page.tsx:74-96 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it">
    Add a **Subject** and your **Message**.
    {/* src: comms/page.tsx:98-103 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Tap **Send announcement**.
    {/* src: comms/page.tsx:105-108 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see how many people it was sent to, and it's listed under **Sent**. {/* src: comms/page.tsx:69-73,117-121 */}

<Note>
  Announcements are always delivered. Players can't turn off club news, and they can't reply to an announcement. For a conversation, use your team's captains or team chat. {/* src: comms/page.tsx:110-112; src/lib/admin/announce.ts */}
</Note>

## Message another club

To reach another club in your league about reschedules, courts or anything else captains don't cover, open **Reach people** > **Messages**. Under **Start a conversation with**, choose the club and tap **Start**. Their club's admins get your message. {/* src: club-hq.ts:53; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/messages/page.tsx:50-69; src/lib/messaging/club.ts:86-95 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "No one is registered with your club or on its teams in a season that’s registering or in play …"
**Cause:** You chose **Everyone in** your club, but no season is registering or in play, or nobody has registered with your club for one yet.
**Fix:** Pick one of your teams instead, or send it once players have registered for the new season. {/* src: src/lib/admin/announce.ts:226-232; comms/page.tsx:81 */}

### "No one is on a roster there yet, so there’s no one to send to."
**Cause:** The team you chose has no players on its roster yet.
**Fix:** Place players on the team first, or choose **Everyone in** your club. {/* src: src/lib/admin/announce.ts:230 */}

### "You can only message clubs in your market."
**Cause:** The other club is in a different league.
**Fix:** Email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) if you need to reach them. {/* src: src/lib/messaging/club.ts:46 */}

### A player says they didn't get my announcement
**Cause:** It may be in their spam folder, or they weren't in the group you sent to. For example, they played for you in a past season but haven't registered for this one.
**Fix:** Ask them to check spam and the notification bell in the app. Check they're on one of your teams or registered with your club this season. {/* src: src/lib/notifications/inbox.ts; announce.ts:68-133 */}

To collect court fees, open **Club HQ** and go to **Court fees**. Tap **Set up with Stripe** to connect your club's own payments account, then choose **How players pay** and **How the fee works**, and tap **Save fee**. Players are charged in the app, and the money goes straight to your club's Stripe account.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/club/[slug]/admin/court-fees/page.tsx:131,331-404; src/lib/court-fees/connect.ts */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be one of the club's owners. Admins don't see **Court fees**.
- Have your club's business details and the bank account you want paid into. You'll give these to Stripe, not to ClubCircuit.
- Your league decides whether court fees are collected in the app this season. The page tells you.

## Set up court fees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Court fees">
    In **Club HQ**, tap **Court fees**.
    {/* src: club-hq.ts:47 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your payments account">
    Tap **Set up with Stripe** and follow Stripe's steps. If you started before, it says **Continue with Stripe**. When you're done, Stripe sends you back to this page.
    {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:131; src/lib/court-fees/connect.ts:79-80 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how players pay">
    Under **How players pay**, choose **In the app** or **At our desk**.
    {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:331-333 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how the fee works">
    Under **How the fee works**, choose **A total per team**, **A fee per player** or **A flat amount per match**, then enter the amounts.
    {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:337-378 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tax, if it applies">
    Under **Tax**, enter your tax rate and how it's shown on the receipt.
    {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:382-398 (Tax rate, Shown on the receipt as) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save fee for** your season.
    {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:404 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Court fees saved.** Players see the fee on the match page from the moment you save. {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:405; court-fees/actions.ts:88 */}

## When players are charged

With **In the app**, each player's card is charged when the fee falls due. Your league sets whether that's when the lineups lock or after the match. Players without a saved card get a link to pay. Under **Coming up**, you can see what's due for each match day and when it's charged. {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:332,155-189; src/lib/court-fees/run.ts (Ruleset.courtFeeChargeAt); src/lib/court-fees/forecast.ts */}

The page also shows how card processing is handled for your club, as agreed with the league. ClubCircuit takes a collection fee from each payment, which the statement shows as its own line. If a sponsor covers part of the fee, players pay less and ClubCircuit pays your club the rest. {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:299-307,233-235,213; docs/adr/0057-court-fee-collection-fee.md; docs/adr/0059-sponsor-court-fee-subsidy.md */}

## See what you've collected

Under **Statement**, you see what's been collected for you, what's still unpaid, and what ClubCircuit owes you or you owe ClubCircuit. Tap **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet. It uses the same words as the screen, like **Paid** or **Card declined**. Each Monday after a week with court fees, owners also get a statement email. Payouts reach your bank through Stripe on your usual payout schedule. {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:195-216; src/lib/court-fees/labels.ts:1-12 (the statement CSV uses the same words); court-fees/statement.csv/route.ts; src/lib/court-fees/weekly-statement.ts:9-10 */}

## Refunds

Refunds, payouts and your tax forms are in your own Stripe Dashboard. A full refund shows here as **Refunded**. If you give back part of a fee, it shows as **Partly refunded** with the amount. {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:136-143,237; src/lib/court-fees/labels.ts:14-21 (clubFeeStatusLabel); src/lib/court-fees/connect-events.ts */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "Only the club’s owners can see or change its money …"
**Cause:** You're a club admin, not an owner.
**Fix:** Ask one of your club's owners. Tap **See who the owners are** to find them. {/* src: src/components/shell/owners-only.tsx:15-19 */}

### "Only this club’s owners can change its court fees."
**Cause:** You're signed in as a club admin who isn't an owner, or your role changed while the page was open. The same goes for setting up payments.
**Fix:** Ask an owner. They're listed under **Owners & admins** in **Club HQ**. {/* src: court-fees/actions.ts:23-24 */}

### "Stripe couldn’t open the payment setup just now."
**Cause:** Stripe didn't respond.
**Fix:** Try again in a few minutes. If it keeps happening, email the league office at [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com). {/* src: src/lib/court-fees/connect.ts:39 */}

### The page says players pay at our desk, but I chose In the app
**Cause:** Your payments account isn't fully set up yet, or your league isn't collecting court fees in the app this season.
**Fix:** Finish **Set up with Stripe**. If the page says the league isn't collecting fees in the app, players pay at your desk this season. {/* src: court-fees/page.tsx:314-320 */}

### "Enter each fee as a dollar amount, like 30 or 30.00."
**Cause:** A fee has letters or symbols in it.
**Fix:** Enter just the number. {/* src: court-fees/actions.ts:61 */}

### A player's fee says Card declined
**Cause:** Their card was declined.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. ClubCircuit retries and sends them a link to pay. {/* src: src/lib/court-fees/labels.ts:8; src/lib/court-fees/run.ts:20-30,263-316 */}

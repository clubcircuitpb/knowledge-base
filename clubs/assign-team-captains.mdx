To make someone captain, open the **Team builder**, open the player's **⋯** menu and tap **Make captain**. You can also tap **Choose a captain** on the team's card in **Club HQ**. Each team has one captain, who sets its lineups and enters its scores.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/club/[slug]/admin/teams/team-builder.tsx:478-479; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/page.tsx:221 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be a club admin or owner.
- The captain has to be on the team's roster first. See [Form your club's teams for a season](/clubs/form-teams-for-a-season).

{/* src: src/lib/club/admin.ts:58 */}

## Make someone captain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team">
    In **Club HQ**, open **Registrations** > **Team builder**, or find the team's card on **Club HQ**.
    {/* src: formation/page.tsx:157; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/page.tsx:212-221 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the player">
    In the Team builder, tap **⋯** next to the player. On a team card, tap **Choose a captain**.
    {/* src: team-builder.tsx:478; page.tsx:221 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make them captain">
    Tap **Make captain**, or tap the player on the team card.
    {/* src: team-builder.tsx:478; captain-picker.tsx */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see that they're captain, with a note about their registration fee. We email them to say they're captain of the team. {/* src: team-builder.tsx:483; captain-picker.tsx:40; registry CAPTAIN_ASSIGNED */}

## Change the captain

Make someone else captain the same way, using **Change captain** on the team card. The previous captain goes back to being a player on the team. A team can't have two captains. {/* src: src/lib/club/admin.ts:71-87 (other captain grants revoked); page.tsx:221 */}

## Captains and registration fees

If your league gives captains a free registration, making someone captain shows one of these:

- **Waived:** they hadn't paid yet, so their fee is waived.
- **Refunded:** they'd already paid, so their fee is refunded in full. They stay on the team.
- **Already used:** this team's free captain place was already used, so their registration stays paid. This happens when you change captains.

{/* src: src/lib/registration/captain-waiver.ts:42-51,165-171,311; docs/adr/0056-captains-register-free.md */}

To name a captain before they register, so they register free from the start, use **Invite one person** on the **Import players** page. See [Invite your players and captains to register](/clubs/invite-players-to-register). {/* src: src/app/club/[slug]/admin/import/page.tsx:93,124 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "A captain must be an active member of the team."
**Cause:** The player isn't on that team's roster.
**Fix:** Place them on the team first, then make them captain. {/* src: src/lib/club/admin.ts:58 */}

### "Their registration fee couldn’t be refunded automatically …"
**Cause:** The refund to their card didn't go through.
**Fix:** Email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com). The league office can refund it. {/* src: src/lib/registration/captain-waiver.ts:165-171 */}

### Club HQ says a team has no captain
**Cause:** Nobody on the team is captain yet, so nobody can set its lineups or enter its scores.
**Fix:** Tap **Name a captain** and pick a player. {/* src: src/lib/club/needs-you.ts:153-155 */}

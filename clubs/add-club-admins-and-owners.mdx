To add someone who'll help run your club, open **Club HQ**, go to **Owners & admins**, enter their name and email under **Add someone**, and tap **Add admin**. To make them an owner as well, tick **Make them an owner too**. Only club owners can add or remove people.

{/* src: leagues-app src/lib/nav/club-hq.ts:69; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/admins/page.tsx:105-112; admin-controls.tsx:36-63; src/lib/auth/guards.ts:198-202 (requireClubOwner) */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be one of the club's owners. Admins can see who runs the club but can't change it.
- Admins run the club day to day: teams, roster, venue, captains and announcements. Owners do all that, and also handle the club's money (court fees, payouts and statements) and decide who runs it.

{/* src: src/app/club/[slug]/admin/admins/page.tsx:51,111; docs/adr/0049-club-owners.md */}

## Add an admin or owner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Owners & admins">
    In **Club HQ**, under **Club settings**, tap **Owners & admins**.
    {/* src: src/lib/nav/club-hq.ts:64,69 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Under **Add someone**, enter their first name, last name and email. They'll sign in with that email address.
    {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:36-43 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose owner or admin">
    To make them an owner, tick **Make them an owner too — they’ll see and decide the club’s money, and who runs it**. Leave it unticked for an admin.
    {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:49 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them">
    Tap **Add admin**.
    {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:63 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** They're added straight away and we email them. They sign in with that email and choose **Club HQ**. They don't need to accept anything. {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:58; src/lib/club/admins.ts:69; src/emails/registry.tsx:1975-1984 */}

## Change someone's role

Next to their name, tap **Make owner** or **Make admin only**, then confirm. {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:112-120 */}

## Remove someone

Next to their name, tap **Remove**, then **Yes, remove**. They lose access to Club HQ straight away. {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:79-85; src/lib/club/admins.ts:116 */}

<Note>
  A club always needs at least one owner and one admin, so you can't remove the last one. Add or promote someone else first. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:104,108,132 */}
</Note>

## Why isn't this working?

### I don't see Add someone
**Cause:** You're an admin, not an owner. Only owners add and remove people.
**Fix:** Ask one of your club's owners. Their names are listed on the same page. {/* src: src/app/club/[slug]/admin/admins/page.tsx:110-112 */}

### "That’s the club’s only owner. Make someone else an owner first."
**Cause:** You tried to remove the club's only owner.
**Fix:** Make someone else an owner first, then remove them. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:108 */}

### "… needs at least one admin. Add someone else first, then remove this one."
**Cause:** You tried to remove the last person who runs the club.
**Fix:** Add someone else first. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:104 */}

### "… already helps run …"
**Cause:** That email address is already an admin or owner of your club.
**Fix:** To change their role, use **Make owner** or **Make admin only** next to their name. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:73 */}

### The person I added can't see Club HQ
**Cause:** They're signed in with a different email address from the one you added.
**Fix:** Ask them to sign out and sign in with the email you entered. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:69 (account keyed by that email) */}

To add someone who'll help run your club, open **Club HQ**, go to **Owners & admins**, enter their name and email under **Add someone**, and tap **Add admin**. To make them an owner as well, tick **Make them an owner too** first, and the button reads **Add owner**. Only club owners can add or remove people.

{/* src: leagues-app src/lib/nav/club-hq.ts:69; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/admins/page.tsx:40,104-108 (canManage: owners and the league office); admin-controls.tsx:40-76 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be one of the club's owners. Admins can see who runs the club but can't change it.
- Admins run the club day to day: teams, roster, venue, captains and announcements. Owners do all that, and also handle the club's money (court fees, payouts and statements) and decide who runs it.

{/* src: src/app/club/[slug]/admin/admins/page.tsx:40,51,110-114; docs/adr/0049-club-owners.md */}

## Add an admin or owner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Owners & admins">
    In **Club HQ**, under **Club settings**, tap **Owners & admins**.
    {/* src: src/lib/nav/club-hq.ts:64,69 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Under **Add someone**, enter their **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**. They sign in with that email address.
    {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:42-51 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose owner or admin">
    To make them an owner, tick **Make them an owner too — they’ll see and decide the club’s money, and who runs it**. Leave it unticked for an admin.
    {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:52-58 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them">
    Tap **Add admin**. If you ticked the owner box, the button reads **Add owner**.
    {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:69-73 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see that they're now an admin, or an owner, and that we've emailed them. They're added straight away and don't need to accept anything. They sign in with that email and choose **Club HQ**. {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:64-67; src/lib/club/admins.ts:71-95; src/emails/registry.tsx (club admin added: "Sign in with this email address and choose Club HQ") */}

## Change someone's role

Next to their name, tap **Make owner** or **Make admin only**, then **Yes, make owner** or **Yes, admin only**. An owner you make admin only keeps running the club, without its money. {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:122-133 */}

## Remove someone

Next to their name, tap **Remove**, then **Yes, remove**. They lose access to Club HQ straight away. {/* src: admin-controls.tsx:83-93; src/lib/club/admins.ts:100-120 */}

<Note>
  A club always needs at least one owner and one admin, so you can't remove the last one. Add or promote someone else first. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:109,113,137; page.tsx:78,93 (Remove shows only when more than one person runs the club) */}
</Note>

## Why isn't this working?

### I don't see Add someone
**Cause:** You're an admin, not an owner. Instead, the page says **The club’s owners add and remove the people who run it.**
**Fix:** Ask one of your club's owners. They're listed on the same page, marked **Owner**. {/* src: src/app/club/[slug]/admin/admins/page.tsx:40,111-113 */}

### "That’s the club’s only owner. Make someone else an owner first."
**Cause:** You tried to remove the club's only owner.
**Fix:** Make someone else an owner first, then remove them. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:113 */}

### "They’re the club’s only owner. Make someone else an owner first."
**Cause:** You tried to make the club's only owner an admin only.
**Fix:** Tap **Make owner** next to someone else first. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:137 */}

### "… needs at least one admin. Add someone else first, then remove this one."
**Cause:** You tried to remove the last person who runs the club.
**Fix:** Add someone else first. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:109 */}

### "… already helps run …"
**Cause:** That email address is already an admin or owner of your club.
**Fix:** To change their role, use **Make owner** or **Make admin only** next to their name. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:78 */}

### The person I added can't see Club HQ
**Cause:** They're signed in with a different email address from the one you added.
**Fix:** Ask them to sign out and sign in with the email you entered. {/* src: src/lib/club/admins.ts:71-90 (account keyed by that email); admin-controls.tsx:50 */}

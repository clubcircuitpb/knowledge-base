Welcome to Club Circuit Pickleball — the all-in-one platform built for players, coaches, and league organizers who want to spend less time on logistics and more time on the court. Whether you're launching a brand-new recreational league or managing a competitive multi-division season, Club Circuit gives you the tools to schedule matches, track standings, record scores, and share rulebooks with every player in your network.

## Get Up and Running in Four Steps

New to Club Circuit? Follow this quick onboarding flow to go from sign-up to your first match in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Account">
    Sign up with your email address or connect with an existing Google or Apple account. Verify your email to activate your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join or Create a League">
    Browse publicly listed leagues near you or accept an invite to a private league. Alternatively, create your own league if you're an organizer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Team">
    Build your roster by inviting teammates, assigning roles, and confirming player skill levels so the platform can seed matches fairly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play and Track Standings">
    Submit match scores after each game and watch your team climb the live standings leaderboard throughout the season.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **New to pickleball?** Don't worry about your skill level right away — you can update it at any time from your profile settings. Starting with an honest self-assessment helps the platform match you with leagues and opponents at the right level, making your first season more enjoyable.
</Note>

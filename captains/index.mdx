This section is for team captains. Your club's admins make you captain of a team, and you'll see your captain tools in the app once they do.

## Before each match

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Manage your team" icon="users" href="/captains/manage-your-team">
    See who can play each match and nudge anyone who hasn't answered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Build your lineup" icon="clipboard-list" href="/captains/build-and-post-a-lineup">
    Put a player on every court, every round, and submit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Find a sub" icon="user-plus" href="/captains/find-a-substitute">
    Offer the spot to eligible subs when you're short.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reschedule a match" icon="calendar-clock" href="/captains/request-a-reschedule">
    Offer the other captain times the host club has offered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Opt in to DUPR" icon="chart-line" href="/captains/opt-in-to-dupr">
    Choose whether your team's games count toward DUPR.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## On match day and after

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Enter and confirm scores" icon="square-pen" href="/captains/enter-and-confirm-scores">
    Enter each court as it finishes; the other captain confirms.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dispute a result" icon="flag" href="/captains/dispute-a-result">
    Raise a wrong score, or report a confirmed result, to the league office.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contact other captains" icon="message-square" href="/captains/contact-other-captains">
    Lineup changes, late-lineup consent and score discrepancies.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Finals day

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Submit your playoff roster" icon="list-check" href="/captains/submit-your-playoff-roster">
    List who will be at finals day, before the deadline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Set playoff lineups" icon="shuffle" href="/captains/set-playoff-lineups-round-by-round">
    Take turns with the other captain, one round at a time.
  </Card>
</Columns>

You're still a player too, so everything in the [Players](/players) section applies to you.

Can't find what you need? Ask your club's admins, or email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com).

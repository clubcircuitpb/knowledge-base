In a playoff match, open the match and tap **Playoff rounds**. When it’s your turn, pick a pair for each court and tap **Submit round**. Captains take turns to lead, and each has a clock.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx (Playoff rounds); src/app/match/[matchId]/rounds/live-rounds.tsx; docs/adr/0078-live-playoff-lineups.md */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be the captain of one of the two teams.
- Send your [playoff roster](/captains/send-your-playoff-roster) first. Only players on it appear in the pickers.
- Your league sets how long each captain’s clock runs and what a late round costs. See your league’s rules in the app.

{/* src: src/lib/playoffs/live.ts submitRoundLineup (requireMatchCaptain; roster check); Ruleset playoffRoundLineupMinutes, playoffLateRoundLineupPenalty */}

## Set a round

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Playoff rounds">
    Open the match and tap **Playoff rounds**.
    {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for your turn">
    One captain leads each round: the higher seed in rounds 1 and 3, the lower seed in rounds 2 and 4. When it’s your turn, the round says **Your turn** with your time left.
    {/* src: src/lib/playoffs/live-rules.ts leaderOf; live-rounds.tsx ("Your turn.", countdown) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a pair for each court">
    For each court, pick two players. The pickers only offer who the court needs: a man and a woman for mixed, two men or two women otherwise.
    {/* src: live-rounds.tsx (pickers filtered by the court's pairing type); live-rules.ts PLAYOFF_FORMAT */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Submit round**. Once submitted, a round can’t be changed.
    {/* src: live-rounds.tsx ("Submit round N"); live-rules.ts canSubmitRound ("A submitted round can’t be changed.") */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** if you led, the other captain now sees your pairs and places theirs. If you answered, the courts are set and show for everyone.

{/* src: live.ts submitRoundLineup messages; live-rules.ts visiblePairs */}

## When a round ends

A round ends when its last score is entered, or when a captain or the finals-day lead taps **Round finished**, whichever comes first. The next round’s clock starts then.

{/* src: live-rules.ts roundEndedAt; live-rounds.tsx ("Round finished"); live.ts finishRound */}

## If the match is level at 8–8

If the match finishes level and neither team has a penalty, there’s a **Deciding round**: a men’s, a women’s and a mixed game, best two of three. Each captain picks three pairs and taps **Submit deciding pairs**. Nobody sees the other team’s pairs until both are in. Players who partnered earlier can partner again.

If a penalty caused the tie, there’s no deciding round: the team without the penalty wins. See your league’s rules in the app for every case.

{/* src: live-rounds.tsx (Deciding round card, "Submit deciding pairs"); src/lib/playoffs/decide-rules.ts decidePlayoffMatch; live.ts submitDecidingPairs; docs/adr/0079 */}

## Swap an injured player

Before a court’s game is scored, you can swap a player out. In the round, open **Swap a player (injury or emergency)**, pick who’s coming off and who’s going on, say why, and tap **Swap**. The new player must be the same gender and on your playoff roster.

{/* src: live-rounds.tsx SwapForm; live.ts swapRoundPlayer */}

## Why isn’t this working?

### It says the other captain leads this round

**Cause:** the other team leads this round, so you answer once they’ve submitted.

**Fix:** wait. The page refreshes on its own, and your form appears when it’s your turn.

{/* src: live-rules.ts canSubmitRound ("The other captain leads this round…") */}

### It says two players have already partnered in this match

**Cause:** partners play together once a match.

**Fix:** pick a different partner for one of them.

{/* src: live-rules.ts roundLineupProblems ("… have already partnered in this match. Partners play together once.") */}

### My round was marked late

**Cause:** you submitted after your clock ran out. A late round costs your team games, applied straight away.

**Fix:** if something stopped you, tell the league office. They can waive it before the result is final.

{/* src: live.ts submitRoundLineup (late → PLAYOFF_LATE_ROUND_LINEUP penalty); docs/adr/0078 decision 4 */}

### We played a round on paper

**Cause:** the round wasn’t set in the app on the day.

**Fix:** ask the league office to enter it from your paper sheet. A round entered that way carries no clock or late penalty.

{/* src: live-rounds.tsx ("Enter … from paper", league office only); live.ts submitRoundLineup afterTheFact */}

To enter scores, open the match on match day and tap the **Scores** tab. Enter each court's score as it finishes; each one saves as you go. When every court is in, tap **Submit scores**. A captain of the other team then taps **Confirm result**, and the standings update.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:312-313,578 (Scores tab from match day); src/app/match/[matchId]/scores/scores-entry.tsx:386,522,537; src/lib/scores/mutations.ts (recompute on confirm) */}

**Before you begin**

- Either team's captain can enter the scores. Only a captain of the other team can confirm them. If your team has two captains, your co-captain can't confirm scores your team submitted.
- Both lineups need players on every court before a score can go in.
- The **Scores** tab appears from midnight on match day.

## Enter the scores

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the match">
    On **Home**, tap **Enter scores**. Or scan the QR code on the printed scoresheet.
    {/* src: src/lib/play/home.ts:413,421; src/app/m/[code]/route.ts; src/components/print/scoresheet.tsx ("Scan to enter scores.") */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Scores">
    Open the **Scores** tab. On a phone you see one court at a time, starting with Round 1, Court 1.
    {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:578; scores-entry.tsx:284 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the court's score">
    Tap **You** or the other team's side, then type the points.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:329,343-355 (keypad) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to the next court">
    Tap **Save and next court**. Repeat for every court in every round.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:377 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    On the last court, tap **Submit scores**.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:386 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Sent to the other captain to confirm.** The match shows **Awaiting confirmation** until they do. The other team's captains get an email titled **Confirm the scores**, with both teams' names and a **Check and confirm** button. {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:150; src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:936; src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:552-600 (notifyScoresSubmitted: the other team's captains only); src/emails/registry.tsx:1357-1384 (SCORES_SUBMITTED, "Confirm the scores — {{ homeTeamName }} vs {{ awayTeamName }}", "Check and confirm") */}

<Warning>
  Once you've submitted, the scores are locked and nobody can edit them. A captain of the other team either confirms them or taps **Something’s wrong**. If you spot a mistake yourself, tell them so they can raise it. See [Dispute a match result](/captains/dispute-a-result). {/* src: src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:118-120 ("Submitted means locked (A2)"); scores-entry.tsx:480,490 (inputs disabled while awaiting) */}
</Warning>

## Record a forfeited game

If a game couldn't be played or finished, for example nobody could fill it, a player was hurt, or a player was too late with no sub, record it as a forfeit instead of a score.

1. On that court, tap **Forfeit this game**.
2. Under **Which team forfeited?**, choose the team.
3. Pick the reason.
4. If the game had started, enter the **Score when play stopped**. Leave it blank if it never started.
5. Tap **Record the forfeit**.

If the team that forfeited was behind, the game stands at that score. Otherwise it's recorded as 1–0 to the other team. A whole-match forfeit, or games played by an ineligible sub, are recorded by the league office. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/scores/forfeit-panel.tsx:58-101; src/lib/scores/forfeit-rules.ts (reason labels); src/lib/scores/forfeits.ts (whole match and ineligible sub: requireMarketAdminForDivision) */}

## Confirm the other team's scores

When the other team submits, you get the **Confirm the scores** email and your **Home** shows **Review and confirm**. Check every court on the **Scores** tab, then tap **Confirm result**. If a score is wrong, tap **Something’s wrong** instead. {/* src: src/emails/registry.tsx:1369,1384; src/lib/play/home.ts:403; scores-entry.tsx:537,544 */}

**Result:** You see **Result confirmed. Standings updated.** Both teams get the result. {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:160; src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:367-410 (SCORES_CONFIRMED to both rosters) */}

If nobody confirms in time, the result may be confirmed automatically. Your league sets whether and when, and the email tells you. {/* src: src/lib/scores/auto-confirm.ts (Ruleset.autoConfirmAfterWindow, confirmationWindowHours); mutations.ts:592-594 (windowLine) */}

If both teams win the same number of games, your league's rules decide the match, for example on point differential. The league office can also apply a game penalty, such as for a late lineup, even after the result is confirmed. If it does, the result and standings update and both teams are told. {/* src: src/lib/scores/decide.ts (Ruleset.matchTieResolution); src/lib/scores/penalties.ts; src/lib/scores/penalty-text.ts */}

## Report a problem with a confirmed result

If a result is already confirmed, by the other captain or automatically, and it's wrong, a captain of either team can report it.

1. Open the match and tap the **Scores** tab.
2. Tap **Report a problem with this result**.
3. Under **What’s wrong with the result?**, name the round and court.
4. Tap **Send to the league office**.

The result stands, and stays in the standings, until the league office rules. Your league may set a time limit for these reports. See [Dispute a match result](/captains/dispute-a-result) for what happens next. {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:424-462 (shown when CONFIRMED), :178; src/lib/disputes/report-result.ts:5-12,44-47 (either team's captain; Ruleset.resultReportWindowHours, null = any time in the season) */}

## Print a scoresheet

On the match's **Rounds** tab, open **More for this match** and tap **Print scoresheet**. Each team keeps score on paper during the match. Photograph the signed sheet when you're done: if scores are disputed, the signed sheet decides. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:644,649; src/components/print/scoresheet.tsx */}

## Why isn't this working?

### I don't see the Scores tab
**Cause:** It isn't match day yet, or you're not a captain of either team in this match.
**Fix:** Check back from midnight on match day. If you should be captain, ask your club's admins. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:312-313; src/lib/scores/match-day.ts:7-10 */}

### "These scores are submitted and waiting for the other captain. If a score is wrong, ask them to dispute it."
**Cause:** You tried to change a score after your team submitted. Submitted scores are locked.
**Fix:** Ask the other team's captain to tap **Something’s wrong** and say what the score should be. {/* src: src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:120 */}

### I don't see Confirm result
**Cause:** Your team submitted these scores, perhaps your co-captain did. Only the other team can confirm them. You see **Waiting for the other captain to confirm.** instead.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. Wait for the other team's captain. {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:526-527; src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:314-318; src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:299 ("Your team submitted these scores, so the other team’s captain confirms them.") */}

### "A game can’t end level — someone has to win it."
**Cause:** You entered the same score for both sides.
**Fix:** Check the scoresheet and enter the winning score. {/* src: src/lib/scores/validate.ts:34 */}

### "The winner has to win by … isn’t enough of a margin."
**Cause:** The winning side didn't win by enough points under your league's rules.
**Fix:** Check the score. If the game went past the target score, the winner needs a clear margin. {/* src: src/lib/scores/validate.ts:64 */}

### "A game goes to … The winning score can’t be lower than that."
**Cause:** The winning score is below the points a game is played to.
**Fix:** Check that you typed the winning side's score correctly. {/* src: src/lib/scores/validate.ts:52 */}

### "Enter a score for every game first — … still to go."
**Cause:** Some courts don't have a score yet.
**Fix:** Go back through the rounds and fill in the courts that are missing. {/* src: src/lib/scores/resolve.ts */}

### "… hasn’t put players on Round …, Court … yet."
**Cause:** A lineup is missing players for that court, so there's nothing to score.
**Fix:** Ask the captain whose lineup is short to fill it, or fill your own with **Set your lineup**. {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:293-300 */}

### "The time to report a problem with this result has passed."
**Cause:** Your league's time limit for reporting a confirmed result is over.
**Fix:** If it's serious, email the league office at [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com). {/* src: src/lib/disputes/report-result.ts:46 */}

### "That didn’t save — check your signal and try again."
**Cause:** Your phone lost its connection.
**Fix:** Move somewhere with better signal and enter that court again. Courts you already saved are kept. {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:127 */}

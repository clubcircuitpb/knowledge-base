To find a sub, open **Manage team**, go to the **Subs** tab and pick the match. Under **Who could fill in**, tap **Offer a spot** for one player, or **Offer to all** for everyone listed. Each sub you offer gets an email, and the first to accept joins your team for that match.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx:165,433-445,473,478,553; src/lib/subs/offer.ts:183-205,221-248 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be your team's captain.
- Only subs who can play this match are listed. They've signed up to sub for your division, pass your league's sub rules for this match, aren't blocked out that day, and live within the distance they'll travel.

{/* src: manage/page.tsx:484-485; src/lib/subs/candidates.ts:157-183; src/lib/rules/rules/substitute.ts:17-32 */}

## Offer the spot to subs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subs tab">
    Go to **Manage team** and tap **Subs**. If **Home** says you're short for a match, tap **Find a sub** to go straight there.
    {/* src: manage/page.tsx:165; src/lib/play/needs-you.ts:125,127 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the match">
    Tap the match you need a sub for. The page tells you who you're short.
    {/* src: manage/page.tsx:396-399,433-445 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who to ask">
    Under **Who could fill in**, check each sub's rating, distance and how often they've subbed.
    {/* src: manage/page.tsx:406-421,473 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the offer">
    Tap **Offer a spot** next to one sub, or **Offer to all** to ask everyone listed at once.
    {/* src: manage/page.tsx:478,553 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** Each sub you offered shows **Offered · waiting**. When one accepts, they show **Accepted**. You're notified, your team chat says they've joined, and they appear in your lineup builder as available for that match. {/* src: manage/page.tsx:360-364; src/lib/subs/offer.ts:221-248,286-301 */}

You can also tap **Need a sub?** on your next match, choose **Gender needed (optional)** and add a **Note to candidates (optional)**, then tap **Send offers**. {/* src: sub-request-control.tsx:62-81 */}

<Note>
  The first sub to accept gets the spot, and the other offers close. Some divisions charge subs an activation before their first match there. The spot isn't held while they pay, so the sub may take a little longer to accept. {/* src: src/lib/subs/offer.ts:151-175,183-218; docs/adr/0058-substitute-activation-per-division.md */}
</Note>

## If nobody accepts

The request closes when the match starts, and we email you to say whether it was filled. Asking a few days ahead gives subs more time to say yes. To try again, tap **Offer again** next to a sub who declined or didn't answer. {/* src: src/lib/subs/request.ts:109 (expiresAt = match start); src/lib/subs/expire.ts; registry SUB_REQUEST_EXPIRED; manage/page.tsx:553 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "No eligible substitutes right now"
**Cause:** Nobody signed up to sub for your division can play this match today under your league's sub rules.
**Fix:** Check again closer to the match, since subs sign up and change their dates. You can also ask the league office for help. {/* src: manage/page.tsx:492 */}

### A sub I know isn't on the list
**Cause:** They aren't signed up to sub for your division, they've blocked out that date, they live outside their travel distance, or your league's sub rules don't allow them in this match.
**Fix:** Ask them to check their **Subbing** page. See [Sign up to sub for other teams](/players/sign-up-to-sub). {/* src: src/lib/subs/candidates.ts:157-183 */}

### "No eligible, opted-in substitutes match this request right now."
**Cause:** No sub fits the request, often because you asked for one gender and nobody of that gender is available.
**Fix:** Try again with **Gender needed (optional)** set to **Either**, if your lineup allows it. {/* src: src/lib/subs/request.ts:92; sub-request-control.tsx:67-73 */}

### The same sub keeps showing in my lineup after their match
**Cause:** A sub who accepts stays on your team's list for the season.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. Only place them in matches they've agreed to play. {/* src: src/lib/subs/offer.ts:234 (ACTIVE SUB membership) */}

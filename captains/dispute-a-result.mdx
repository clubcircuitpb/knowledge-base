To dispute a result, open the match's **Scores** tab, tap **Something’s wrong**, say which court is wrong and what the score should be, then tap **Raise it with the league**. The league office reviews it and rules, and both captains are told.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/match/[matchId]/scores/scores-entry.tsx:456,463-476; src/lib/disputes/ruling.ts:325-453 */}

**Before you begin**

- Only the captain who didn't submit the scores can raise a dispute, and only while the match shows **Awaiting confirmation**.
- Have the signed paper scoresheet ready. A photo of it usually settles a dispute.

## Raise a dispute

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the match's Scores tab">
    From **Home**, tap **Review and confirm**, or open the match and tap **Scores**.
    {/* src: src/lib/play/home.ts:403-405; src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:516 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Something's wrong">
    Tap **Something’s wrong** instead of **Confirm result**.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:449,456 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what's wrong">
    In **What’s wrong with the result?**, say which court and what the score should be. The other captain sees this too.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:463-465 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to the league">
    Tap **Raise it with the league**.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:476 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** The match shows **Disputed — with the league manager**, and the other captain is told. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:860; scores-entry.tsx:149; registry SCORES_DISPUTED */}

## Add your side and a photo

While a dispute is open, either captain can add more on the match page. Write your side under **Add your side** and tap **Add to the dispute**. To add the scoresheet, use **Add a photo**, say what it shows, and tap **Add photo**. Only the two teams and the league office can see photos. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/dispute-panel.tsx:135-165; src/lib/disputes/evidence.ts:79-91 */}

## What happens next

The league office may ask both captains for more information, and you'll get an email if it does. When it rules, the match page shows either **The league ruled: the score was changed** or **The league ruled: the scorecard stood**, with the reasons. Both captains get the ruling by email, and the result and standings update. {/* src: dispute-panel.tsx:90-94,128; registry DISPUTE_MORE_INFO, DISPUTE_RESOLVED; src/lib/disputes/ruling.ts:325-453 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### I don't see Something's wrong
**Cause:** You submitted these scores yourself, the result is already confirmed, or the match isn't waiting on confirmation.
**Fix:** If you submitted them, ask the other captain to raise it. If the result is already confirmed, email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) with the match and what's wrong. {/* src: src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:388; registry SCORES_AUTO_CONFIRMED ("If that looks wrong, contact the league manager.") */}

### "That photo is still too large after shrinking. Try a different one."
**Cause:** The photo file is too big.
**Fix:** Take a new photo, or crop the one you have, and try again. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/actions.ts:239 */}

### "Say what the photo shows …"
**Cause:** You left the photo's description blank.
**Fix:** Add a short note, like "Our scorecard, round 3". {/* src: src/lib/disputes/evidence.ts:26 */}

### "Your team has added … photos already — that’s the most for one dispute."
**Cause:** Each team can add only a limited number of photos to one dispute.
**Fix:** Describe anything else under **Add your side**. {/* src: src/lib/disputes/evidence.ts:35 */}

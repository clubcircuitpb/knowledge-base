To dispute a result, open the match's **Scores** tab, tap **Something’s wrong**, say which court is wrong and what the score should be, then tap **Raise it with the league**. If the result is already confirmed, tap **Report a problem with this result** instead. Either way, the league office reviews it and rules, and both captains are told.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/match/[matchId]/scores/scores-entry.tsx:432,544,551,564; src/lib/disputes/report-result.ts; src/lib/disputes/ruling.ts */}

**Before you begin**

- While the match shows **Awaiting confirmation**, only a captain of the team that didn't submit the scores can raise a dispute. A co-captain of the team that submitted can't.
- Once the result is confirmed, a captain of either team can report a problem with it. Your league may set a time limit for this.
- Have the signed paper scoresheet ready. A photo of it usually settles a dispute.
- This is for scores. To report someone's behaviour at the match, use **Report a conduct problem** on the match page instead. Only the league office sees those reports.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the match's Scores tab">
    From **Home**, tap **Review and confirm**, or open the match and tap **Scores**. The **Confirm the scores** email's **Check and confirm** button opens it too.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Something's wrong">
    Tap **Something’s wrong** instead of **Confirm result**.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:537,544 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what's wrong">
    In **What’s wrong with the result?**, say which court and what the score should be. The other captain sees this too.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:551-553 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to the league">
    Tap **Raise it with the league**.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:564 */}
  </Step>

**Result:** The match shows **Disputed — with the league manager**, and the captain who submitted the scores is told. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:938; scores-entry.tsx:189; src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:501-507 (SCORES_DISPUTED) */}

## Report a problem with a confirmed result

If the result has already been confirmed, by the other captain or automatically, you can still ask the league office to look at it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the match's Scores tab">
    Open the match and tap **Scores**. It shows **This result is confirmed.**
    {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:61 (CONFIRMED keeps the Scores tab, A4); scores-entry.tsx:421 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Report a problem with this result">
    Tap **Report a problem with this result**.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:432 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what's wrong">
    Under **What’s wrong with the result?**, name the round and court, and what the score should be.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:437-438 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Tap **Send to the league office**.
    {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:450 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Sent to the league office. The result stands until they rule; both captains will hear by email.** The result stays in the standings until the league office rules. {/* src: scores-entry.tsx:178; src/lib/disputes/report-result.ts:5-12 */}

## Add your side and a photo

While a dispute is open, either captain can add more on the match page. Write your side under **Add your side** and tap **Add to the dispute**. To add the scoresheet, use **Add a photo**, say what it shows, and tap **Add photo**. Only the two teams and the league office can see photos. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/dispute-panel.tsx:135-165; src/lib/disputes/evidence.ts */}

## What happens next

The league office may ask both captains for more information, and you'll get an email if it does. When it rules, the match page shows either **The league ruled: the score was changed** or **The league ruled: the scorecard stood**, with the reasons. Both captains get the ruling by email, and the result and standings update. {/* src: dispute-panel.tsx:88-94; registry DISPUTE_MORE_INFO, DISPUTE_RESOLVED; src/lib/disputes/ruling.ts */}

## Why isn't this working?

### I don't see Something's wrong
**Cause:** Your team submitted these scores, the result is already confirmed, or the match isn't waiting on confirmation.
**Fix:** If your team submitted them, ask the other team's captain to raise it. If the result is already confirmed, tap **Report a problem with this result** instead. {/* src: src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:472-480; scores-entry.tsx:424,529 */}

### "Your team submitted these scores. If one is wrong, ask the other team’s captain to dispute it, or tell the league office."
**Cause:** Your co-captain submitted the scores, so only the other team can dispute them.
**Fix:** Ask the other team's captain to tap **Something’s wrong**. {/* src: src/lib/scores/mutations.ts:479 */}

### "You’ve already reported this result."
**Cause:** You've already sent a report on this match, and the league office hasn't ruled yet.
**Fix:** Add anything new under **Add your side** on the match page. The league office replies by email. {/* src: src/lib/disputes/report-result.ts:53 */}

### "The time to report a problem with this result has passed."
**Cause:** Your league's time limit for reporting a confirmed result is over.
**Fix:** If it's serious, email the league office at [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com). {/* src: src/lib/disputes/report-result.ts:46 */}

### "That photo is still too large after shrinking. Try a different one."
**Cause:** The photo file is too big.
**Fix:** Take a new photo, or crop the one you have, and try again. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/actions.ts:62 */}

### "Say what the photo shows …"
**Cause:** You left the photo's description blank.
**Fix:** Add a short note, like "Our scorecard, round 3". {/* src: src/lib/disputes/evidence.ts:26 */}

### "Your team has added … photos already — that’s the most for one dispute."
**Cause:** Each team can add only a limited number of photos to one dispute.
**Fix:** Describe anything else under **Add your side**. {/* src: src/lib/disputes/evidence.ts:35 */}

To post your lineup, open the match's lineup from **Home** or **Manage team**, put a player on every court in every round, or tap **Auto-fill**, then tap **Submit**. Your changes save as you go. When you submit, each player in the lineup is told which rounds and courts they're playing.

{/* src: leagues-app src/lib/lineup/cta.ts:46; src/app/match/[matchId]/lineup/lineup-builder.tsx:285-297 (autosave), :695,1124 (Submit); src/lib/lineup/save.ts:154-221 (LINEUP_SUBMITTED to each placed player) */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be the team's captain.
- Ask your players whether they can play first. The builder shows each player's answer. See [See who can play and manage your team](/captains/manage-your-team).
- Your lineup has a deadline, and it locks at a set time. Both are set by your league for the season. The builder shows when yours is due.
- This is for regular-season matches. On finals day, playoff matches are set round by round at the venue instead. See [Set your playoff lineups round by round](/captains/set-playoff-lineups-round-by-round).

## Build and submit your lineup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the lineup">
    On **Home**, tap **Set lineup** on the match. You can also open it from **Manage team**, or from **Lineup** in the **Captain** menu on a computer.
    {/* src: src/lib/lineup/cta.ts:46 (Set lineup / Finish lineup / Edit lineup); src/lib/play/home.ts:436-438; src/lib/nav/play.ts:98; manage/page.tsx:30-34 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to a round">
    Use **Previous round** and **Next round** to move between rounds. Each round shows its courts and whether it's mixed doubles or men's and women's doubles.
    {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:584,623,84-86,828-837 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place players">
    Tap an empty court, then tap a player to put them there. Players who said they can't play are greyed out. Next to each name you can see how often they've already partnered that player in this match.
    {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:1226,1272,1304-1306,1357-1364 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or let Auto-fill do it">
    Tap **Squad**, then **Auto-fill the rest**. Auto-fill replaces the whole lineup, not just the empty courts. Tap **Undo** to go back.
    {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:1116,1157,663; src/lib/lineup/autofill.ts (replaces the whole lineup; prefers In, then Maybe, then No answer) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix anything flagged">
    If something breaks your league's rules, like a pair playing together too often or a court with the wrong mix, a message tells you what to change. On a phone it's in the bar at the bottom. On a computer it's under **Checks**.
    {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:1046,1098,1702-1733; src/lib/rules/rules/lineup.ts:83-227 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    When the bar says **Ready to submit**, tap **Submit**. On a computer, it's **Submit lineup**.
    {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:1107,1124,695 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see "Lineup submitted at …", and each player in it is told which rounds they're playing. A message thread for this week's lineup opens in **Messages**. {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:418; src/lib/lineup/save.ts:154-221; src/lib/messaging/lineup-thread.ts:48 */}

## When the other team sees your lineup

Your league decides when each team sees the other's lineup. Until then, their courts show **Hidden until reveal**. The builder tells you whether you submit first, are waiting on the other team, or can see theirs. {/* src: src/lib/lineup/reveal.ts:34-47,311-324 (Ruleset.revealPolicy); lineup-builder.tsx:816,957 */}

## Change a lineup you've already submitted

Make your changes, then tap **Resubmit**. On a computer, it's **Resubmit lineup**. Only the players whose rounds changed are told, and the other captain if what they see changed. You can keep changing it until it locks. {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:701,1127,419-423; src/lib/lineup/save.ts:197-200,272-277 (LINEUP_CHANGED to players whose rounds changed; notifyCounterCaptains) */}

<Note>
  Your edits are saved, but they stay private until you resubmit. The status line says **Edited — the other team sees the posted lineup until you resubmit**. The other team, the match page and the scoresheet keep showing the lineup you last submitted. {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:547-554; src/lib/lineup/save.ts:44-68 (saveAssignments: draft once submitted, A5), :192-195 (posted on resubmit) */}
</Note>

## Change a lineup after the deadline

After the deadline, a **clean swap** keeps your lineup on time: one new player takes exactly the games of a player who can't make it, and nothing else changes. Any other change after the deadline marks the lineup **Submitted late**. Either way, the other captain and the league office are told what changed. {/* src: src/lib/lineup/save.ts (cleanSwap → SUBMITTED, otherwise LATE; notifyChangeAfterDeadline) */}

A late lineup can cost your team games. The penalty is applied automatically once your league's warning weeks are over, unless the other captain agreed to the late lineup. See [Find and contact other captains](/captains/contact-other-captains) to ask for that consent. Your league's rulebook has the details. {/* src: src/lib/lineup/save.ts (applyLateLineupPenalty); src/lib/scores/penalties.ts:65-80 (Ruleset.lateLineupPenaltyGames, lateLineupWarningWeeks); src/lib/captains/messages.ts (consent removes the penalty) */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "Add 1 more men to the squad — each round needs … of each on court."
**Cause:** Every round needs a set number of men and women on court, and you haven't placed enough.
**Fix:** Place more players of that gender. If you don't have enough, tap **Ask for a sub**. It opens the **Subs** tab on **Manage team** for this match. See [Find a sub when you're short](/captains/find-a-substitute). {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/lineup.ts:80-83; lineup-builder.tsx:1073,1731-1733; src/lib/team/manage-links.ts:6-10 (?tab=subs&match=) */}

### "… are paired in rounds … This league allows a pair to play together … a night"
**Cause:** Two players are partnered more often than your league allows.
**Fix:** Pick a different partner for the round the message names. {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/lineup.ts:227 */}

### "… is a mixed court — it needs one man and one woman."
**Cause:** A court has the wrong mix of players for its type.
**Fix:** Swap one player for someone of the other gender. {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/lineup.ts:153 */}

### "Fill … more places to complete …"
**Cause:** Some courts are still empty.
**Fix:** Place a player on each empty court in the rounds named, or tap **Auto-fill the rest**. {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/lineup.ts:196 */}

### A player is greyed out with "Owes a court fee"
**Cause:** Your league blocks players with an unpaid court fee from an earlier match.
**Fix:** Ask them to pay from the link in their email or on **My payments**, or pick someone else. {/* src: src/lib/rules/rules/lineup.ts:261 (Ruleset.courtFeeUnpaidBlocksLineup); lineup-builder.tsx:1357-1364 */}

### "Couldn’t fill … without repeating a pairing too often"
**Cause:** Auto-fill ran out of combinations with the players who said they can play.
**Fix:** Fill those rounds by hand, or find a sub. {/* src: src/lib/lineup/autofill.ts:307 */}

### "This lineup has locked, so it can’t change here."
**Cause:** Your league locks lineups at a set time (the deadline or the match start, depending on the season's rules), and that time has passed. The builder shows **Locked — this lineup can’t change any more.** next to **Submit**, and changes no longer save.
**Fix:** If a change is needed, email the league office at [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com). {/* src: src/lib/lineup/save.ts:38 (assertLineupEditable); lineup-builder.tsx:557 */}

### "This match has started or finished, so its lineup can’t change here."
**Cause:** The match is under way or over, so the lineup can't be edited. You may also see "This lineup locked when the match started, so it can’t be changed here."
**Fix:** If something needs correcting, email the league office at [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com). {/* src: src/lib/lineup/save.ts:35-37; src/lib/rules/rules/lineup.ts:37 */}

### "One of those players isn’t on your roster for this match."
**Cause:** Someone you placed has left your roster for this match since you opened the builder, for example a sub whose match is settled.
**Fix:** Reload the lineup and pick from your squad. {/* src: src/lib/lineup/save.ts:39-41 */}

### "That change didn’t save — check your signal and try again."
**Cause:** Your phone lost its connection.
**Fix:** Move somewhere with better signal and make the change again. {/* src: lineup-builder.tsx:293 */}

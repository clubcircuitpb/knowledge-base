To see who can play, open **Manage team**. On a phone, tap **Captain** in the bar at the bottom of the screen. For each upcoming match it shows who's in, who's out and who hasn't answered, plus where your lineup stands. Tap **Nudge** to remind everyone who hasn't answered.

{/* src: leagues-app src/lib/nav/play.ts:105 ("Manage team"), :130 ("Captain" tab); src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx:157,303-306,324; nudge-button.tsx:22 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be your team's captain. Your club's admins make you captain.
- Your club's admins, not captains, add players to your team and move them between teams. To change who's on your roster, ask them.

{/* src: src/lib/auth/guards.ts:105-117 (requireTeamCaptain); src/lib/club/teams.ts:163, src/lib/club/admin.ts:278,375 (roster changes are requireClubAdmin); src/app/team/[teamId]/page.tsx:422 ("Once the club adds players to this team, they appear here.") */}

## Check who can play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage team">
    On a phone, tap **Captain** at the bottom of the screen. On a computer, select **Manage team** in the **Captain** menu. You can also tap **Manage team** on your team's page.
    {/* src: src/lib/nav/play.ts:86,105,130; src/app/team/[teamId]/page.tsx:163 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at your next match">
    The **Next match** card lists your players under **In**, **Maybe**, **Out** and **Haven’t answered**.
    {/* src: src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx:303-306,324 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remind anyone who hasn't answered">
    Tap **Nudge the 1 who hasn’t answered**, or **Nudge the 3 who haven’t answered**. The number on the button is how many haven't answered. They get a reminder email with buttons to answer.
    {/* src: src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/nudge-button.tsx:22; src/lib/availability/nudge.ts (sends AVAILABILITY_REMINDER_MANUAL with answer buttons) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your lineup status">
    Next to each match, the lineup shows as **Not submitted**, **Draft**, **Submitted** or **Late**. Tap **Set lineup**, **Finish lineup** or **Edit lineup** to open the lineup builder.
    {/* src: src/components/ui/badge.tsx:77-80; src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx:30-34 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You can see at a glance who's playing each match and which lineups still need you. Below the next match, **Still to play** lists the rest of your matches. {/* src: src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx:205 */}

## Other things you can do from Manage team

- **Decide on DUPR.** Below your matches, the **DUPR** section shows whether your team's games count toward DUPR, and lets you opt in. It appears only if your league offers DUPR this season. See [Choose whether your games count toward DUPR](/captains/opt-in-to-dupr). {/* src: manage/page.tsx:239-243 (DuprTeamPanel at 241); src/components/dupr/dupr-team-panel.tsx:11-46 */}
- **Submit your playoff roster.** If your team is playing on finals day, tap **Playoff roster** at the top of the page. See [Submit your playoff roster for finals day](/captains/submit-your-playoff-roster). {/* src: manage/page.tsx:63-67,161-165 */}
- **Message your team.** Tap **Team chat**. As captain, you can tick **Announce (emails the roster)** to send a message by email as well as in the chat. {/* src: manage/page.tsx:167; src/app/team/[teamId]/chat/team-chat.tsx */}
- **Find a sub.** Open the **Subs** tab, or tap **Need a sub?** on a match. See [Find a sub when you're short](/captains/find-a-substitute). {/* src: manage/page.tsx:180; sub-request-control.tsx:88 */}
- **Contact another captain.** Tap **Captains directory**. See [Find and contact other captains](/captains/contact-other-captains). {/* src: manage/page.tsx:170 */}
- **See your full roster.** Open your team's page and tap **Roster**. Subs appear on it only for the match they're playing. {/* src: src/app/team/[teamId]/page.tsx:27,201; src/lib/subs/expire.ts:61-89 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### I can't find Manage team or the Captain tab
**Cause:** You're not set as this team's captain yet. Only the team's captain sees these.
**Fix:** Ask your club's admins to make you captain. {/* src: src/lib/auth/guards.ts:105-117; manage/page.tsx:53-58 (404 when not captain) */}

### I want to add or remove a player
**Cause:** Captains can't change who's on the roster. Your club's admins place players on teams.
**Fix:** Ask your club's admins. For one match, you can bring in a sub instead. See [Find a sub when you're short](/captains/find-a-substitute). {/* src: src/lib/club/admin.ts */}

### I don't see Playoff roster
**Cause:** It appears only for a team playing on finals day.
**Fix:** Check the standings. If your team has made the playoffs and it still isn't there, ask the league office. {/* src: manage/page.tsx:63-67 (semifinal on a division with a finals day) */}

### "You nudged this match recently."
**Cause:** You can nudge each match only so often, so players aren't flooded with reminders.
**Fix:** Wait for the time shown, then try again. {/* src: src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/actions.ts:14; src/lib/rate-limit/limiter.ts:108 */}

### "Everyone on your team has answered — nobody to nudge."
**Cause:** Every player has already answered for that match.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. You're ready to set your lineup. {/* src: src/lib/availability/nudge.ts:46 */}

### "This match has already started or isn’t scheduled, so there’s no one to nudge."
**Cause:** You can only nudge players for an upcoming match.
**Fix:** Nothing to do for this match. {/* src: src/lib/availability/nudge.ts:38 */}

### "No upcoming matches"
**Cause:** The league hasn't published your team's schedule yet, or your season's matches are all played.
**Fix:** Check back once the schedule is published. {/* src: manage/page.tsx:197 */}

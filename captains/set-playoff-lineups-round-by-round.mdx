On finals day, playoff lineups are set one round at a time at the venue. Open the match's lineup. When it says **Your turn**, pick a pair for every court, tap **Submit round … pairs** or **Submit round … response**, then **Yes, lock it in**. The other captain sees your pairs and responds, or you respond to theirs.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/match/[matchId]/lineup/page.tsx:80-114 (turn-based playoff match, ADR 0081); src/app/match/[matchId]/lineup/playoff-rounds.tsx:146-153,289-304 */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be your team's captain, at the venue with your phone.
- Your playoff roster has to be in. Only players on it, and approved by the league office if they needed it, can be placed. See [Submit your playoff roster for finals day](/captains/submit-your-playoff-roster).
- Each round has a leading team and a responding team, and each captain has a set time for their turn. A late turn can cost your team games. See [Playoffs](/rules/rally-21/playoffs) in the rulebook for the order and the times.

## Set your pairs for a round

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the lineup">
    Open the playoff match and tap **Set lineup**. The screen is called **Round-by-round lineups**.
    {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/page.tsx:272-283 (lineupCta, href /match/[matchId]/lineup); src/lib/lineup/cta.ts:46; src/app/match/[matchId]/lineup/page.tsx:94 (title "Round-by-round lineups") */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for your turn">
    The bar at the top says whose turn it is. When it's yours, it starts with **Your turn**, and the clock says **You have … left.**
    {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:146-153,173-176,320-330 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a pair for each court">
    Each court is labelled, for example **Round 1, Court 2 · Mixed**. Choose a player in each box. If you're responding, each court shows who you're playing **Against**.
    {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:194-224 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix anything flagged">
    If a pair breaks a rule, like teammates who already partnered in this match, a message under the courts says what to change.
    {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:230-236; src/lib/playoffs/turns.ts:170-218 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Submit round … pairs** if you're leading, or **Submit round … response** if you're responding.
    {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:302-304 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lock it in">
    You're asked "Lock in round …? You can’t change it after." Tap **Yes, lock it in**, or **Keep editing** to go back.
    {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:289-299 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Round … pairs submitted and locked.** or **Round … response submitted and locked.** The other captain's screen updates by itself. When both lineups for a round are in, the round shows **On court**. {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:129,86-91,257 */}

## Start the next round

When a round's games are done, tap **Round … is finished**. The next round's clock starts, and the captain who leads it gets their turn. {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:135-142,154,178-183; src/lib/playoffs/round-lineup.ts:285-292 */}

## If the match ends level: the deciding round

If the match is level after the regular rounds, a **Deciding round** card appears. Each captain picks a pair for each deciding court. Pairs that already played together may play together again.

1. Choose a player in each box.
2. Tap **Submit deciding pairs**.
3. Tap **Yes, lock them in**.

Your picks stay hidden until the other team is in too, then both are revealed at once. {/* src: src/app/match/[matchId]/lineup/playoff-decider.tsx:42,67,74-88,140-155 */}

## If the app isn't working

Use the paper scoresheet, swap pairs on paper in the same order, and tell the league office. {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:281-284; rules/rally-21/playoffs ("If the app isn't working, captains write their pairs on paper and swap them in the same order.") */}

## Why isn't this working?

### "Your playoff roster isn’t in, so nobody can be placed."
**Cause:** Your team didn't submit a playoff roster.
**Fix:** Use paper and tell the league office. {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:168-172; src/lib/playoffs/round-lineup.ts:214 */}

### "… leads round …. Wait for their pairs — you’ll respond to them."
**Cause:** The other team leads this round, and hasn't submitted yet.
**Fix:** Wait. Their pairs appear by themselves, and then it's your turn. {/* src: src/lib/playoffs/turns.ts:123; playoff-rounds.tsx:155 */}

### "The clock starts when the round before is finished."
**Cause:** Nobody has tapped **Round … is finished** for the round before.
**Fix:** When the round's games are done, tap **Round … is finished**. {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:322 */}

### The clock says "… late."
**Cause:** Your turn's time has run out.
**Fix:** Submit as soon as you can. A late turn is recorded, and can cost your team games. {/* src: playoff-rounds.tsx:329,130 */}

### "… already partnered in round … — teammates partner once in a playoff match."
**Cause:** That pair already played together in this match.
**Fix:** Split them up on the court the message names. {/* src: src/lib/playoffs/turns.ts:214-215 */}

### "… that player isn’t on today’s playoff roster, or is still waiting on the league office."
**Cause:** The player isn't on your playoff roster, or needs the league office's approval first.
**Fix:** Pick someone else, or ask the league office about the approval. {/* src: src/lib/playoffs/turns.ts:193 */}

### "Round … is locked — both lineups are in."
**Cause:** That round's lineups are already locked.
**Fix:** Nothing to change. If something's wrong, tell the league office. {/* src: src/lib/playoffs/turns.ts:119 */}

To choose who can play for your team in the playoffs, open **Manage team**, tap **Playoff roster**, tick your players and tap **Send playoff roster**. Only players on it can go in a playoff lineup, so send it before the deadline.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/team/[teamId]/playoff-roster/page.tsx; src/lib/lineup/save.ts (playoff lineups take only rostered players); docs/adr/0077-playoff-eligibility-and-roster.md */}

**Before you begin**

- Your team needs to be in the playoffs. The tab appears once the league office seeds them.
- You need to be the team’s captain, or an admin of the team’s club.
- Your league sets how many of each gender you can list, the deadline, and how many games a player needs to be a regular. The page shows them. See your league’s rules in the app for the details.

{/* src: src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx (Playoff roster tab when seeded); src/lib/auth/guards.ts requireTeamCaptainOrClubAdmin; src/lib/playoffs/roster.ts getPlayoffRosterForm (max per gender, due time and minimum from the Ruleset) */}

## Send your roster

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your playoff roster">
    As captain, open your team’s page, tap **Manage team**, then **Playoff roster**. As a club admin, open **Overview** in Club HQ and tap **Playoff roster** on the team’s card.
    {/* src: src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx; src/app/club/[slug]/admin/page.tsx (Playoff roster, seeded teams only) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each player’s standing">
    Each player shows their games for your team and a label: **Regular**, **Regular in a lower division**, **Substitute, approved**, **Needs league approval** or **Regular in a higher division — can’t play**.
    {/* src: src/lib/playoffs/eligibility-rules.ts STANDING_LABEL */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick your players">
    Tick the men and women who may play. Players marked **Needs league approval** or **Regular in a higher division — can’t play** can’t be ticked.
    {/* src: playoff-roster/page.tsx (checkboxes disabled unless the standing is rosterable); eligibility-rules.ts ROSTERABLE */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Tap **Send playoff roster**. To change it later, tick or untick players and tap **Update playoff roster**.
    {/* src: playoff-roster/page.tsx (button labels) */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** the page says when the roster was sent, and only those players can go in your playoff lineups.

{/* src: playoff-roster/page.tsx ("Sent …"); src/lib/playoffs/roster.ts submitPlayoffRoster ("Saved. Only these players can go in your playoff lineups.") */}

## Who can play

- **Regulars** have played your league’s minimum number of regular-season games for your team.
- A **regular on a lower-division team** of your club can play too.
- A **regular on a higher-division team** of your club can’t. If they play anyway, every game they play is forfeited.
- **Anyone else** needs the league office’s approval first, and costs your team part of a game for each game they play.

## Why isn’t this working?

### I don’t see the Playoff roster tab

**Cause:** your team isn’t in the playoffs yet, or you aren’t its captain or one of its club’s admins.

**Fix:** the tab appears once the league office seeds the playoffs and your team qualifies. If you should have access, ask your club’s admins.

{/* src: manage/page.tsx (tab only when seeded); playoff-roster/page.tsx ("Not in the playoffs") */}

### A player says Needs league approval

**Cause:** they haven’t played enough regular-season games for your team, and aren’t a regular on a lower-division team of your club.

**Fix:** email [support@clubcircuitpb.com](mailto:support@clubcircuitpb.com) and ask the league office to approve them. Once approved, they show as **Substitute, approved** and you can tick them.

{/* src: eligibility-rules.ts (NEEDS_APPROVAL → APPROVED_SUB once the league office records an override); src/lib/playoffs/roster.ts classifyForTeam (override rule PLAYOFF_MIN_GAMES) */}

### It says the roster deadline has passed

**Cause:** your league’s deadline for playoff rosters is over.

**Fix:** send it anyway. A late roster costs your team games in each playoff match that day, so send it as soon as you can.

{/* src: playoff-roster/page.tsx ("The roster deadline has passed…"); roster.ts applyLateRosterPenalties */}

### My lineup says a player isn’t on our playoff roster

**Cause:** a playoff lineup only takes players on your playoff roster.

**Fix:** take them out of the lineup, or add them to the roster if they can play.

{/* src: src/lib/lineup/save.ts playoffRosterProblem ("… isn’t on your playoff roster.") */}

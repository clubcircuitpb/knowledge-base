To contact another captain, open **Manage team** and tap **Captains directory**. Find their name, then tap what you need: **Notify of a lineup change**, **Request late-lineup consent**, **Propose reschedule dates** or **Flag a score discrepancy**. Each one is recorded on the match, and the other captain is notified.

{/* src: leagues-app src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx:170; src/app/captains/captains-directory.tsx:19-25,397; src/app/captains/page.tsx */}

**Before you begin**

- You need to be a captain (or a club admin) to see the directory.
- You can send a request only to a captain whose team plays yours in a match that isn't finished yet.

{/* src: src/lib/captains/directory.ts:100-105 (captain, club admin, market or super admin; a plain player gets a 404), :156 (actions only for a shared match); captains-directory.tsx:165,202 */}

## Send a request to another captain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Captains directory">
    Go to **Manage team** and tap **Captains directory**.
    {/* src: src/app/team/[teamId]/manage/page.tsx:170 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the captain">
    Type their name in **Search captains by first or last name**, or look under their club.
    {/* src: src/app/captains/page.tsx:48; captains-directory.tsx:49-53 (grouped by club) */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what you need">
    Tap **Notify of a lineup change**, **Request late-lineup consent**, **Propose reschedule dates** or **Flag a score discrepancy**.
    {/* src: src/app/captains/captains-directory.tsx:19-25,205-218 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    If you play them more than once, choose **Which match**. Then say what's changing, why you need consent, or what doesn't match.
    {/* src: captains-directory.tsx:411,426-434 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Tap **Send**, or **Request consent** for a late-lineup request.
    {/* src: captains-directory.tsx:447 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** You see **Sent, and recorded on the match. The other captain has been notified.** {/* src: captains-directory.tsx:397 */}

To move a match, see [Ask to reschedule a match](/captains/request-a-reschedule). **Propose reschedule dates** opens its own form, which checks your league's reschedule rules and the host club's courts before it sends. {/* src: captains-directory.tsx:234-236; src/app/captains/actions.ts (sendCaptainMessageAction accepts only the three message kinds; reschedules go through proposeRescheduleAction) */}

## Answer a request

Requests other captains send you appear at the top of the directory under **Waiting on you**. For a late-lineup request, tap **Grant consent** or **Decline**. For a lineup change or a score discrepancy, tap **Acknowledge**. For a reschedule request, pick a date or tap **None of these work**; see [Ask to reschedule a match](/captains/request-a-reschedule). {/* src: captains-directory.tsx:461,592,636-640 */}

## See the reschedule requests you've sent

Below **Waiting on you**, **Sent requests** lists the reschedule requests your team sent that the other captain hasn't answered yet, with the times you offered. To take one back, tap **Withdraw**, then **Yes, withdraw**. The match stays on its original date, and the other captain is told. {/* src: captains-directory.tsx:42,477-540; src/lib/captains/directory.ts (sent: RESCHEDULE_PROPOSAL still SENT); src/lib/reschedule/flow.ts:334-361 */}

<Tip>
  If your lineup went in late and the other captain taps **Grant consent**, the late-lineup penalty is removed automatically. If it's still showing, open the match page and tap **Appeal the penalty**. How late lineups are penalized is in your league's rulebook. {/* src: src/lib/captains/messages.ts (granting LATE_LINEUP_CONSENT removes the AUTO late-lineup penalty); src/app/match/[matchId]/late-lineup-appeal.tsx:43 */}
</Tip>

## Share your phone number with other captains

Other captains can't see your email address. They see your phone number only if you choose to share it, and then only other captains in your league and the league office see it, not club admins who aren't captains. To share it, open your account, turn on **Share my phone number with other captains** under **Your details**, and select **Save details**. {/* src: src/lib/captains/directory.ts:198-201 (phone: opted in AND viewer captains a team or is league office; email: league office or the captain's club admins only); src/app/me/page.tsx:203,246,264 */}

## Why isn't this working?

### There are no buttons on a captain's card
**Cause:** Your teams don't have a match together that's still to be played or confirmed. Requests are always about a specific match.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. If you need them for something else, ask the league office. {/* src: src/lib/captains/directory.ts:156; captains-directory.tsx:226 ("No shared match with this captain yet.") */}

### I can't open the Captains directory
**Cause:** The directory is only for captains and club admins.
**Fix:** If you've just been made captain, sign out and back in. If it still doesn't open, ask your club's admins to check that you're set as captain. {/* src: src/lib/captains/directory.ts (404 for a plain player) */}

### "That request has already been answered."
**Cause:** You, or another captain on your team, already answered it.
**Fix:** Nothing to do. The answer is recorded on the match. {/* src: src/lib/captains/messages.ts */}

### "No late-lineup consent was recorded from the opposing captain, so the penalty stands."
**Cause:** You appealed a late-lineup penalty, but the other captain hasn't granted consent.
**Fix:** Ask them to tap **Grant consent** in the Captains directory, then appeal again. {/* src: src/lib/captains/messages.ts */}

### "No captain matches …"
**Cause:** No captain in your league has that name.
**Fix:** Check the spelling, or clear the search and look under their club. {/* src: captains-directory.tsx:46 */}

### "Answer a reschedule request by picking one of its dates, or None of these work."
**Cause:** A reschedule request can't be acknowledged like a message.
**Fix:** Pick one of the dates under **Pick one** and tap **Accept**, or tap **None of these work**. {/* src: src/lib/captains/messages.ts (RESCHEDULE_PROPOSAL refused by respondConsent) */}

Seasons are how Club Circuit organizes active periods of competition within your league. Each season has a defined start and end date, contains one or more rounds of play, and produces a final set of standings when closed. You can run as many seasons as you like under a single league, keeping your historical data intact while always moving forward.

## Season vs. Round Structure

Understanding the two-level structure makes it easier to plan your competition calendar:

- **Season** — The top-level time window for a competition period (e.g., "Spring 2025"). A season holds all the rounds, matches, registrations, and standings for that period. When the season ends, its standings are archived and visible to all participants.
- **Round** — A subdivision within a season representing a distinct phase of play (e.g., "Week 1", "Round 1", "Playoff Round"). Rounds help you group matches logically, especially for round-robin or multi-week formats. A season must have at least one round before you can schedule matches.

## Create a New Season

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your League">
    From the **Leagues** dashboard, click into the league where you want to add a season.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Seasons">
    Select the **Seasons** tab, then click **New Season**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name Your Season">
    Enter a season name (e.g., "Fall 2025" or "Q3 Competitive Season"). This name is visible to all registered players and appears on standings pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Start and End Dates">
    Choose the season's start and end dates. These define the registration window defaults and determine when the season appears as active on your league page. You can adjust dates later as long as the season hasn't been closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Divisions">
    Choose which league divisions are active in this season. You can include all divisions or just a subset — useful if a division doesn't have enough registrations to run in a given period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Activate">
    Click **Save Season**. The season is created in **Draft** status. When you're ready to open registration and begin scheduling, click **Activate Season**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add Rounds to a Season

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Season">
    From the Seasons tab, click on the active season you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Rounds">
    Select the **Rounds** tab within the season view, then click **Add Round**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Round">
    Give the round a name or number (e.g., "Round 1" or "Semifinal"). Set the round's start and end dates. Optionally, add a description to communicate what this round represents to players.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Matches to the Round">
    Once the round is saved, use the **Schedule** tool to assign matches to it. You can auto-generate a round-robin schedule or add matches manually. See [Scheduling](/matches/schedule) for full details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can create all your rounds upfront at the start of a season — even before matches are scheduled — so players can see the full competition calendar as soon as the season activates.
</Tip>

## Close Out a Season

When all matches are complete and results are finalized, close the season to lock standings and archive the data.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify All Scores Are Submitted">
    Go to **Matches** within the season and confirm there are no matches in a pending or disputed state. Unresolved matches will be flagged before you can close the season.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Final Standings">
    Open the **Standings** tab to review rankings across each division. Make any manual adjustments if needed (for forfeits, defaults, or corrections) before closing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the Season">
    Click **Close Season** from the Season Settings menu. You'll be prompted to confirm. Once closed, the season's standings are published as final and the season is marked as **Archived**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share Final Standings">
    After closing, use the **Share Standings** button to distribute a public link to the final standings page — great for social media, email newsletters, or your club website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Closing a season is irreversible. Any matches still in **Scheduled** or **In Progress** status will be automatically marked as **Cancelled** when the season closes. Make sure all results are submitted and any disputes are resolved before you proceed. If you need to reopen a closed season, contact Club Circuit support.
</Warning>

## Running Multiple Seasons Concurrently

<Tip>
  Club Circuit supports multiple active seasons at the same time within a single league. This is useful if you run overlapping divisions on different schedules (e.g., a recreational season that runs year-round alongside a competitive season that runs quarterly). Each season manages its own registration, rounds, and standings independently — players registered in one season don't automatically appear in another.
</Tip>

## League Roles

Club Circuit uses three roles to define what participants can do inside a league:

- **League Admin** — Full control over the league. You can create and edit seasons, manage registration, publish schedules, record scores, and access all league settings. Typically the organizer or facility manager.
- **Team Captain** — Manages a single team within the league. Captains can submit scores, communicate with their team, and manage their roster up to the limits you define.
- **Player** — A registered participant. Players can view their schedule, check standings, and report scores if you enable that permission.

You assign roles when inviting participants or when approving registrations. A single person can hold multiple roles across different leagues.

## Leagues vs. Seasons

<Note>
  A **League** is the top-level container — it holds your brand, format settings, division structure, and rules. A **Season** is a time-bounded period of play that runs inside a league. One league can host many seasons (e.g., Spring 2024, Fall 2024, Winter 2025) without you needing to recreate your league settings each time. Think of the league as the organization and the season as the competition window.
</Note>

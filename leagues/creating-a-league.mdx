Setting up a new league on Club Circuit takes just a few minutes. Once you've created your league, you can invite co-admins, define your division structure, configure registration settings, and start building seasons. Everything you set here forms the foundation for every season you run under this league.

## Create Your League

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Leagues">
    From your Club Circuit dashboard, select **Leagues** in the left navigation panel, then click **Create New League**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set League Name and Description">
    Enter a clear, public-facing name for your league (e.g., "Riverside Pickleball Club — Competitive League"). Add a description that tells prospective players what to expect — skill level, location, and style of play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Format">
    Select the play format for your league. This determines how matches are structured across the season. See [League Formats](#league-formats) below for details on each option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Division Structure">
    Add one or more divisions to your league (e.g., 3.5+, 4.0+, Open). Each division can have its own registration limits, team sizes, and scheduling rules. You can edit divisions later, but changes may affect existing season configurations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Registration Settings">
    Set whether registration is open to the public or invite-only. Define capacity limits, team size requirements, and any registration fee. You can also require players to agree to a waiver before completing sign-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish Your League">
    Review all your settings, then click **Publish League**. Once published, your league page is live and visible according to your privacy settings. You can still edit settings before opening registration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## League Formats

Club Circuit supports three primary competition formats. Choose the one that best fits your league's competitive style and season length.

| Format | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Round Robin** | Every team or player competes against every other participant in their division. Points accumulate across matches to determine standings. | Recreational and competitive leagues with consistent participation |
| **Bracket** | Single or double elimination. Teams are seeded and compete in a tournament-style draw until a champion is crowned. | End-of-season playoffs or standalone tournaments |
| **Ladder** | Players or teams are ranked on a ladder and can challenge those above them to move up. The ladder updates after each match result is submitted. | Ongoing, flexible leagues without fixed schedules |

<Note>
  You can combine formats within a single season — for example, run a round-robin regular season followed by a bracket playoff. Configure this when you set up a new season under your league.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the league format after publishing?">
    You can update the format before any season has started. Once a season is active with scheduled matches, changing the format requires ending or deleting that season first. We recommend finalizing your format before opening registration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many divisions can I add to a league?">
    There is no hard limit on the number of divisions. However, each division requires at least one active season to appear on your public league page. Divisions with no scheduled seasons are hidden from players.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I co-admin a league with someone else?">
    Yes. After creating your league, go to **League Settings → Admins** and invite additional League Admins by email. Co-admins have the same permissions as you within that league.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I need to delete a league?">
    Deleting a league permanently removes all associated seasons, matches, registrations, and standings. This action cannot be undone. If you want to pause a league temporarily, set it to **Inactive** in League Settings instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I duplicate a league I've already set up?">
    Yes. From the Leagues list, click the **⋯** menu on any existing league and select **Duplicate League**. This copies your format, division structure, and settings into a new draft league — registration and match data are not copied.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Public vs. Invite-Only Leagues

<Note>
  **Public leagues** appear in Club Circuit's league directory and can be found by any registered player. Anyone can view the league page and submit a registration request.

  **Invite-only leagues** are hidden from the directory. Only players who receive a direct invitation link can access the registration form. Use this option for private club leagues, corporate events, or curated competitive groups.

  You can switch between public and invite-only at any time from **League Settings → Visibility**, as long as registration is not currently open.
</Note>

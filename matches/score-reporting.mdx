Accurate score reporting keeps your league standings current and ensures every team gets the points they've earned. Club Circuit's score submission flow is quick to complete from any device — enter your game scores right from the court, wait for your opponent to confirm, and watch your standings update automatically. Both teams play a role in keeping the record accurate and fair.

## Reporting a Score After Your Match

Either team can submit the score first. To report match results:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your match">
    Open the **Matches → Schedule** view and find the match you just played. Completed matches are flagged with a **"Score Pending"** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Report Score'">
    Select the match and tap or click the **Report Score** button to open the score entry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the game scores">
    Input the score for each game played (e.g., 11–7, 11–9). If you played a tiebreak game, toggle **Tiebreak Played** and enter those scores separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the match outcome">
    Confirm which team won. Club Circuit will validate that your scores are consistent with the outcome you've selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your score">
    Click **Submit Score**. Your opponent will receive a notification asking them to confirm the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Score Confirmation

Club Circuit uses a two-party confirmation system to ensure accuracy. After one team submits a score, the opposing team must review and confirm the result before it becomes official and updates the standings.

To confirm a score your opponent reported:

1. Open the notification you received or go to **Matches → Pending Confirmations**.
2. Review the submitted scores for each game.
3. If the scores are correct, click **Confirm Score**.
4. The result is locked in and standings are updated immediately.

<Info>
  Both teams receive a confirmation receipt once a score is accepted. You can view the finalized result at any time under **Matches → Results**.
</Info>

## Disputed Scores

If the score your opponent submitted doesn't match your records, you can flag it as disputed instead of confirming.

1. On the confirmation screen, click **Dispute Score**.
2. Enter the scores as you recorded them and add a brief note explaining the discrepancy.
3. Your league organizer will be notified and will review both submissions.
4. The organizer will either correct the score manually or reach out to both teams to resolve the issue.

<Note>
  While a dispute is open, the match result remains **Pending** and does not affect standings. Standings update only after the dispute is resolved.
</Note>

## Score Submission Deadline

<Warning>
  Scores must be submitted within **24 hours** of the match end time. After this window closes, the submission form is locked and you'll need to contact your league organizer directly to record the result. Repeated late submissions may result in a forfeit being recorded.
</Warning>

## Forfeit Rules

<Note>
  If a team fails to show up for a scheduled match without notifying the organizer in advance, the match may be recorded as a forfeit. A forfeit counts as a loss for the absent team and a win for the opposing team, with a standard score applied based on your league's rulebook settings. Contact your league organizer if you believe a forfeit was recorded in error.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if my opponent doesn't confirm the score?">
    If your opponent hasn't confirmed the score within **48 hours** of your submission, the result is automatically escalated to your league organizer for review. The organizer can approve the score unilaterally so your standings aren't delayed indefinitely. You can check the status of any pending confirmation under **Matches → Pending Confirmations**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a submitted score?">
    You can edit a submitted score **before your opponent confirms it**. Go to **Matches → Pending Confirmations**, find your submission, and click **Edit Score**. Once your opponent confirms, the score is locked. If you need to correct a confirmed score, contact your league organizer — only organizers can modify finalized results.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

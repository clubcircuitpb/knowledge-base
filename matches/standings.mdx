Club Circuit updates league standings automatically every time a match score is confirmed, so you always have an accurate, real-time picture of where your team sits in the division. Understanding how standings are calculated — and what happens when teams are tied — helps you stay focused on what matters most as the season progresses toward playoff cutoffs.

## How Standings Are Calculated

Your position in the standings is determined by the points your team accumulates across all confirmed matches in the season. Points are awarded as follows:

- **Win** — 2 points
- **Loss** — 0 points
- **Bye (scheduled rest round)** — 1 point

In addition to match points, Club Circuit tracks game-level statistics that come into play for tiebreakers and provide a fuller picture of your team's performance.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Rank** | Your current position in the division standings |
| **Team** | Your registered team name |
| **W** | Total matches won |
| **L** | Total matches lost |
| **PF** | Points For — total points scored across all games |
| **PA** | Points Against — total points scored against you across all games |
| **PD** | Point Differential — PF minus PA |
| **Points** | Total standing points (2 per win, 0 per loss, 1 per bye) |

## Tiebreaker Rules

When two or more teams finish with the same number of standing points, Club Circuit resolves ties using the following criteria, applied in order:

1. **Head-to-head record** — the team with a better record in direct matchups between the tied teams ranks higher.
2. **Point differential (PD)** — if head-to-head doesn't break the tie, the team with the higher PD ranks higher.
3. **Points For (PF)** — if PD is also equal, the team that scored more total points across the season ranks higher.
4. **Points Against (PA)** — in the rare case that all prior criteria are equal, the team that allowed fewer total points ranks higher.
5. **Organizer discretion** — if all statistical tiebreakers are still equal, your league organizer resolves the tie manually and notes the reasoning in the league announcements.

<Info>
  Tiebreaker calculations update automatically alongside standings whenever a new score is confirmed. You can always see the live tiebreaker breakdown by hovering over a tied rank in the standings table.
</Info>

## Playoff Qualification Cutoffs

Each league sets its own playoff qualification threshold, which your organizer configures when the season is created. Common formats include:

- **Top N teams** — the top 4, 6, or 8 teams by standing points advance to playoffs.
- **Division winners** — the first-place team from each division qualifies automatically, with additional wildcard spots for the next-highest ranked teams.
- **Points threshold** — teams must reach a minimum standing points total to qualify.

Your league's specific playoff format is listed on the **Standings** page under the **Playoff Format** tab. Teams currently inside the qualification cutoff are highlighted in the standings table with a green indicator. Teams on the bubble are marked in yellow.

<Note>
  Playoff brackets are generated automatically by Club Circuit once the regular season ends and all scores are confirmed. Organizers can also seed the bracket manually if your league uses a custom seeding method.
</Note>

## Monitoring Your Standings Throughout the Season

<Tip>
  Check your standings after every confirmed match — not just at the end of the season. Staying aware of your position early gives you time to focus on point differential during dominant wins, which can be the deciding factor if you're chasing down a tiebreaker late in the season. You can access the live leaderboard at any time via **League → Standings** or the **Standings** tab in the mobile app.
</Tip>

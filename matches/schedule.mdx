Club Circuit keeps your entire match calendar in one place so you always know when and where you're playing. Whether you're a player checking your next game or a league organizer building out a full season, the Schedule section gives you real-time visibility into every match — from court assignments to opponent details — so nothing catches you off guard.

## Viewing Your Upcoming Matches

Your personal schedule shows every match assigned to you for the current season. To access it, navigate to **Matches → Schedule** from the main sidebar.

Each match card displays the following information:

- **Date and time** — the scheduled start time for your match
- **Location** — the facility name and address
- **Court assignment** — the specific court number or name at the venue
- **Opponent** — your opposing team's name and division
- **Match format** — singles, doubles, or mixed doubles

<Tip>
  Click any match card to open the full match detail view, where you can see player rosters, any organizer notes, and the match history between your two teams.
</Tip>

## League Organizer Tools

If you manage a league, Club Circuit gives you a full suite of scheduling tools to build and adjust your season calendar without ever touching a spreadsheet.

### Generating a Round-Robin Schedule

Club Circuit can automatically generate a balanced round-robin schedule based on the teams, courts, and time slots you configure. To generate a schedule for a new round:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your league dashboard">
    Go to **Manage League → Schedule** and select the season you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your teams and venues">
    Confirm that all registered teams and available court slots are listed. Add any missing venues or time blocks before generating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure round settings">
    Set the number of rounds, matches per round, and any blackout dates when courts are unavailable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the schedule">
    Click **Generate Schedule**. Club Circuit will produce a conflict-free round-robin draw and show you a preview before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Review the generated schedule for any adjustments, then click **Publish** to make it visible to all league members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Manual Adjustments

After generating a schedule, you can drag and drop individual matches to different time slots or courts directly on the schedule grid. Changes are saved automatically and players are notified of any updates.

<Info>
  You can also add one-off matches — such as makeup games or playoff rounds — by clicking **Add Match** and filling in the details manually.
</Info>

## Exporting Matches to Your Calendar

Never miss a match by syncing your Club Circuit schedule with your personal calendar.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="google" href="/matches/schedule">
    Click **Export → Add to Google Calendar** on any match card or on the full schedule view. You'll be redirected to Google Calendar with the event pre-filled.
  </Card>
  <Card title="iCal / Apple Calendar" icon="calendar" href="/matches/schedule">
    Click **Export → Download .ics** to download an iCal file. Open it to import the match into Apple Calendar, Outlook, or any iCal-compatible app.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can also export your entire season schedule at once by going to **Schedule → Export All** and choosing your preferred format.

## Schedule Change Notifications

<Note>
  Whenever a league organizer modifies a published schedule — including time changes, court reassignments, or cancellations — you'll automatically receive a notification via email and in-app alert. You don't need to manually refresh your schedule to see updates.
</Note>

## Mobile App Schedule View

<Tip>
  The Club Circuit mobile app includes a streamlined schedule view optimized for on-the-go access. Swipe between weeks, tap a match for full details, and add it to your phone's native calendar with a single tap — all without opening a browser.
</Tip>

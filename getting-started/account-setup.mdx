Before you can join a league or start tracking your matches, you need a Club Circuit account. Creating one takes just a few minutes — all you need is a valid email address or a linked Google or Apple account. Once you're in, completing your player profile ensures that league organizers and teammates have everything they need to place you correctly and keep your season running smoothly.

## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Go to [clubcircuit.app/signup](https://clubcircuit.app/signup) and enter your email address, or click **Continue with Google** / **Continue with Apple** to register instantly. Choose a strong password if signing up with email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Club Circuit and click the **Confirm My Account** link. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam folder or request a new verification email from the login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Player Profile">
    After verifying your email, you'll be guided through the profile setup wizard. Fill in your display name, select your skill level, and enter your city or region so leagues near you can surface in search results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Profile Photo">
    Add a profile photo so teammates and opponents can recognize you. Select a clear headshot or action shot — your photo appears on your public player card, team rosters, and match scorecards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Required Profile Fields

To participate in leagues on Club Circuit, your profile must include the following information:

- **Display Name** — The name shown on leaderboards, rosters, and match results. This can be your real name or a recognized player handle.
- **Skill Level** — Choose from Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Competitive. This helps organizers place you in the right division and ensures fair matchups.
- **Location / City** — Your city or region is used to surface nearby leagues and local events. Your exact address is never shared publicly.

<Tip>
  **Choosing the right skill level:** Be honest with yourself when selecting your skill level — it directly affects which divisions you'll be eligible for. If you're unsure, lean toward Beginner or Intermediate until you've played a few competitive matches. You can always update your skill level later from **Profile → Edit Profile**.
</Tip>

## Account Settings

Once your profile is complete, take a moment to review your account settings. Navigate to **Settings → Account** to configure:

- **Email Preferences** — Choose which notifications you'd like to receive, including match reminders, score confirmations, league announcements, and Club Circuit product updates.
- **Privacy Settings** — Control who can view your profile, stats, and match history. You can set your profile to Public, League Members Only, or Private.

<Note>
  You can update your email address or password at any time under **Settings → Security**. If you signed up with Google or Apple, you can add a password to your account to enable direct email login as well.
</Note>

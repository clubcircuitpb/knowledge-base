Your Club Circuit dashboard is the central hub for everything happening in your leagues. From the moment you log in, you'll see an at-a-glance summary of upcoming matches, recent results, your team's standing, and any important messages from your league organizer. Understanding the layout helps you stay organized and never miss a game day detail.

## Dashboard Sections

The dashboard is divided into four primary sections, each giving you quick access to a different part of your league experience.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="My Schedule" icon="calendar" href="/matches/schedule">
    View all upcoming and past matches in a clean calendar or list format. See match times, court locations, and opponent details — and sync your schedule directly to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team Roster" icon="users" href="/players/team-management">
    Manage your team's active players, view each player's profile and skill level, and handle any pending roster invites or requests from your captain or co-captain.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Standings" icon="trophy" href="/matches/standings">
    Track your team's current rank within your division. Standings update automatically after every confirmed match result — wins, losses, point differentials, and tiebreakers are all calculated for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Announcements" icon="bell" href="/communication/announcements">
    Stay in the loop with league-wide messages from your organizer. Announcements cover schedule changes, rule clarifications, playoff seeding details, and end-of-season event information.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Top Navigation and Sidebar

The **top navigation bar** runs across the top of every page and gives you quick access to:

- **Notifications** (bell icon) — Real-time alerts for match reminders, score confirmations, teammate invites, and league announcements.
- **Profile Menu** (your avatar) — Jump to your player profile, account settings, or log out.
- **League Switcher** — If you're enrolled in more than one league, use the dropdown in the top bar to switch context between them instantly.

The **left sidebar** is your primary navigation tool. It organizes features into logical sections:

- **Home** — Returns you to the main dashboard overview.
- **Leagues** — Browse your active leagues, find new ones, or manage a league you organize.
- **Schedule** — View and manage upcoming matches.
- **Teams** — Access your roster, invite players, and review team settings.
- **Standings** — Review rankings across all divisions in your league.
- **Rules** — Read the official rulebook for your league.
- **Settings** — Manage your account, notifications, and privacy preferences.

<Tip>
  **Play on the go with the Club Circuit mobile app.** The full dashboard experience is available on iOS and Android. Download the app to receive push notifications for match reminders, submit scores directly from the court, and check standings in real time — no laptop required.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Managing multiple leagues:** If you're a member of more than one active league, each league has its own isolated schedule, roster, standings, and announcements. Use the **League Switcher** in the top navigation bar to move between leagues without losing your place. Your profile and account settings are shared across all leagues.
</Note>

Club Circuit connects players with local and regional pickleball leagues so you can find a competitive home no matter where you are. Once your account and player profile are set up, you can browse open leagues, review formats and schedules, and register for the season that fits your schedule and skill level — all without leaving the platform.

## Join a League

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Leagues">
    From your dashboard, click **Leagues → Find a League** in the sidebar. Use the search filters to narrow results by location, skill level, format (singles, doubles, mixed), season start date, or league type (recreational, competitive, or tournament).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a League">
    Click on any league listing to open its details page. Review the season dates, division structure, match format, fees, and the organizer's contact information before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View League Details">
    On the league detail page, check the **Schedule**, **Divisions**, and **Rules** tabs to confirm the league is the right fit. Pay close attention to the match day and time commitments — some leagues require a set number of available dates before registering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register">
    Click **Register Now** and complete the registration form. Depending on the league, you may register as an individual player or as a full team. If fees apply, you'll be directed to a secure checkout to complete payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Registration">
    After submitting your registration, you'll receive a confirmation email with your league details, season schedule, and a link back to your team's dashboard. Your league will also appear under **My Leagues** on your main dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Public vs. Invite-Only Leagues

League organizers can control who sees and joins their league:

- **Public Leagues** are visible to all Club Circuit users and appear in search results. Any player who meets the eligibility requirements (skill level, location, etc.) can register directly.
- **Invite-Only Leagues** are hidden from public search. To join, you must receive a direct invite link or join code from the league organizer. These are common for private club leagues, workplace groups, or curated competitive divisions.

If a league is invite-only and you believe you should have received an invitation, contact the league organizer directly or reach out to Club Circuit support.

<Note>
  **Registration deadlines matter.** Most leagues lock registration one to two weeks before the season start date to allow organizers to finalize schedules and divisions. Keep an eye on the **Registration Closes** date shown on each league listing — late registrations are not guaranteed.
</Note>

<Info>
  **Waitlists:** If a league or division is full, you can join the waitlist with a single click on the league page. Club Circuit automatically notifies you by email if a spot opens up. Joining a waitlist does not guarantee a place in the league, and no payment is collected until you officially confirm a roster spot.
</Info>
